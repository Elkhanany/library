## Conflicts
- The weight of the GEX sign. The Edge Book (9.5) gives it 10% to 15% of one filter's weight and ignores it when it disagrees with the term structure or the realized-volatility filter. The Dealer Mechanics Lab chapters make it, with the term structure, the decision boundary for choosing strategies (Chapter 8B), the axis of the regime table and classifier (Chapters 9A and 9C), a dashboard read with the flip distance and nearest large strike (Chapter 11A), and a factor in the conviction score (Chapter 13B). The chapter states both, and notes that both treat the sign as context and both reject level-by-level scalping (Chapters 10I and 13F).
- The Cboe 2025 figure. The Edge Book (spx:1.5, 9.5, 10.5) says "~0.2% of SPX daily liquidity"; dl:12E (Chapter 13F) says "~0.2% of futures liquidity". The chapter keeps the Edge Book's wording, as Chapters 1G, 8B, 9C and 11A do. The notes of 1G raised this too; it is still unsettled.
- Thresholds. The Edge Book fires its liquidity filter at a TED spread of 200 bps, a MOVE of 150 or an HY OAS of 600 bps, and gives full size only below 100 bps, 100 and 500 bps. The Lab's dashboard (Chapter 11A) reads HY OAS above 450 bps as elevated and above 700 as stress, and a MOVE above 120, sustained, as rates stress. The ratio of the VIX to the VIX3M is a same-day stress line at 1.0 in the dashboard, and a phased filter at 1.00, 1.05 and 1.15 with 3 closes here. The chapter states both and calls the thresholds conventions to calibrate, as 9.4 itself does.
- Variants of the same filter within the Edge Book: the kill criteria of spx:6.2 (Chapter 10C) and spx:2 (Chapter 1D) cut a put-write by 50% when 21-day realized volatility exceeds 30%, while 9.3 compares realized with implied. Both are kept in their chapters.
