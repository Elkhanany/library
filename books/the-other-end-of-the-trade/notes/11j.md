## Figures
- The chapter places no figure. Six figure ideas: the drift after a surprise, by surprise size and quality; the first morning after a report, with the opening range and VWAP; why seven factors compress; half off and trail against other exit rules; three vehicles over the drift; one report, two trades.
- half-off-and-trail (new, from a figure idea): 400 made-up trades, each a random walk in units of R (eight steps a day, a daily spread of 0.25R) with its own drift, the slider's value times an exponential draw, so most drift a little and a few a lot. Each is stopped at −1R, closed by day 60 at the latest and traded three ways: all out at +0.75R; half out at +0.75R with the rest behind a stop trailing the highest price by the distance set; and all held to day 60. Stops and targets fill at their levels. Defaults: drift 0.03R a day, trailing stop 0.75R, seed 1. First state: means +0.28R, +0.54R and +1.45R; losing trades 27%, 27% and 42%; 90th percentiles 0.75R, 1.77R and 4.80R. The readout "worst once half is off = 0.375R" is [eq:half] with the trailed half stopped at the entry price, the text's 0.375R, and the half-off line shows it as a flat stretch. With the drift at zero the three means come out at −0.01R, +0.02R and +0.06R: the exits reshape the results but create no edge.
