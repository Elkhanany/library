## Changed
- Fixed: the sign of the carry formula (spx:6.4). The source gives \(\text{Carry}_{i,t} = (F_{i,t} - S_{i,t})/S_{i,t}\), "futures-spot ratio, simplified". Its own definition of carry is "the expected return assuming prices don't change", and its own example says commodities in backwardation pay positive carry. For a long futures position with spot unchanged, the return is \((S - F)/F\), which is positive in backwardation (\(F < S\)); the source's ratio has nearly the same size and the opposite sign, and is negative there. The chapter uses \((S - F)/F\) (eq:carry), states the source's form, and says it is the premium of the futures over spot, the carry of a short position. See Questions.
- The link to Chapter 4B is added: with \(F_0 = S_0 e^{(r-q)T}\), carry is about \((q - r)T\), and the chapter names \(q\) for an equity index (the dividend yield), a currency (the foreign interest rate), a commodity (the convenience yield) and a bond (its yield over financing).
- "Most G10 banks moved toward zero rates" is written "Most central banks of the G10 currencies, the ten most traded"; the gloss of G10 is mine.

## Conflicts
- Carry's category. Edge Book 5.2 and 6.5 list cross-asset carry among the four foundation premia; 6.4 says "Don't treat as foundation; treat as supplementary diversifier." The chapter states both and explains them as category (why it pays) against retail sizing (how much you can hold).
- The size of the foundation: "~70-80%" (6.5), the sleeve ranges of 6.5 that sum to 75% to 100%, and "60-70%" (5.2, Chapter 10A). All three are stated.
- The allocation table is in shares of portfolio *risk*, while the stress table weights a $1 million portfolio 60/15/15/10 in *dollars* (its ERP column is 60% of $1 million). The source moves between the two without comment; the chapter keeps each as given.
- Consistent with Chapter 1F: carry's unconditional correlation with equities of about 0.0 to 0.3 and about 0.7 in liquidity events, and trend's near-zero correlation.
