## Conflicts
- GEX levels. The Edge Book (this chapter, and 10I) rejects level trading and allows the sign of GEX only as low-confidence regime context. The Dealer Mechanics Lab gives positioning more weight: its dashboard (Chapter 11A) reads the largest call wall above spot as potential resistance and the largest put wall below as potential support, and its conviction score (Chapter 13B) has a "GEX path support" factor. Both agree that level-by-level scalping is not supported (the Lab's pattern to avoid, Chapter 13F). The chapter states both positions and links 8A, 11A, 13B and 13F.
- The Cboe 2025 figure is "~0.2% of SPX daily liquidity" here (spx:1.5) but "~0.2% of futures liquidity" in dl:12E (Chapter 13F). The chapter keeps this source's wording. The writers of 10I and 13F should settle which is right.
- Classification of calendar effects. This chapter's source lists seasonality (Halloween, January) under behavioral edges and "pin risk / OPEX dynamics" under microstructure. The Edge Book's Chapter 5 (Chapter 10A) calls the structural overlays, Halloween and the OPEX-week tilt among them, "mostly institutional frictions and some behavioral", and its Chapter 8 (Chapter 10G) files OPEX as "microstructure / institutional". The chapter's last check answer says so. Chapters 10A and 10G should use one classification.
