## Conflicts
- Iron condors. The atlas (dl:11.0, Example 3) prefers a defined-risk iron condor for a neutral view with rich IV, sized at 0.25% of the portfolio rather than 1%. The Edge Book (spx:10.6, now Chapter 10I) rejects systematic iron condors for retail accounts and allows only occasional, discretionary condors at extreme implied volatility, sized small. The chapter states both after Example 3 and in the decision rule, and links Chapters 10I, 10A and 12C. The Dealer Mechanics Lab's own do-not-trade card (old 12E, now 13F) also rejects systematic condors, so the atlas's position is best read as "occasional and small", which is close to the Edge Book's caveat.
- The selection tree against the matrix: the figure keeps the app's rules word for word (README, "Where the import departs from the app"), and the text lists where they differ: the horizon feeds no rule; bearish, rich IV and a negative catalyst give the trend list; bullish, cheap IV and a positive catalyst leave out the call debit spread. The text also names the tree's other departures: "extreme" as IVp > 80, the ranking, an added list for long volatility with normal IV or no catalyst, and "No clean fit" where the matrix has no row.
