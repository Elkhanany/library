## Conflicts
- Practitioners against the evidence on the calendar: the "vanna and charm rally" into monthly expiration and the "window of weakness" after it (Karsan; a secondary account; Nomura's "unclenching") against the evidence, which is single-stock (Stivers and Sun: strong option-expiration weeks, modest underperformance after) and untested on SPX. The text states the practitioner account, its mechanical reading, and three reasons it stays lore.
- Skylit's tenor rule ("For options 0–5 DTE, gamma is dominant; for 7–180 DTE, vanna is dominant") against Black-Scholes, which gives a smooth √T trend for options of the same delta, no change for a fixed strike and near-zero vanna at the money, with no break at 5 or 7 days. Stated with the table.
- "GEX governs the range; VEX governs the drift" (Skylit) against Hull and White's minimum-variance delta and Willeboordse's result, which say the two flows overlap when volatility moves with price. Both stated.
- Volland's claim that dealers are "predominantly more sensitive to changes in implied volatility than to the movement in the underlying" is reported as the vendor's, with no test.
