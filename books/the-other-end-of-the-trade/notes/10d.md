## Conflicts
- Entry timing. This source says "Don't time entries — buy and hold across cycles." The conviction example for trend in Edge Book 4.5 (Chapter 13B) scores the regime at 0.55 ("don't allocate at peak; current is mid-cycle") and advises "build the position over time, don't lump-sum, and scale up if a drawdown creates a better entry point", which leans toward timing. The chapter states both and reads the staging as a way of spreading the entry. The writer of 13B should see this.
- Consistent with other chapters: the haircut chain (0.8 to 1.2, 0.3 to 0.5, 0.25 to 0.45, 0.20 to 0.40) matches Edge Book 3.5 (Chapter 13A); the near-zero correlation and the "8 of the 10 largest 60/40 drawdowns" match Chapter 1F.

## Questions for the author
- "Positive in 8 of 10 largest 60/40 drawdowns — including 2008 and 2022": Hurst, Ooi and Pedersen (2017) run to 2016, so which study or data set covers 2022?
- "Positive in every decade since 1880": does the 2010s decade, which contains the 2011 to 2018 drought, count as positive in the study's decades (which may run 2007 to 2016)?
- Live indices "realized Sharpes 0.3-0.5 over 20 years": which years? Edge Book 3.5 gives 2003 to 2024 for the SG Trend Index and the BTOP50.
- DBMF's "1-2 month lag": is it a lag in replicating positions, or in reporting? The chapter says the fund infers positions from recent returns and lags by 1 to 2 months.
