- IBS with RSI(2): dl:10G calls the pair "genuine, not multicollinear", while this failure mode lists them among five measures of one quantity. Both are stated, with pointers to Chapters 11C and 11H, which flag the same inconsistency.
- For the Chapter 13H writer: the trade journal template in dl:12F's appendix says "[Allowed: stop trailed UP on winners, or full exit. NO stop migration toward profit.]". That contradicts this chapter's rule, from the same source, that stops move only toward profit; the template presumably means "away from profit".

This chapter uses only the Trend Pullback's 5 days.
