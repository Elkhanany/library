## Conflicts
- The mechanical-sleeve examples include "a fixed monthly Iron Condor sale at 30 DTE". The Dealer Mechanics Lab teaches the condor (Chapter 12C); the Edge Book rejects systematic condors for retail accounts (Chapter 10I). Both positions are stated, with links.
- Within the Edge Book: the investable-index haircut for the PUT index (spx:3.1 against 3.4), and factor-ETF fees (spx:3.2 against chapter 7). Both stated.
- Within the Dealer Mechanics Lab: execution's 20% to 30% against \(h_2\) of 0.80 to 0.90, and a paper 1.0 realizing 0.3 to 0.5 against 0.4 to 0.6. Both stated.
- The swing example's exit rule, "21-EMA reclaim or 3-day time stop", is not Chapter 11H's Trend Pullback (dl:10G: a 5-day time stop, a target of 1.5R to 2R or a close above the prior 5-day high). dl:12C gives "21-EMA reclaim achieved OR time stop at 3 days" as the kill rule of the IBS + RSI setup instead. The chapter keeps the example's rules as given and calls it "a trend-pullback setup of the kind Chapter 11H formalizes".

## Questions for the author
- The swing example's tax step takes the Sharpe ratio from 0.65 to 0.45 at an effective 40% rate. A proportional tax with full loss offsets scales the mean and the volatility alike and leaves the ratio unchanged; the example implies that tax cuts the mean but not the swings. Please confirm the intended model, or give the example's \(h_3\) another basis.
- Please confirm the corrected haircut of 68% to 73% (source 65-70%) and the recomputed cumulative haircuts of the PUT table.
- Morningstar's Mind the Gap figures (100 to 200 bps a year for two decades) carry no edition year in either source. Which editions?
- "A single force-liquidation event during a 30 VIX environment can wipe out 12-18 months of accumulated edge" has no source; kept at its strength.
- The figure's buy-and-hold baseline, SPX at a user Sharpe ratio of about 0.40, comes from the app with no source in the prose. The text attributes it to the figure.
