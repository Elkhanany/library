## Dropped
- "RV" (spx:15.3): the card writes \(\text{VRP}_t = \text{IV}_t^2 - E_t[\text{RV}_{t,T}^2]\). The book writes implied and realized volatility as \(\sigma_I\) and \(\sigma_R\) (CONVENTIONS), so the formula reads \(\sigma_{I,t}^2 - \mathbb{E}_t[\sigma_{R,t,T}^2]\), as in Chapter 10C.

- "Mi", "Xi" and "RCi" (spx:15.3): the card's subscripted symbols \(M_i\), \(X_i\) and \(\text{RC}_i\), which the facts check reads without their subscripts. R2 writes them as maths, in the conviction score and the risk contribution.

## Changed
- The card's fourteen formulas are joined by the rest of the book's, grouped by part, each linked to the numbered display where a chapter derives it.
- Carry is \((S - F)/F\), as Chapter 10E corrects it: the card's \((F - S)/S\) is negative in backwardation, where the Edge Book says carry is positive.
- The VIX term-structure filter: the card's thresholds (1.05 and 1.10 for 3 days, re-entry below 1.00 for 5 days) differ from the Edge Book's Chapter 9, which Chapter 10H follows (1.00, 1.05 and 1.15 for 3 closes). R2 gives both and says which is fuller.
