- "ES95" is written \(\text{ES}_{95}\) in the chapters, and "ETNs" is the plural of the new entry "exchange-traded note".

## Changed
- R1 is built from glossary/*.json: every term the chapters define, with the chapter that defines it. The Edge Book's glossary is covered by those entries, whose facts are in the chapters that teach each term.
- Three terms appear only in the Edge Book's glossary, so they have entries in glossary/R.json: maximum adverse excursion (MAE) and maximum favorable excursion (MFE), which no chapter uses and which link to no chapter, and exchange-traded note (ETN), linked to Chapter 9A, where VXX and UVXY appear.
- The MOVE index's entry gives its full name, the ICE BofA Treasury MOVE Index, as the Edge Book's glossary does.
