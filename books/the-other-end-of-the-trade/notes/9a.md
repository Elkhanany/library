## Changed
- Fixed (CONVENTIONS, known error): the opening hedge. A dealer short puts is long delta and hedges by selling. The chapter works the hedge on an assumed 30-day expiry with a zero rate (the source gives no expiry): d₁ = 0.374, put delta −0.354, so the dealer is long 35,400 share-equivalents and sells 35,400. Step 4 of the figure then sells 11,000 more (0.11 of delta on 1,000 contracts of 100, the readout's number), taking the short hedge to 46,400. This agrees with the figure, whose README entry records the same correction.
- Fixed (CONVENTIONS, known error): "a 1% downside move in SPX re-prices the put wing far more than a 1% upside move re-prices the call wing." The claim is kept and attributed to the Dealer Mechanics Lab, with its mechanism: the 1/K² weights (a put at 4,400 counts 1.14 times a call at 4,700 of the same price), the steeper put wing of the skew, and implied volatility that stays attached to its strikes, so that a fall moves the money onto the steep put side. A toy on the figure's default smile shows the at-the-money IV rising 0.18 of a point on a 1% fall and falling 0.04 on a 1% rise. The text says the figure moves its whole smile with the forward and so cannot show this (18.507, 18.505, 18.503, as README gives). It adds a calculation the figure does not make: with the default smile held fixed to its strikes, a 5% fall lifts the computed VIX from 18.505 to about 19.22 (+0.72) and a 5% rise lowers it to about 18.07 (−0.44). ## Conflicts
- VIX derivatives. The Dealer Mechanics Lab says to use the VIX as a regime indicator and to trade VIX futures and options as separate instruments. The Edge Book (spx:10.8, the source of Chapter 10I; spx:9.1 and 9.2, of Chapter 10H) rejects VIX ETPs, long and short, for retail accounts, puts long-ETP roll decay at 5% to 15% a year in normal times, and treats the term structure as a filter, never a trade. The chapter states both, with links, in the section on trading the VIX and in the decision rule.
- Five regimes against four. This chapter's table (dl:9A) has five regimes (Stable, Normal, Transition, Stress, Crisis); the regime classifier of Chapter 9C has four (Stable, Range, Caution, Stress). Chapter 9C maps one onto the other.
- GEX. The Edge Book's lower opinion of GEX is set out in Chapter 9C (and Chapters 1G, 10H and 10I); this chapter points there.
- VIX options. Chapter 4A (dl:4A) says VIX options reference VIX futures; dl:9A calls them options "on the VIX index level". The chapter keeps its source's words; see Questions.

## Questions for the author
- The put-wing asymmetry at the index level. With the figure's default smile held fixed to its strikes, a 1% shock moves the computed VIX by +0.12 on a fall and −0.11 on a rise, nearly symmetric; the gap only opens at larger moves (+0.72 against −0.44 at 5%). The at-the-money IV does move 4.5 times more on the fall. So in this toy the −0.80 correlation cannot come mainly from the strike weighting of a fixed smile; it needs the level of implied volatility to rise in falls. Should the text say so?
- The vanna figure's steps are stylized well beyond Black-Scholes for a put 2% below spot: its vanna step of 0.07 for a 1.8-point IV rise is about 16 times what a 30-day put gives (0.004). Should the figure state an expiry and a strike further out, or should its caption say the vanna step is exaggerated for visibility?
- Step 5 of the figure reads a delta shift of +20,000 but a hedge flow of 19,500. Is the gap meant to be a lagging hedge? The text reports both readouts as they are.
- "weighted continuously per the CBOE methodology": what was meant? The chapter drops the phrase.
- VIX futures settlement: the source says Friday morning; Chapter 2A and the Wednesday expiry say Wednesday. The chapter follows Chapter 2A. Please confirm.
- UVXY is given as 2× leveraged long VIX futures. Please confirm the multiple against the issuer's current documents.
- VXX's "approximately −10% annualized" drag sits uneasily with the Edge Book's "VXX has lost 99.84% from 2009 inception through 2024" (Chapter 10I), which compounds to a loss of about 35% a year. Both are kept in their own chapters; which should this chapter's figure be?
- At K₀ the toy uses a single quote ($52). If Cboe's rule differs (an average of the put and the call there), should the toy say so? The chapter adds nothing about it.
- The −0.80 correlation, "reliable stress indicator" and "preceded major SPX drawdowns in nearly every multi-year cycle" have no cited study. Is there one to name?
- VIX options: do they reference the VIX index (dl:9A) or VIX futures (dl:4A, Chapter 4A)? The two chapters now read differently.
- Length: the prose is about 4,400 words against dl:9A's 1,900, about 2.3 times, above the brief's 1.2 to 1.5. The excess is the derivation the brief asked for, the fixes and the chapter shape. The first cuts, if needed, are the roll-down worked example, the log-contract figure idea and the "Common mistake" callout.
