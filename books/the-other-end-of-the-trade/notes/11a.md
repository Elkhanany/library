## Conflicts
- GEX levels. The Dealer Mechanics Lab reads the flip distance, the call wall and the put wall as levels (resistance, support, pinning) and the GEX sign as the regime. The Edge Book keeps only the sign, as low-confidence context worth 10% to 15% of one filter's weight, rejects trading the walls, rejects sizing on magnitude, and calls open-interest concentration descriptive, not predictive (Chapters 1G, 10H, 10I). Both are stated, with Chapter 8A's point that the sign convention is an assumption.
- The trade-family table. "XSP/SPX systematic premium" against the Edge Book's single allowed vehicle, monthly cash-secured at-the-money XSP put writing (10C, 10I). "Defined-risk strangles" are iron condors in effect, which the Edge Book rejects as a systematic strategy (10I). "Short-term mean reversion" and "fade overbought intraday" are microstructure edges, which 1G calls essentially institutional-only. The text names all three cells.
- The central thesis itself: the Dealer Mechanics Lab treats conditional short-horizon mean reversion in SPY as tradable; the Edge Book files 1-to-5-day reversal as microstructure, institutional-only (1G). Stated briefly here and in full in 11B.
- The VIX/VIX3M ratio. The dashboard reads it the same morning (below 1.0 is normal); the Edge Book's filter waits for three closes above 1.00 or 1.05 (10H). Different questions rather than a contradiction; both are stated.
- Liquidity thresholds differ: the dashboard marks HY OAS above 450 bps as elevated and the MOVE above 120 as stress; the Edge Book's full short-volatility size needs HY OAS below 500 bps, the MOVE below 100 and the TED spread below 100 bps (10H).
