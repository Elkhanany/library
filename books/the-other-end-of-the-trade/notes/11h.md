## Conflicts
- The evidence grade of Connors' RSI(2). dl:10G grades it Tier B, "not a Tier A result". dl:12B's evidence scale lists "Connors RSI(2) mean reversion in trending markets" among its Tier 1 examples (0.8 to 1.0), and its worked example scores the Trend Pullback's evidence at 0.85. Both are stated, with Chapter 13B linked.
- IBS and RSI(2). dl:10G, and dl:12D's Example 6, call the pair "genuine, not multicollinear". dl:10B (Chapter 11C) and dl:10L (Chapter 11N, failure mode 8) list RSI(2) and IBS among indicators that compute the same latent quantity. Both are stated; the text asks the reader to measure the correlation on their own instrument.
- GEX. Three setups require positive GEX and two rest on dealer hedging. The Edge Book treats the GEX sign as low-confidence context worth 10% to 15% of one filter's weight, ignored when it disagrees with the term structure or realized volatility (10H), and rejects level trading for retail (10I). Both are stated.
- Short-horizon mean reversion. The Edge Book files 1-5 day reversal under microstructure and flow (1G) and doubts that retail accounts can capture it; the text points to 11B for the two positions side by side.
