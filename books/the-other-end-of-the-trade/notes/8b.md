## Conflicts
- The Dealer Mechanics Lab and the Edge Book on the sign: the Lab reads the 0DTE-era evidence as SPX dealers net long gamma on most days and makes the sign central (Chapter 8D); the Edge Book keeps the sign as low-confidence context worth 10% to 15% of one filter's weight (Chapter 10H). The text states both and says the evidence cuts both ways.
- The Lab and the Edge Book on Garleanu, Pedersen and Poteshman: foundation of the GEX framework against an option-pricing effect that does not validate level rules (Chapter 10I). Both stated.
- Vendors against position data on single stocks: SpotGamma assumes dealers short both calls and puts on equities; Barchart signs calls + and puts − for every ticker; CBOE data for 1990 to 2001 show market makers net long both (Lakonishok and co-authors). Stated.
- The research files disagree on the Cboe 0.2% (resolved by the coordinator in practice.md's favor), on the author order of SSRN 5113405 (evidence.md: Vasquez, Amaya, Pearson, Garcia-Ares; practice.md and theory.md: Amaya, Garcia-Ares, Pearson, Vasquez; the text uses the latter), on whether the GPP sample years are verified, and on which Adams paper "(2025)" means.
