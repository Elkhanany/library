## Conflicts
- Weight of the GEX sign. The Dealer Mechanics Lab makes the sign of net dealer gamma, with the VIX term structure, the most concise characterization of regime and the basis of a decision boundary. The Edge Book treats it as a low-confidence soft input, worth 10% to 15% of one filter's weight, and ignores it when it disagrees with the term structure (10H). Both are stated, with reasons, and a check question applies both to a case where they disagree.
