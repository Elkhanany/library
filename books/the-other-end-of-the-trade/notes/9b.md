## Conflicts
- The size of 0DTE hedging. The Dealer Mechanics Lab (Cboe, Xu, September 2023) puts it at 0.04% to 0.17% of futures liquidity at the median; the Edge Book cites a 2025 Cboe estimate of about 0.2% of SPX daily liquidity (spx:1, spx:9.3, spx:10.5; Chapters 1G, 10H, 10I). The two agree on the order of magnitude. They differ on what follows: the Dealer Mechanics Lab still reads dealer gamma as the main mechanical force of the day (Chapter 9C), while the Edge Book calls the dealer-gamma narrative overstated. The chapter states both and links them; Chapter 9C sets out the disagreement in full.
- 0DTE's share of SPX volume: 62.4% in August 2025 here, "40%+" in the Edge Book (spx:10.5, undated). Not a contradiction, since the dates differ; the chapter uses the dated figures.
- Premium selling. The Dealer Mechanics Lab presents retail 0DTE premium selling as the flow that keeps dealers long gamma; the Edge Book counts 0DTE premium selling as one more short-volatility sleeve that moves with the others in a liquidity event (spx:9, Chapter 10H) and rejects systematic iron condors (Chapter 10I). The practice callout gives the Edge Book's view.

## Questions for the author
- How is the upper end of "0.04% to 0.17%" derived? The median of $173 million per point against $400 billion gives 0.043% for a 1% move; 0.17% would need a move of about 4%, or another gamma figure.
- "Dealer gamma in 0DTE was likely net short" on the April 2025 and October 10, 2025 days: is there a source for the estimate? Kept with "likely".
- The 110 million contract record: is that all US listed options? The chapter says "the single-day options volume record", as the source does.
- The source cites Brogaard, Han and Won (2024) as a University of Utah working paper; is there a title to give?
