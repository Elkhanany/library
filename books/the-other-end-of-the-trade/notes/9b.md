## Conflicts
- The size of 0DTE hedging. The Dealer Mechanics Lab cites Cboe's 2023 note (Xu, September 2023): 0.04% to 0.17% of S&P futures liquidity, which the Lab places "at the median" and Cboe derives from the average through the day. The Edge Book cites Cboe's 2025 note (Xu, "0DTEs Decoded", May 2, 2025): at best about 0.2% of SPX daily liquidity (spx:1, spx:9.3, spx:10.5; Chapters 1G, 10H, 10I). Both are Cboe's, both measure same-day hedging only, and they agree on the order of magnitude. They differ on what follows: the Dealer Mechanics Lab still reads dealer gamma as the main mechanical force of the day (Chapter 9C), while the Edge Book calls the dealer-gamma narrative overstated. The chapter states both and links them; Chapter 9C sets out the disagreement in full.
- 0DTE's share of SPX volume: 62.4% in August 2025 here, "40%+" in the Edge Book (spx:10.5, undated). Not a contradiction, since the dates differ; the chapter uses the dated figures.
- Premium selling. The Dealer Mechanics Lab presents retail 0DTE premium selling as the flow that keeps dealers long gamma; the flow data do not support it (see Changed). The Edge Book counts 0DTE premium selling as one more short-volatility sleeve that moves with the others in a liquidity event (spx:9, Chapter 10H) and rejects systematic iron condors (Chapter 10I). The practice callout gives the Edge Book's view.
- Damping or amplifying. The Lab's "academic consensus" against the literature, which is split by method (positions against volume) and market (the index against single stocks). The chapter states the Lab's view, attributed, and each side's studies with their designs.
