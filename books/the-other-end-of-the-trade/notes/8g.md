## Conflicts
- Does 0DTE trading damp or amplify? Position studies (Dim, Eraker and Vilkov; Adams and co-authors; Amaya and co-authors' bound; Cboe) against a volume study with an instrument (Brogaard, Han and Won), with single-stock weeklies (Ellahie and co-authors) on the amplifying side. Both are stated, with the reading that both can hold.
- Who is on the other side of 0DTE: the Lab (customers sell premium) against Cboe (balanced) and Beckmeyer, Branger and Gayda (an auction-identified retail slice bought more than it sold). Stated, with Chapter 8B for detail.
- Which convention fits SPX: Terstegge (short puts by contracts, calls flat) against Dim, Eraker and Vilkov (positive gamma on average). Stated, with 8B's resolution (contracts against gamma).
- The last half hour: dealer gamma (Baltussen and co-authors; Beckmeyer and Moerke; Barbon, Beckmeyer, Buraschi and Moerke) against a cross-sectional reversal gamma does not explain (Baltussen, Da and Soebhag).
- Leveraged ETFs: amplifiers against economically insignificant once investor flows are counted.
- GME: SEC staff against the academic committee (Chapter 8H).
- Does GEX add to the VIX: SqueezeMetrics' claim, Jonsson and Nyberg, and Ardia and Vaudescal (half-hour) against FlashAlpha's pre-registered daily test.
- What suppressed volatility in 2023 and 2024: a BIS box (dealers hedging structured products) against Cboe (option-income funds not a primary cause; the economic outlook).
