## Conflicts
- None between the two sources in this section.
- Within the dealer-lab curriculum, eq:hedgevar says hedging at intervals adds noise with zero mean, while dl:6F's table (Chapter 6E) has coarser hedging capture less of the variance on average. Chapter 6E reconciles the two: under a random walk the expected capture does not depend on the interval, and the dl:6F table assumes a mean-reverting intraday path. This chapter says the error averages zero and points to Chapter 6E.
