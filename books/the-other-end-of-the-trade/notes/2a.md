## Conflicts
- Where dealers hedge. This chapter (dl:3A) says an SPX dealer hedges in ES futures, with cash-share hedging only at the margin. dl:3B (Chapter 2B) says dealer hedging runs through "lit-exchange spot SPY/SPX shares for delta hedges" and ES "for larger or more cost-sensitive hedges". Chapter 2B states both and points here.
- Spread widths. Here SPY options are slightly tighter than XSP ($0.01 to $0.02 against $0.05 to $0.10). dl:3E (Chapter 2D) gives SPX "the tightest bid-ask spreads on liquid strikes" and XSP spreads "sometimes wider proportionally than SPX". They agree once SPY's advantage is read in dollars and SPX's in proportion to the contract; Chapter 2D says so.
