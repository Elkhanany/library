- "If the bid-ask of a synthetic position is much wider than the bid-ask of its direct equivalent, you've identified a cheap execution route": read as "comparing the two shows which is the cheaper route to the same exposure", since a wider synthetic spread points to the direct route. - The expensive-borrow example: kept, with one added sentence of mechanism. A borrow fee enters parity the way a dividend yield does, lowering C − P, so puts are dearer against calls and a synthetic long captures the difference. The $4.88 on a hypothetical 20% fee for three months is arithmetic on parity (100 × (1 − e^(−0.05))).
