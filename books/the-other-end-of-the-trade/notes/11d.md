## Dropped
- "Flagpole" (dl:10C): capitalized at the start of the source's anchor list; the text writes "flagpole".

## Changed
- The source names its thresholds but not how to measure them. The text defines the measurements it uses: a pullback's depth as its fall over the high it fell from, a consolidation's range as high minus low over the price (the worked example divides by the pivot high), and a retracement as the share of a prior move given back.
- "Positive Mansfield relative strength" is defined as the price ratio to the benchmark over its own n-bar moving average, minus one. The source gives no formula and no period; the text says the period is not fixed.
- The handle rule, "should not retrace more than 50% of the cup's depth", is made exact as (handle high − handle low) / (left lip − cup bottom) ≤ 0.5.
- "Flagpole at least 2-3× recent daily range" is read with the average true range of 11C as the recent daily range. The text keeps "2 to 3" and a check question shows a pole that passes one reading and not the other.
- The VCP's dealer reading ("dealers short calls into the consolidation see their delta hedges shrink... have to chase the move") is explained through Part VI: a short call's hedge shrinks as its delta decays and grows as the breakout lifts it, which is short gamma trading with the move (6D) and, at the extreme, the gamma squeeze of 8C.
- The structure-versus-noise section adds the positive predictive value of a detector, with hypothetical sensitivities, false-positive rates and prevalences, to show why the source says "the fix is not a stricter detector — it's the framework".
- The Tier C grading of 11B is applied to the three patterns (VCP detection, O'Neil's pivot rules), as 11B's source does.
- Old references translated: 10A.5 to 11B, 10D to 11E.

## Conflicts
- The dealer readings of the VCP breakout and the bull flag's pole rest on dealers being short gamma in the calls. Chapter 8A shows that the sign is an assumption about who holds what, and the Edge Book judges the dealer-gamma narrative overstated, citing the Cboe finding of about 0.2% of SPX daily liquidity (1G, 10I). Both cautions are stated after the VCP's dealer note.
- The Edge Book does not evaluate chart patterns. The chapter applies its general red flags for candidate edges (10I) and says it does so.

## Questions for the author
- Which period should Mansfield relative strength use? The source leaves it open.
- Is the "recent daily range" of the flag rule the ATR, and is the minimum 2 or 3 of them?
- The handle is "typically 3-12 weeks" and "shorter" than a cup "lasting weeks to months". A 12-week handle can outlast a short cup. Should the handle's length be tied to the cup's?
- "Final pivot should have a range less than 5% of price": which price, the pivot high, its low or the close? The text divides by the pivot high.
