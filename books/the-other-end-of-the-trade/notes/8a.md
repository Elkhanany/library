## Conflicts
- The cause of put-heavy open interest. The Dealer Mechanics Lab: more put than call open interest because SPX skew is steep. Demand-based option pricing (Garleanu, Pedersen and Poteshman 2009; Bollen and Whaley 2004): heavy demand for index puts is what steepens the skew. Both are stated.
- The size of aggregate dealer gamma. The Lab: $20 billion to $100 billion per 1% move (gross, every expiry, sign assumed). Cboe: market makers' net 0DTE gamma of $170 million to $670 million, 0.04% to 0.17% of futures liquidity, and "at best, just 0.2%" in 2025 (measured, same-day only). The text says they measure different things and does not reconcile them further.
- The weight of the sign: the Lab reads it as the regime (Chapter 8D); the Edge Book keeps it as low-confidence context (Chapter 10H). The decision rule carries both.
