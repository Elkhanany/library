## Conflicts
- Sizing by conviction. The Edge Book (spx:4.9) says conviction tells you whether to trade, not how much; sizing comes from tail loss and portfolio context. The Dealer Mechanics Lab scales size with conviction (full size at 0.5 or more; the figure's 60% to 80% at medium; the reduced sizes of the examples in Chapter 13D) and says the trades that clear all five gates "should be larger and more durable". The section "Where the traditions differ on size" states both, and what they share: both size from an explicit loss budget, which is the ceiling.
- Dealer positioning. Both traditions reject scalping GEX levels (Chapters 10A and 13F). The Lab reads the GEX sign into the regime factor and dealer walls into the Real Value score's G factor; the Edge Book gives dealer positioning low confidence (Chapter 10H). Stated in the text.
- DBMF entry timing. spx:4.5 advises building the position over time and scaling up if a drawdown creates a better entry; spx:6.3 (Chapter 10D) says do not time entries, buy and hold across cycles. Both are stated; Chapter 10D's writer flagged the same tension from the other side.
- Single stocks scored with C. dl:12B says to use the Real Value score for single-name swings, but dl:12D's examples (Chapter 13D) score AAPL and NVDA with C. Noted in 13D.
