These are the papers and books behind the book's claims. The first four lists are the Edge Book's reading list, in its order, with the chapters that use each source. The last lists every other study the chapters cite. Where a finding bears on a decision, its chapter gives the number, the sample and the caveats, so read a study through the chapter before you act on it.

The journals are named in full: the *Journal of Finance*, the *Journal of Financial Economics*, the *Review of Financial Studies*, the *Journal of Portfolio Management*, the *Journal of Monetary Economics*, the *Review of Accounting Studies* and *Mathematical Finance*. A reference such as "15:145-161" is the volume, then the pages.

## Foundational papers

The Edge Book's list of papers to read first.

- **Mehra and Prescott (1985)**, "The Equity Premium: A Puzzle", *Journal of Monetary Economics* 15:145-161. It named the equity premium puzzle: the observed premium is too large to fit standard models of utility. Chapter 1D and Chapter 10B.
- **Jegadeesh and Titman (1993)**, "Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers", *Journal of Finance* 48:65-91. The canonical study of momentum. Chapter 3B and Chapter 10F.
- **Fama and French (1993)**, "Common Risk Factors in the Returns on Stocks and Bonds", *Journal of Financial Economics* 33:3-56. The canonical study of value. Chapter 10F.
- **Carr and Wu (2009)**, "Variance Risk Premiums", *Review of Financial Studies* 22:1311-1341. Chapter 10C.
- **Asness, Moskowitz and Pedersen (2013)**, "Value and Momentum Everywhere", *Journal of Finance* 68:929-985. Chapter 10D and Chapter 10F.
- **Frazzini and Pedersen (2014)**, "Betting Against Beta", *Journal of Financial Economics* 111:1-25. Chapter 10F and Chapter 13A.
- **Jurek and Stafford (2015)**, "The Cost of Capital for Alternative Investments", *Journal of Finance* 70:2185-2226. The Edge Book calls it required reading for the variance risk premium (VRP). Chapter 1D and Chapter 10C.
- **Daniel and Moskowitz (2016)**, "Momentum Crashes", *Journal of Financial Economics* 122:221-247. Chapter 10F.
- **Hurst, Ooi and Pedersen (2017)**, "A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following Investing", *Journal of Portfolio Management* 44:15-29. Chapter 3B, Chapter 10D and Chapter 13E.

## Practitioner and data sources

- **Damodaran**, NYU Stern: regular updates of the implied equity risk premium (ERP), at [pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar). Chapter 10B.
- **Dimson, Marsh and Staunton**, the UBS Yearbook, published every year. Chapter 10B.

- **AQR Insights**, at [aqr.com](https://www.aqr.com): factor research of practitioner quality. Chapter 10F.
