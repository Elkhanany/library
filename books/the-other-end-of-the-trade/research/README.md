# The literature search behind Part VIII

These files are the search that Part VIII (Chapters 8A to 8J) was rewritten from. They were
written in September 2026, before the rewrite, and they are working notes, not part of the
book.

- `brief.md`: what the search was asked to find, and its rules.
- `theory.md`: models of hedging feedback, portfolio insurance and 1987, pinning,
  demand-based option pricing, rebalancing flows that look like gamma, vanna and charm, and
  market impact, with what each family of models predicts.
- `evidence.md`: the empirical studies, then the book's claims that the studies correct, then
  a synthesis.
- `practice.md`: Skylit's Heatseeker in depth, the other vendors and their methods, the
  measurement problem, how practitioners use the maps, the critiques, and notes for the
  heatmap figure.

Each entry has a key, such as `GolezJackwerth2012`, which the chapters' notes files cite as
"research: GolezJackwerth2012". An entry gives the citation, the search results that confirmed
it, the question, the data and method, the findings with their units and sample, the limits,
and how the book used it.

What to trust:

- Most publisher sites, SSRN, arXiv and skylit.ai could not be fetched directly, so much of the
  search ran through search-engine summaries of those pages and through Consensus. A number
  seen only in a summary is marked as such. A detail that no result confirmed is marked
  [unverified], and the chapters do not use it.
- In `practice.md`, [vendor claim] marks a vendor's statement about its own product, and
  [third party] a review or affiliate site. The chapters give vendors' statements as their
  claims.
- The chapter numbers in these files are those at the time of the search. "8A" to "8D" there
  mean the old four-chapter Part VIII, whose material is now spread over 8A to 8J.
- Read a study itself before you quote it from these notes.
