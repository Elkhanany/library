## BS-6970 Scope, and the two distinct decisions this chapter serves

This chapter serves two decisions that share a subtype and almost nothing else.

The first belongs to a patient taken straight to surgery. The specimen is out and the pathology is
final. No tumour in this patient was ever exposed to a drug under observation. Everything that
follows is inferred from stage, nodal status, hormone receptor status, age and comorbidity. Sections
[[BS-6980]], [[BS-6990]], [[BS-7000]] and [[BS-7020]] serve that decision.

The second belongs to a patient who received neoadjuvant therapy and has residual invasive disease
at surgery. That tumour has already met HER2-directed therapy and survived it. The selection
variable here is observed rather than predicted, which is why [[BS-7010]] serves that decision and
no other section does.

Merging the two is the commonest error in this subtype. The adjuvant trials randomised patients
after definitive surgery, so their populations contain nobody whose response was known. The
post-neoadjuvant trials enrolled only patients whose response was known and inadequate. A hazard
ratio from one population does not transfer to the other.

Neighbouring chapters carry the rest, beginning with the choice between surgery first and treatment
first in [[BC-500]] and [[BS-4370]]. Neoadjuvant regimens are in [[BC-830]], with hormone
receptor-positive HER2-positive disease taking its own algorithm in [[BC-840]]. Conjugate design,
resistance and toxicity are in [[BC-460]] and are cited here rather than repeated.

Read the tables this way, since each is generated from the trial registry at build time. The result
and overall survival cells are taken from the publication named in the reference column, so a row
and its citation always agree. Longer follow-up, subgroup structure and cross-trial comparison are
argued in the prose.

The story below is the argument the rest of this chapter is evidence for, and it carries no
figures at all. It states what each group of trials was built to find out, what came back, and
what the design cannot settle, so the tables are read as answers to a question rather than as a
list. It is one node of the [Chapter Stories](stories.html) map.

## BS-6980 The trastuzumab adjuvant trials and the duration question

```evidence setting=early subtype=HER2+ line=adjuvant topic=duration sort=year cols=acronym,n,arms,endpoint,result,os caption=Adjuvant_trastuzumab_and_the_four_trials_that_tested_less_than_one_year
```

The registration trials settled that adjuvant trastuzumab works without settling how long to give
it. One year was a design assumption that became a standard by repetition, and it has since been
tested in both directions.

Longer is now closed. HERA, the only trial that randomised a second year, gained nothing on
disease-free survival while costing cardiac function. A fall in left ventricular ejection fraction
occurred in 7.2% of the two-year group and 4.1% of the one-year group, and grade 3 or 4 adverse
events in 20.4% against 16.3% [@goldhirsch2013hera].

Shorter is not closed, and the four trials that tested it disagree. Read their margins before their
results.

PERSEPHONE allowed three percentage points of four-year disease-free survival. Its control arm had
an event rate near one in ten, so the margin permits that rate to rise by about a third in relative
terms. PHARE allowed a hazard ratio of 1.15, Short-HER allowed 1.29 and SOLD allowed 1.3. Only
PERSEPHONE declared non-inferiority against the margin it had set.

Now put the point estimates beside the margins. PHARE observed 1.08 and did not declare
non-inferiority, while PERSEPHONE observed 1.07 and did. Two trials asking one question in broadly
similar populations reached opposite conclusions on nearly identical effect estimates. What
separated them was the margin and the scale on which it was set. A conclusion that flips on the
margin is a conclusion about the margin, and [[BS-6370]] sets out how to read one.

Nine weeks is a different proposition from six months. Both trials of nine weeks failed to exclude
their margins, and SOLD's point estimate sat outside its own margin entirely.

```practice
One year remains the default on evidence that is a design convention validated by failure to improve
on it, rather than a trial that compared durations prospectively at the outset.

Six months is defensible where cardiac risk, frailty or logistics make twelve unattractive. The
supporting trial declared non-inferiority against a margin permitting a relative rise of roughly a
third in events, which is a sentence the patient should hear [@earl2019].

Nine weeks is for a patient who has already had a cardiac event on treatment, or who has very low
baseline risk. Neither trial of nine weeks demonstrated non-inferiority [@conte2018; @joensuu2018].

None of the four duration trials used dual blockade, and most predate routine pertuzumab. A duration
result obtained under trastuzumab alone does not license a shortened course under trastuzumab and
pertuzumab.
```

## BS-6990 Dual blockade in the adjuvant setting

```evidence setting=early subtype=HER2+ line=adjuvant topic=pertuzumab sort=year cols=acronym,n,arms,endpoint,result,os caption=Adding_a_second_HER2-directed_agent_to_adjuvant_trastuzumab
```

Three ways of adding a second HER2-directed agent have been tested after surgery, of which one
produced a small benefit and two produced none.

APHINITY is the one that worked, and its benefit should be described honestly. The whole-trial
absolute difference at the primary analysis was under one percentage point. Longer follow-up did not
widen it much. At six years the node-positive cohort gained about five percentage points of invasive
disease-free survival, and the node-negative cohort gained nothing at all [@piccart2021aphinity].
Overall survival did not cross its significance boundary.

That split is the whole clinical content of the trial. Nodal status is not a subgroup here in the
usual weak sense. It is the variable that decides whether the drug has any measurable effect, and it
was a stratification factor rather than a post-hoc slice.

ALTTO is the instructive failure, because its drug pair had raised pathological complete response
substantially in the neoadjuvant setting. The adjuvant trial then enrolled more than eight thousand
patients and missed its threshold. That sequence is the subject of [[BS-7070]] and its methodology
is in [[BS-6360]].

KAITLIN tested a different idea, which was substituting a conjugate for the taxane rather than
adding an agent. Invasive disease-free survival was unchanged in both coprimary populations.
Completion of the planned 18 cycles fell from 88.4% to 65.0%, largely through discontinuation for
laboratory abnormality [@krop2022kaitlin]. A regimen that two patients in five do not finish is a
different regimen from the one tested.

```practice
Add pertuzumab for node-positive disease, quoting the absolute figure rather than the hazard ratio,
because the relative effect flatters it [[BS-5690]].

Do not add pertuzumab for node-negative disease, where the randomised evidence shows no benefit at
all [@piccart2021aphinity].

Do not substitute trastuzumab emtansine for the taxane after an anthracycline. It did not improve
outcome and it was completed by far fewer patients [@krop2022kaitlin].
```

## BS-7000 De-escalated adjuvant therapy for small node-negative tumours

```evidence setting=early subtype=HER2+ line=adjuvant phase=2 sort=year cols=acronym,n,population,arms,endpoint,result caption=De-escalated_adjuvant_regimens_for_stage_I_HER2-positive_disease
```

Patients with small node-negative tumours were largely excluded from the registration trials
[@romond2005]. The regimen used for them was therefore built rather than inherited, and the way it
was built constrains what can be claimed for it.

APT has no comparator, so a single-arm trial in a population with a very low event rate can show
only that outcomes are excellent. It cannot show that a shorter regimen is equivalent to a longer
one, because the longer one is absent from the dataset. No margin was stated and none could have
been tested.

ATEMPT was randomised, and its primary endpoint was toxicity rather than efficacy. It was designed
to detect a difference in clinically relevant toxicity, not to exclude a loss of efficacy. The 3:1
allocation left roughly 120 patients in the comparator arm. An efficacy non-inferiority claim from
that arm is not available at any margin a clinician would accept.

The honest position is therefore narrow. Twelve weeks of paclitaxel with trastuzumab produces
excellent outcomes in node-negative tumours of 3 cm or less, and that is the whole of what has been
shown. Trastuzumab emtansine produces excellent outcomes in stage I disease with a different
toxicity profile rather than a smaller one. Neither statement is a demonstration of non-inferiority
against a full regimen, and [[BS-6370]] explains what such a demonstration would require.

```caution
An impressive event-free rate in a low-risk population is not evidence of equivalence. It is mostly
evidence that the population was low risk. The rate would also have been high on a longer regimen,
and nothing in either trial measures the gap between the two. Treat the phrase "non-inferior" as
unavailable here, because no margin was ever set.
```

```practice
For a node-negative tumour of 2 cm or less, weekly paclitaxel with trastuzumab is the default
[@tolaney2015]. Describe the evidence as a single-arm trial with an excellent rate.

Choose trastuzumab emtansine where neuropathy or alopecia dominates the conversation rather than
where total toxicity does, since clinically relevant toxicity was not reduced [@tolaney2021atempt].

T1a and T1b node-negative tumours sit outside even these datasets. Treatment there is extrapolation,
and the consultation should say so.
```

## BS-7010 Post-neoadjuvant intensification for residual disease

```evidence setting=early subtype=HER2+ line=post-neoadjuvant modality=adc sort=year cols=acronym,n,population,arms,endpoint,result,os,status caption=Post-neoadjuvant_therapy_for_residual_invasive_HER2-positive_disease
```

Residual invasive disease after adequate neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy is the strongest
selection rule available in early breast cancer. It identifies a tumour that met the drug and
survived it. That is a measured property of this tumour rather than a prediction about tumours like
it.

KATHERINE is now mature and the maturity matters. At a median follow-up of 8.4 years, seven-year
invasive disease-free survival was 80.8% with the conjugate against 67.1% with trastuzumab. The
hazard ratio for death was 0.66 with an interval of 0.51 to 0.87, and seven-year overall survival
was 89.1% against 84.4% [@geyer2025]. An early recurrence benefit was followed long enough to become
a survival benefit, which is uncommon in this book.

DESTINY-Breast05 replaced the comparator with the drug KATHERINE established. Its eligible
population is narrower and this is routinely missed. Entry required residual invasive disease
together with node-positive disease at surgery, or inoperable disease at diagnosis [@loibl2026db05].
A patient with small-volume node-negative residual disease was studied in KATHERINE and was not
studied in DESTINY-Breast05.

```caution
The recurrence benefit in DESTINY-Breast05 is large and the price is respiratory. Adjudicated
drug-related interstitial lung disease occurred in 9.6% of patients on trastuzumab deruxtecan
against 1.6% on trastuzumab emtansine, and two patients with it died [@loibl2026db05]. Overall
survival is immature, so the current position is a measured recurrence benefit, an unmeasured
survival benefit, and two treatment-related deaths from a toxicity that is detectable early. The
surveillance that separates grade 1 from grade 5 is in [[BS-4100]] and belongs in place before the
first dose.
```

```practice
Ask one question before choosing the agent: was the disease node-positive at surgery, or inoperable
at diagnosis? If yes, the patient matches the DESTINY-Breast05 population. If no, the patient
matches KATHERINE and the randomised evidence for the newer conjugate does not cover them.

Adequate neoadjuvant therapy is part of the selection rule. Residual disease after an inadequate
regimen is a different finding, because the tumour was never properly tested.

Score the specimen so that the rule can be applied. Nodal status after treatment is the variable
both trials selected on, and the conventions are in [[BS-0790]].
```

## BS-7020 Extended adjuvant therapy after trastuzumab

```evidence setting=early subtype=HER2+ line=adjuvant topic=neratinib sort=year cols=acronym,n,arms,endpoint,result,os caption=Extended_adjuvant_HER2-directed_therapy_after_completion_of_trastuzumab
```

ExteNET added a year of oral pan-HER inhibition after a year of trastuzumab. The five-year analysis
gave a hazard ratio of 0.73 with an interval of 0.57 to 0.92, and five-year invasive disease-free
survival of 90.2% against 87.7% [@martin2017].

State the trade plainly, because it comes to about two and a half percentage points of five-year
invasive disease-free survival. The price was grade 3 diarrhoea in 40% of patients, against 2% on
placebo, in a trial conducted without antidiarrhoeal prophylaxis [@chan2016; @martin2017].

The hormone receptor-positive subgroup is where this drug is now used, and the claim needs handling.
Hormone receptor status was a randomisation stratification factor, so the subgroups are balanced.
Balance is not the issue, because a comparison between two subgroups is not itself a randomised
comparison, and a difference in effect across strata carries far less weight than the overall
result. Treat the restriction as a plausible reading of a stratified analysis rather than as a
demonstrated interaction.

A second feature of the trial constrains the population further. Eligibility was amended during
recruitment from stage 1 to 3c down to stage 2 to 3c [@martin2017]. The enrolled population
therefore changed partway through accrual, so a reader applying the result should be clear which
population they mean.

```caution
ExteNET predates routine adjuvant pertuzumab and predates post-neoadjuvant conjugates entirely. A
patient who has completed trastuzumab emtansine or trastuzumab deruxtecan for residual disease was
not represented in it. Extending with neratinib after a conjugate is extrapolation, and the toxicity
is not.
```

## BS-7030 Cardiac safety across the adjuvant trials

The two cardiac toxicities in this chapter behave differently and the mechanisms are set out in
[[BS-4450]]. Surveillance schedules and the management of asymptomatic decline are in [[BC-640]] and
[[BS-5480]]. This section adds only what the adjuvant trials themselves settle.

The duration trials are the cleanest cardiac dataset in breast oncology, because they randomised
exposure time rather than observing it. HERA randomised a second year, with cardiac events rising
alongside it. Secondary cardiac endpoints occurred in 7.3% of the two-year group, 4.4% of the one-year group
and 0.9% of the observation group [@cameron2017]. PERSEPHONE randomised half the exposure and early
discontinuation for cardiotoxicity fell from 8% to 3% [@earl2019]. Short-HER randomised nine weeks
and reported a cardiac event risk ratio of 0.33, with an interval of 0.22 to 0.50 [@conte2018]. SOLD
reported better maintenance of ejection fraction with nine weeks [@joensuu2018].

Read those four results together. Trastuzumab cardiotoxicity is usually described as idiosyncratic
and reversible, in contrast to the dose-dependent anthracycline injury. The duration trials show
that exposure time behaves as a dose. That is a randomised dose-response relationship, and it is the
reason a shortened course is a genuine cardiac intervention rather than a concession.

The anthracycline is the other lever and it was tested directly. In BCIRG-006, congestive heart
failure and cardiac dysfunction were significantly more frequent with the anthracycline and
trastuzumab regimen than with docetaxel, carboplatin and trastuzumab. Seven of the eight reported
leukaemias arose in anthracycline-containing groups [@slamon2011].

```practice
Treat planned trastuzumab duration as a modifiable cardiac exposure in a patient with cardiac risk
factors. The evidence that shorter means safer is randomised in four trials.

Treat anthracycline omission as the larger cardiac decision, since a non-anthracycline regimen with
trastuzumab was not less effective in BCIRG-006 and was clearly less cardiotoxic [@slamon2011].

Measure ejection fraction before the anthracycline and again at the end of chemotherapy, and
concentrate surveillance in the first year [[BS-4450]].

A fall on HER2-directed therapy warrants holding the drug and reassessing. A fall after an
anthracycline is treated rather than observed.
```

## BS-7040 Unresolved questions and what reads out next

```evidence setting=early subtype=HER2+ line=adjuvant,post-neoadjuvant status=ongoing,awaited cols=acronym,phase,population,arms,endpoint,status caption=Adjuvant_and_post-neoadjuvant_HER2_trials_still_running
```

CompassHER2 RD asks whether adding tucatinib to trastuzumab emtansine improves on trastuzumab
emtansine alone in high-risk residual disease, with brain metastasis-free survival among its
outcomes [@osullivan2021]. Its answer will arrive against a changed background, since it was
designed when trastuzumab emtansine was the only established post-neoadjuvant option.
DESTINY-Breast05 has since displaced that comparator in the node-positive population, so a positive
result will still leave the comparison that matters unmade.

Four questions have no trial addressing them at all.

Whether the DESTINY-Breast05 benefit extends to node-negative residual disease. The trial excluded
that group and there is no plan to study it separately.

Whether trastuzumab deruxtecan or trastuzumab emtansine with tucatinib is the better choice for a
patient at risk of brain metastasis, a comparison that no randomised trial exists or is planned to
make.

Whether a shortened trastuzumab course is acceptable under dual blockade. Every duration trial
predates routine pertuzumab, and the cardiac argument for shortening is strongest in exactly the
patients who now receive two antibodies.

Whether molecular residual disease can select post-neoadjuvant intensification better than pathology
can. The assay question is in [[BS-3480]] and the lead-time problem in [[BS-3490]].

One older question remains open on its own terms. Overall survival in APHINITY has not crossed its
boundary at six years [@piccart2021aphinity]. The trial continues, and a node-positive patient
consenting to pertuzumab today is consenting on a recurrence benefit rather than a survival one.

```interplay target=BC-350
Both decisions in this chapter are attempts to act on a tumour that is no longer in the body. The
adjuvant decision infers risk from a resected specimen. The post-neoadjuvant decision infers it from
what a specimen looked like after treatment. Part VI argues that a single specimen misrepresents a
heterogeneous tumour, and residual disease is where that argument bites hardest. The surviving
compartment after HER2 blockade need not carry the antigen level the diagnostic biopsy reported
[[BS-2340]]. That is one reason a bystander-capable conjugate outperforms a non-bystander one in
exactly this population, and the mechanism is in [[BS-4060]] rather than here.
```
