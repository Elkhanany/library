## BS-4330 Adjuvant PARP inhibition for germline carriers

```evidence setting=early subtype=BRCA line=adjuvant modality=parp cols=acronym,n,population,arms,endpoint,result,os caption=The_only_randomised_adjuvant_PARP_inhibitor_trial_in_early_breast_cancer._Its_population_spans_two_subtypes_with_different_entry_criteria.
```

One row, and the population cell is the part to read twice. {{trial:olympia}} enrolled both
hormone receptor-positive and triple-negative disease, with risk criteria that differed between
the two [@tutt2021]. A hormone receptor-positive carrier entered on stage and nodal count. A
triple-negative carrier usually entered on residual disease after neoadjuvant chemotherapy. The
trial was not designed to separate them, so the transferable claim is about germline carriers at
high risk rather than about either subtype on its own. The triple-negative half of the same
argument is in [[BS-4500]]. Invasive disease-free survival separated first and overall survival
followed [@geyer2022].

Two observations matter for the consent conversation and neither is in the table. The separation
was in distant disease-free survival as well as in invasive disease-free survival, at 87.5%
against 80.4% at three years [@tutt2021]. The effect is therefore not carried by second primaries
and non-breast events. And olaparib was added after chemotherapy rather than in place of it, so a
carrier who declines chemotherapy sits outside this evidence [@tutt2021].

A single row also means a single set of choices that nobody varied. Duration was one year in
everyone. The comparator was placebo, not another agent. Eligibility required a germline BRCA1 or
BRCA2 variant, so carriers of PALB2 and of other homologous recombination genes are outside the
evidence entirely. The mechanism is developed in [[BC-180]] and the agents in [[BC-450]]. Germline
testing should precede the adjuvant decision rather than follow it, and the testing strategy is in
[[BC-070]].

```caution
Sequencing olaparib with adjuvant CDK4/6 inhibition in a dually eligible patient is unresolved and
should be presented that way. {{trial:olympia}} did not permit a CDK4/6 inhibitor and {{trial:monarche}} did not
require germline testing, so neither trial observed the combination or the sequence. Choosing one,
choosing the other, or giving them in series are three strategies with no comparative evidence
behind any of them. A patient who is offered a definite order should be told that the order is a
judgement.
```

## BS-4860 PARP inhibition in germline-altered disease

Germline BRCA status is the branch most often checked late and it changes both treatment and
family counselling.

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=BRCA modality=parp status=reported cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,result,os caption=PARP_inhibition_in_advanced_disease._Two_of_these_set_a_single_agent_against_physician's_choice_chemotherapy._One_adds_a_PARP_inhibitor_to_platinum_and_a_taxane_rather_than_replacing_them,_and_one_is_the_expansion_study_that_tested_genes_other_than_BRCA1_and_BRCA2.
```

Two of these four rows ask one question with two drugs. Each set a single oral PARP inhibitor
against the physician's choice of single-agent chemotherapy in germline BRCA1 or BRCA2 carriers,
with progression-free survival by blinded central review as the primary endpoint. Both favoured
the inhibitor and both raised the response rate. Both then failed to separate on overall survival
at their final analyses [@robson2019; @litton2020]. The agreement runs through the negative half
as well as the positive half, which is what makes it a class finding rather than two results.

The third row is a different question wearing the same label. {{trial:brocade3}} did not replace
chemotherapy with a PARP inhibitor. It added veliparib to carboplatin and paclitaxel and compared
that with the same chemotherapy plus placebo [@diras2020]. Its control arm is platinum-based
combination treatment rather than a single agent, and its survival result is not on the record.
Reading it alongside the other two as though all three tested one strategy is the commonest error
made with this table.

{{trial:tbcrc048}} is single-arm and its subject is the gene list rather than the drug. Confirmed
responses appeared in two groups only, germline PALB2 carriers and patients with somatic BRCA1 or
BRCA2 mutations. No response occurred with ATM or CHEK2 alterations alone [@tung2020]. Homologous
recombination related is therefore not an eligibility category. A panel that reports those genes
under one heading invites a decision the data do not support. Repair biology is in [[BC-180]] and
resistance in [[BS-3010]].

What no row tests is the comparison the biology most invites. Neither physician's-choice menu
included platinum. Both were built from capecitabine, eribulin and vinorelbine, with gemcitabine
added in one. A PARP inhibitor has therefore never been randomised against the other drug class
that exploits the same repair defect. Nor has one been randomised against endocrine-based therapy,
so these trials establish activity in hormone receptor-positive disease without establishing where
in the sequence it belongs. The populations were defined by a germline mutation rather than by
receptor status, which leaves the hormone receptor-positive patients inside them a subgroup nobody
powered.
