## BS-5020 Biomarker-driven treatment selection

This chapter is about how to approach the patient. Risk, disease burden, symptom load, biomarker
triage and what the line of treatment is for. The trial record itself, arranged by line, is in
[[BC-920]]. Where a decision here rests on a result, the evidence for it is tabulated there and is
pointed to rather than restated.

The division matters because the two questions come apart in clinic. Which regimen has the better
hazard ratio is answered by [[BC-920]]. Whether this patient should start a regimen at all, and what
to measure to find out if it worked, is answered here.

Triple-negative is defined by what is not there. Three receptor assays report below threshold and
the tumour is assigned to a group on that basis. The category is therefore an artefact of an assay
panel, and the biological diversity inside it is real [@lehmann2011]. The classification argument is
developed in [[BC-120]].

What has changed is that management no longer runs on the absence. Three positive markers now
determine the first decision, and they are checked in a fixed order.

PD-L1 determines whether first-line treatment includes a checkpoint inhibitor [[BS-5030]]. It is an
assay output with an antibody clone, a scoring algorithm and a cut-point attached, not a property of
the tumour.

Germline BRCA1 and BRCA2 status, extended to PALB2 and to somatic BRCA alterations, determines
eligibility for PARP inhibition [[BS-5040]]. It also changes family counselling, which is a second
reason to test early.

HER2 expression level determines whether trastuzumab deruxtecan is available, because a proportion
of tumours called triple-negative score immunohistochemistry 1+ or 2+ without amplification. The
eligibility argument is in [[BC-590]].

Trop-2 expression is measured in trials and is not a selection criterion. In the biomarker analysis
of {{trial:ascent}}, sacituzumab govitecan outperformed chemotherapy across high, medium and low
Trop-2 expression, and the low-expression group was too small for a firm conclusion
[@bardia2021ascentbio]. Tumour mutational burden is high in only a small minority of breast cancers
and is not a routine selection tool here [@barrososousa2020].

```practice
Order all three at the first metastatic presentation, not sequentially as options run out.

PD-L1 on the most recent available tissue, with the assay and cut-point named in the report.

Germline BRCA1 and BRCA2 if never tested, with PALB2 included.

HER2 immunohistochemistry on recent tissue, read for the 0 against 1+ boundary specifically.

The order matters because the first-line decision depends on PD-L1 and cannot wait for the
others. The others determine the second and third decisions and there is time to get them right.
```

## BS-5030 Immunotherapy-based strategies and PD-L1 assessment

Checkpoint blockade works in this disease in a defined subgroup, and the boundary of that subgroup
is an assay boundary rather than a biological one.

```

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC line=1L modality=immunotherapy cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,result,os caption=The_first-line_chemoimmunotherapy_record._Read_the_assay_column_before_the_result_column.
```

The clinical shape of that table is a gradient. In {{trial:keynote355}} the survival benefit is
present at a combined positive score of 10 or more, weaker and not significant at a score of 1 or
more, and absent in the whole randomised population [@cortes2022keynote355]. Benefit concentrates
where the marker is high and disappears as the threshold is lowered. That is what a genuine
predictive biomarker looks like, and it is what makes the cut-point load-bearing rather than
administrative.

The decision that follows is narrow. Most triple-negative disease does not reach the high cut-point,
so this branch covers a minority of patients seen in clinic. For that minority the first line is
settled and should not be delayed while other results are chased.

{{trial:ascent04}} then asked whether the chemotherapy partner can be replaced by a conjugate in
exactly this PD-L1-positive population, and it can [@tolaney2026ascent04]. The practical consequence
is that a positive PD-L1 result no longer determines the whole regimen. It determines only that a
checkpoint inhibitor belongs in it.

```caution
A PD-L1 result is the output of one antibody clone scored by one algorithm at one cut-point. It is
not a property of the tumour, and the same specimen scored by a different assay can cross the
boundary in either direction. The predictive biomarker problem is developed in [[BS-4130]] and the
immune escape biology in [[BS-3020]]. One further point restrains over-reading. The early disease
result was obtained without PD-L1 selection at all [@schmid2020; @schmid2024k522os]. The marker
predicts benefit in the metastatic setting and does not in the neoadjuvant one, and nobody has
explained why.
```

## BS-5040 DNA repair directed treatment

Germline BRCA status identifies a population in which a targeted oral agent outperforms
chemotherapy. Both registration trials enrolled hormone receptor-positive and triple-negative
patients, so the evidence is shared with [[BC-560]].

```story SQ-0810
```

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC,BRCA modality=parp status=reported cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,result,os caption=Reported_PARP_inhibitor_trials_reaching_this_population.
```

Three things in that table govern the decision. {{trial:olympiad}} and {{trial:embraca}} both improved
progression-free survival and neither improved overall survival, which is discussed as an
interpretive problem in [[BS-7740]]. {{trial:tbcrc048}} widened the eligible set and then narrowed it,
because confirmed responses occurred only with germline PALB2 and with somatic BRCA alterations
[@tung2020]. A repair gene on a panel is not by itself an indication.

What the table does not carry is the reason to test early. The eligible population is defined by a
germline result, so a patient never tested is a patient never eligible, and the result also changes
counselling for relatives. Testing is therefore part of the first consultation rather than part of
the second-line decision [[BC-690]].

Platinum belongs in this section because it exploits the same defect. That shared mechanism also
predicts cross-resistance, and the prediction holds. Secondary intragenic mutations that restore the
BRCA2 reading frame confer resistance both to platinum and to PARP inhibition [@sakai2008]. The full
account is in [[BS-3010]] and the platinum trial record is in [[BS-7760]].

```caution
A homologous recombination deficiency score records past genomic instability. Scars persist after
repair function returns, so a tumour can score [[term:hrd]] high while repairing normally at the
moment of testing. The score describes history. The drug needs the present. Repair biology is in
[[BC-180]] and agent detail in [[BC-450]].
```

## BS-5050 Antibody-drug conjugates

Conjugates have moved from the refractory setting to the first line in this subtype within five
years. The trials should be read as three separate populations rather than as one story.

```story SQ-0840
```

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC modality=adc cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,result,os caption=Randomised_TROP2_conjugate_trials_in_triple-negative_disease._The_control_arm_column_is_the_one_to_read_for_expectation_setting.
```

{{trial:ascent}} established the class in patients who had already had at least two chemotherapy
regimens, and the striking figure in it is the comparator rather than the drug
[@bardia2021ascent]. What single-agent chemotherapy achieves in that population is the baseline
every later conversation with a patient should start from.

{{trial:ascent03}} and {{trial:tropion-b02}} then took the class into the first line for patients who
are not candidates for immunotherapy, which is most of them. {{trial:ascent04}} took it into the
first line for those who are. The practical result is that a conjugate is now the default first
cytotoxic for nearly every patient in this chapter.

```caution
The first-line conjugate trials differ in what they have shown. {{trial:tropion-b02}} carried overall
survival as a primary endpoint and met it [@dent2026tropionb02]. {{trial:ascent03}} reports
progression-free survival with survival not yet mature, and a published critique argues that
crossover-adjusted analysis is needed before first-line sacituzumab govitecan is treated as settled
[@poggio2026]. The two are frequently presented as equivalent first-line options. They are
equivalent in indication and not in the strength of what has been demonstrated.
```

```interplay target=BC-350
Triple-negative disease is where the heterogeneity argument of Part VI meets a category built from
three negative results. Trop-2 is expressed across most of these tumours and its level does not
select patients [@bardia2021ascentbio], while HER2 at immunohistochemistry 0 still supports conjugate
responses [@mosele2023]. Both findings point the same way. Antigen abundance is not the variable
governing conjugate benefit at the low end of the range, and a subtype defined by receptor absence is
not thereby a subtype without targets. What varies within these tumours is not only how much antigen
is present but how the payload is handled once delivered, and no routine assay reports that.
```

## BS-5060 Chemotherapy sequencing and realistic expectations

Chemotherapy still carries most of the treatment time in this disease. The expectations attached to
it should be stated rather than implied, and the honest source for them is the control arm column of
the conjugate table in [[BS-5050]].

Read the {{trial:ascent}} and {{trial:ascent03}} control arms together. Single-agent chemotherapy in
the refractory setting delays progression by a matter of weeks. In the first line without
immunotherapy it delays progression by several months. The difference between them is the effect of
line rather than of agent, and it is the most useful single thing to have in mind before a
fourth-line conversation.

Three principles follow.

Sequential single agents are preferred to combinations outside a situation requiring immediate
response. Combinations raise response rate and toxicity without a consistent survival gain.

Platinum has a specific indication in germline BRCA-altered disease and no general superiority
otherwise [[BS-5040]].

Response duration shortens with each line, and the shortening is steeper here than in hormone
receptor-positive disease. A regimen that works in the fourth line is more often working for weeks
than for months.

Agent-level detail belongs to [[BC-440]] and the randomised comparisons between cytotoxics to
[[BS-7760]]. The point specific to this chapter is that expectation setting is a clinical act, and
it is done with absolute medians rather than with hazard ratios.

## BS-5070 Androgen receptor and other subtype-directed targets

Transcriptional subtyping divides triple-negative disease into groups with different biology,
including a luminal androgen receptor group [@lehmann2011]. The clinical question is whether any of
those groups has a therapy attached. Mostly they do not.

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC modality=endocrine weight=exploratory cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,endpoint,result caption=Subtype-directed_targets_tested_inside_triple-negative_disease._Every_row_is_exploratory_and_none_supports_an_indication.
```

Androgen receptor directed therapy is the best tested of these and the results are modest. Activity
is real and it is not a treatment. No androgen receptor directed agent is approved in this setting.

The measurement problem is the reason, and it is worth being precise about. Eligibility in these
trials was set at a nuclear staining threshold chosen inside the study rather than validated across
observers [@gucalp2013]. Benefit was higher in the receptor-defined subgroup than in the whole
enrolled population [@traina2018], which is what an imperfect selection assay produces. A threshold
that has not been reproduced between laboratories cannot support an indication however the responses
distribute around it.

The general lesson recurs across this chapter. A transcriptional subtype is a research
classification. Converting it into an eligibility criterion requires an assay that ordinary
laboratories can run reproducibly, and the gap between those two things is the same gap described for
HER2 scoring in [[BS-2360]]. Trial design consequences are in [[BC-740]], and the alteration-defined
options that do cross this subtype are in [[BS-7770]].

Immune-directed and repair-directed options are covered in [[BS-5030]] and [[BS-5040]]. Everything
else in this subtype remains investigational, and saying so is more useful than listing targets
without evidence.

## BS-5080 Rapidly progressive and refractory disease

This subtype produces the fastest clinical deterioration in breast cancer, and the interval between
a decision and its consequences is short.

Two practical features follow. The first is that a line of therapy must be assessed early. Waiting
twelve weeks for a scan in a patient deteriorating over three is a decision to spend the remaining
time on a failing regimen. The second is that performance status falls faster than in other
subtypes, so eligibility for anything, including trials, is lost quickly.

The judgment that is hardest and most important is when not to treat. The evidence for it is
indirect and consistent. Response rates on single-agent chemotherapy in the refractory setting are
in single figures, as the control arm of {{trial:ascent}} shows [[BS-5050]]. A patient with declining
performance status has a low probability of benefit and a high probability of toxicity, and the
toxicity arrives first.

```practice
Set a reassessment interval that matches the tempo of the disease, not the convention of the
department. In rapidly progressive disease that is often six weeks.

Decide in advance what would make you stop, and say it out loud before starting. A named
stopping rule is easier to act on than a judgment made in the room.

Involve palliative care in parallel with active treatment rather than after it
[@temel2010]. The referral trigger that works is disease status, not exhaustion of options
[[BC-680]].

Performance status is the variable that closes doors. Record it at every visit, because the
trial that is open today will not be open at the next decline.
```

## BS-5090 The unmet need after conjugate failure

The population that defines this disease has moved. Five years ago the unmet need was after
chemotherapy. It is now after conjugates, and the evidence base behind that position is
retrospective.

The relevant data are the sequential conjugate series. Among 85 patients treated with both
trastuzumab deruxtecan and sacituzumab govitecan, progression-free survival on the second conjugate
was shorter than on the first in 75.2%. Fourteen
of the 85 were still on treatment at the data cutoff
[@mai2025]. Longer progression-free survival on the first conjugate predicted longer survival on the
second, which is the signature of partial rather than complete cross-resistance. A second conjugate
is therefore a reasonable option with a smaller expected magnitude.

What makes the position difficult is that three of the payloads now in routine use are
topoisomerase I inhibitors, and one of the standard salvage chemotherapies, irinotecan, shares that
mechanism. A patient who has received sacituzumab govitecan, datopotamab deruxtecan and trastuzumab
deruxtecan has had three exposures to the same payload class delivered by three different antibodies.
That is not three lines of non-cross-resistant therapy, whatever the line count says.

The mechanisms that are not antigen loss remain unmeasurable in clinic. PTEN loss was associated with
de novo resistance to trastuzumab deruxtecan and not to sacituzumab govitecan despite the shared
payload class [@mai2025], which shows that the relevant variable is handling rather than target. The
full taxonomy is in [[BS-3000]].

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC,HER2-low modality=adc status=ongoing cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,endpoint,status caption=The_only_prospective_study_addressing_this_position.
```

{{trial:trade-dxd}} will give the first prospective estimate of how much activity remains after a
conjugate fails. Its cohorts are non-comparative, so it will not rank sequences, and what it will
and will not settle is set out in [[BS-7780]].

The honest summary is that this is now the largest gap in the management of the disease. Trial
enrolment is the most useful thing on offer, and it should be discussed at the start of the
post-conjugate line rather than after it fails [[BS-4800]]. Emerging platforms are in [[BC-760]] and
[[BC-770]].
