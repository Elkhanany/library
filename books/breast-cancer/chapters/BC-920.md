## BS-7720 Scope, and the biomarker triage at diagnosis

This chapter is the trial record for metastatic triple-negative disease, arranged by line. It says
what was tested, what came back, and what each design cannot settle. How to approach the patient
who carries this disease is in [[BC-580]], which covers risk, burden, symptoms and goals. Where the
two chapters touch, [[BC-580]] points forward to the tables here.

Triple-negative is a description by absence, and treating it as one disease is the error this
chapter exists to prevent. The transcriptional and genomic diversity inside the label is set out in
[[BC-120]] [@lehmann2011].

Three tests decide first-line therapy. All three should be sent at metastatic diagnosis rather than
at progression, because the first-line decision cannot wait for a result ordered later.

PD-L1, with the assay and the threshold named in the report. The antibody clone and the scored
compartment differ between the trials that produced the indications, so a bare positive is not a
statement about the patient.

Germline BRCA1 and BRCA2, extended to PALB2 and to somatic BRCA alterations. A patient never tested
is a patient never eligible, which makes the access argument in [[BC-690]] a treatment argument
here.

And HER2 immunohistochemistry, read specifically at the 0 against 1+ boundary. A tumour called
triple-negative may be HER2-low or HER2-ultralow, and therefore eligible for a conjugate approved on
expression that the older classification called negative.

That third point is the one most often missed. HER2-low is not a new biology. It is the same tumours
under a threshold that moved, which is the measurement argument in [[BC-110]] and [[BS-2360]]. The
order in which to send the three tests, and what to do while they are outstanding, is set out in
[[BS-5020]] rather than here.

Each section below opens with the question its trials were built to answer, taken from the
[Chapter Stories](stories.html) map, and closes with a table filtered on that same question. The
story says what was being looked for and what the design cannot settle. The table says what else
is in the cell. The prose argues the decision.

## BS-7730 First-line chemoimmunotherapy and the PD-L1 threshold

```story SQ-0820
```

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC line=1L modality=immunotherapy cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,result,os caption=Randomised_first-line_trials_adding_a_checkpoint_inhibitor_to_a_chemotherapy_backbone._They_differ_in_the_antibody,_the_backbone_and_the_PD-L1_assay.
```

There is no single PD-L1 test in that table. {{trial:impassion130}} used SP142 and scored staining on
immune cells as a percentage of tumour area. {{trial:keynote355}} used 22C3 and scored a combined
positive score across tumour cells, lymphocytes and macrophages. A tumour positive by one is not
necessarily positive by the other. The practical consequence is that the request has to name the
assay the intended regimen was registered with. A report giving a score without a clone cannot be
mapped onto either indication. The general form of this problem is in [[BC-470]] and
[[BS-4130]].

The statistical structure of {{trial:impassion130}} is worth walking through, because it is a worked
illustration of the hierarchical testing point in [[BC-740]]. Overall survival was to be tested first
in the intention-to-treat population. Only if it was significant there would it be tested in the
PD-L1-positive subgroup. It missed in the intention-to-treat population, so the hierarchy stopped
[@schmid2018impassion130]. Every survival figure quoted for the assay-positive group therefore
descends from a test that was never formally performed [@emens2021]. The drug is used on that basis
anyway, which is a defensible clinical judgement and not a statistical result.

{{trial:impassion131}} paired the same antibody with a plain taxane and separated nothing at any
level of the score [@miles2021]. The comparison between the two atezolizumab trials is across
trials rather than randomised. They differ in the taxane, in the corticosteroid premedication a
plain taxane requires, and in the population enrolled. The failure cannot be assigned to the
backbone alone. {{trial:torchlight}} then reproduced the selection pattern with a third antibody and
a nab-paclitaxel backbone [@jiang2024].

{{trial:keynote119}} is the negative that completes the first-line picture. Single-agent
pembrolizumab after one or two prior treatments did not improve survival at any threshold tested
[@winer2021keynote119]. Checkpoint blockade in this disease needs a cytotoxic partner and needs to be
given early. Nothing in the later-line record contradicts that.

```caution
The PD-L1 threshold selects in metastatic disease and does not select in the early setting, where
{{trial:keynote522}} showed benefit without selection at all [@schmid2020; @schmid2024k522os]. Do not
carry the metastatic selection rule backwards into the neoadjuvant decision, and do not carry a
neoadjuvant PD-L1 result forwards into this one. [[BC-860]] sets out why the same marker behaves
differently on either side of surgery.
```

## BS-7740 PARP inhibition for germline carriers

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC,BRCA modality=parp status=reported cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,result,os caption=Reported_PARP_inhibitor_trials_reaching_this_population._Both_registration_trials_enrolled_hormone_receptor-positive_patients_as_well.
```

{{trial:olympiad}} and {{trial:embraca}} established single-agent PARP inhibition against the
physician's choice of chemotherapy in germline carriers. Both met their progression-free survival
endpoint. Neither improved overall survival on final analysis [@robson2019; @litton2020].

The gap between those two endpoints is the interpretive problem. It has a candidate explanation
rather than a settled one. In {{trial:embraca}}, 32.6% of the chemotherapy arm received a PARP
inhibitor afterwards against 4.5% of the talazoparib arm [@litton2020]. Adjusting for subsequent
PARP inhibitor and platinum use moved the hazard ratio for death from 0.848 to 0.756, with a
bootstrap interval still crossing one. Crossover is a reason the survival signal may be diluted. It
is not evidence that the signal exists.

{{trial:tbcrc048}} widened the eligible set and, more usefully, narrowed it. Confirmed responses
occurred only with germline PALB2 and with somatic BRCA1 or BRCA2 alterations. Nothing responded
with ATM or CHEK2 alterations alone [@tung2020]. That is a result about which gene rather than
about how much repair deficiency. It does not license a panel-wide indication.

{{trial:brocade3}} asked the different question of whether a PARP inhibitor belongs with chemotherapy
rather than instead of it, and reported longer progression-free survival for the combination
[@diras2020]. It enrolled any HER2-negative disease, so hormone receptor-positive patients sit
inside a result filed here. {{trial:dora}} then tested maintenance without chemotherapy after
platinum. Its durable benefit did not track germline BRCA status or PD-L1 [@tan2024]. Both arms
were judged against a historical control rather than against each other.

One number from outside this table belongs in the argument. In {{trial:tnt}}, germline BRCA status
raised the response rate to carboplatin and not to docetaxel [@tutt2018]. The same defect that
PARP inhibition exploits also selects for platinum, which is why the two decisions are made
together rather than in sequence. The mechanism is in [[BC-180]] and the resistance shared between
them in [[BS-3010]].

## BS-7750 Conjugates in second line and beyond

```story SQ-0840
```

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC modality=adc cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,result,os caption=Randomised_TROP2_conjugate_trials_in_triple-negative_disease,_from_the_refractory_setting_to_the_first_line.
```

{{trial:ascent}} established the class in a population that had already received at least two lines of
chemotherapy [@bardia2021ascent]. The comparator arm is the figure that changed practice, because a
median progression-free survival of 1.7 months on the physician's choice of single agent is the
honest baseline this disease had before conjugates [@bardia2024].

The class then moved forward in three randomised trials that split the first line by whether
checkpoint blockade was an option. {{trial:ascent03}} took the patients for whom it was not.
{{trial:ascent04}} took the PD-L1-positive patients and compared the conjugate plus pembrolizumab
against chemotherapy plus pembrolizumab. {{trial:tropion-b02}} took a second conjugate into the
immunotherapy-ineligible slot.

Two features of that record should shape a ranking argument rather than a prohibition.
{{trial:tropion-b02}} carried overall survival as a dual primary endpoint and met it
[@dent2026tropionb02]. {{trial:ascent03}} reports progression-free survival with survival not yet
mature, and a published methodological critique argues that crossover-adjusted analysis is needed
before the first-line position is treated as settled [@poggio2026]. The two are frequently presented
as equivalent. They are equivalent in indication and not in what has been demonstrated.

{{trial:ascent04}} leaves its own question open. Its control arm was chemotherapy with
pembrolizumab, not the conjugate alone, so the trial cannot say whether the checkpoint inhibitor is
still needed once the conjugate is in the regimen. The single-arm cohorts of {{trial:begonia}} pair
the other conjugate with PD-L1 blockade and report the highest confirmed response rate recorded at
this position [@schmid2026]. Neither cohort has a control arm. One enrolled all comers and the other only PD-L1-high tumours,
which makes the two rates a difference in selection rather than a comparison.

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=HER2-low line=2L,3L+ modality=adc cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,result,os,status caption=The_HER2_route_out_of_the_triple-negative_label,_for_tumours_scoring_1+_or_2+_without_amplification.
```

{{trial:destiny-b04}} brought trastuzumab deruxtecan into a population the older classification
called HER2-negative [@modi2022]. Its practical consequence for this chapter is a reporting demand.
HER2 immunohistochemistry has to be read and reported at the low end, where pathologist concordance
is poorest [[BS-6710]]. {{trial:daisy}} argues that the boundary is arbitrary in the other direction
too, because confirmed responses occurred in immunohistochemistry 0 disease at a rate well above
zero [@mosele2023].

Toxicity rather than efficacy separates the three conjugates in practice, and the dominant toxicity
differs by linker and payload handling rather than by target [[BS-4100]]. Sacituzumab govitecan is
limited by neutropenia and diarrhoea, and UGT1A1 genotype is worth knowing before the first dose in a
patient with a suggestive history. Trastuzumab deruxtecan carries the interstitial lung disease risk
that should shape ordering in anyone with poor baseline reserve or prior thoracic radiotherapy.
Datopotamab deruxtecan is dominated by mucosal and ocular events instead, which are more tolerable in
a frail patient and more disruptive in a working one.

```interplay target=BC-350
Three conjugates now reach this population and all three carry a topoisomerase I inhibitor payload.
A patient who receives sacituzumab govitecan, datopotamab deruxtecan and trastuzumab deruxtecan has
had three exposures to one payload class delivered by three antibodies. The line count says three
lines. The biology says one. Every assay in clinical use reports antigen abundance, which answers
whether the antibody can bind and nothing about internalisation, lysosomal transport, efflux or
repair capacity [[BS-4090]]. A proportion of what is recorded here as conjugate failure is therefore
allocation failure. No improvement in the payload addresses it.
```

## BS-7760 Chemotherapy sequencing when no target is present

```story SQ-0810
```

A substantial fraction of patients have no PD-L1 positivity above threshold, no germline or somatic
repair alteration and no HER2 expression. For them the sequence is chemotherapy. The randomised record
behind it is older and thinner than the record behind everything above.

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC modality=chemo topic=versus cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,result,os caption=Head-to-head_first-line_chemotherapy_comparisons_in_this_population.
```

{{trial:tnt}} compared carboplatin against docetaxel in unselected advanced disease and found no
difference in response rate [@tutt2018]. Its primary endpoint was response, so a difference in how
long benefit lasted would not have shown. {{trial:cbcsg006}} compared a platinum doublet against a
taxane doublet in untreated disease and favoured the platinum arm [@hu2015]. It enrolled at Chinese
centres only, so its superiority is not a general statement about platinum against a taxane at any
line. {{trial:tbcrc-009}} gave single-agent platinum with a gene expression predictor written in as a
coprimary endpoint. The predictor failed outright [@isakoff2015].

{{trial:calgb-40502-ncctg-n063h}} settles the taxane question in the opposite direction from
expectation. Neither nab-paclitaxel nor ixabepilone beat weekly paclitaxel [@rugo2015]. The newer
agent was not the better one.

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC modality=chemo line=2L,3L+ cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,result,os caption=Randomised_single-agent_comparisons_after_the_first_line.
```

{{trial:embrace}} showed that a single cytotoxic agent can extend survival after two to five prior
regimens [@cortes2011]. {{trial:study301}} then compared eribulin with capecitabine directly and
found neither superior on either co-primary endpoint [@kaufman2015]. Within the class there is no
evidence of a best agent.

Three principles follow. All three are frequently violated. Sequential single agents are preferred to
combinations outside a situation demanding immediate response, because combinations raise response
rate and toxicity without a consistent survival gain. Treat to progression or intolerance rather
than to a fixed number of cycles. And choose the agent by prior exposure, residual neuropathy,
marrow reserve and what the patient will tolerate, because the comparative trials show no efficacy
difference to choose on. Agent detail is in [[BC-440]].

## BS-7770 Antigen-defined and alteration-defined options

```story SQ-0800,SQ-0850
```

The search for something inside this disease to aim at has produced one instructive failure and a
handful of small positives. The failure is the largest and is worth reading closely.

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC modality=pi3k-akt cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,endpoint,result,os caption=The_AKT_axis,_carried_from_randomised_phase_II_to_two_phase_III_trials.
```

Both phase II trials favoured the AKT inhibitor. {{trial:lotus}} read progression-free survival in
the intention-to-treat population and in a prespecified PTEN-low population, and separated nothing
in the selected group [@kim2017]. {{trial:pakt}} read progression-free survival against a one-sided
threshold of 0.10 [@schmid2019a]. Both phase III trials were negative.
{{trial:ipatunity130-cohort-a}} admitted only pathway-altered tumours and found no difference
[@dent2024]. {{trial:capitello-290}} enrolled regardless and read altered tumours alongside the whole
population, and missed on both dual primary endpoints [@schmid2025].

The programme changed the question between phases, and differently for each drug. One trial moved
from a one-sided progression-free survival threshold to overall survival as a phase III primary, so
the measurement that justified going forward is not the measurement that refuted it. The other kept
progression-free survival but moved to a biomarker-restricted population, so it never tested the
population its signal came from. That pattern is the general warning in [[BC-740]].

Earlier candidate dependencies failed in ways their designs could not separate.
{{trial:tbcrc-001}} added EGFR blockade to carboplatin with protocol crossover, so the same patients
supply more than one response group [@carey2012]. {{trial:tbcrc-019}} set its primary endpoint as the
response rate within each arm rather than the difference between arms [@forerotorres2015].
{{trial:tbcrc-011}} gave bicalutamide to androgen receptor-positive tumours defined at a 10% nuclear
staining threshold, and asked for oestrogen and progesterone receptor negativity without specifying
HER2 [@gucalp2013]. {{trial:tbcrc-032}} added a PI3K inhibitor to enzalutamide and reported a clinical
benefit rate rather than a controlled comparison [@lehmann2019].

Four current options are defined by an alteration or a histology rather than by receptors, and all
four are single arms. {{trial:sgntuc-019}} treated activating HER2 mutations in conventionally
HER2-negative disease [@okines2025]. {{trial:nimbus}} selected on tumour mutational burden within
HER2-negative disease [@barrososousa2025]. {{trial:dart-metaplastic}} entered patients on metaplastic
histology alone [@adams2021]. {{trial:talazoparib-beyond-brca}} treated repair gene mutations outside
BRCA1 and BRCA2, and its regressions were confined to a single gene [@gruber2022].

None of these is a trial of triple-negative disease. Each enrolled on a criterion that crosses the
label. Several report no hormone receptor or HER2 split at all. Match the patient to the entry criterion rather
than to the cell in the table. The alteration-defined logic and its trial designs are in [[BC-590]],
and the case for broad sequencing here is in [[BC-750]].

## BS-7780 Unresolved questions and what reads out next

```story SQ-0830
```

What to do after a conjugate is the dominant unanswered question. It is also now the commonest
clinical situation in later-line disease. The only clinical evidence is a retrospective series of 85
patients treated with two conjugates in sequence [@mai2025]. It is consistent with partial rather
than complete cross-resistance.

```evidence setting=metastatic subtype=TNBC,HER2-low modality=adc status=ongoing cols=acronym,phase,n,population,arms,endpoint,status caption=The_first_prospective_study_designed_around_conjugate_sequencing.
```

{{trial:trade-dxd}} treats patients whose disease has progressed on a previous conjugate, in cohorts
defined by which conjugate came first. Its endpoint is response rate and its cohorts are
non-comparative, so it will not rank sequences. It will produce the first prospective estimate of how
much activity remains after a conjugate fails.

Whether responsiveness to checkpoint blockade can be manufactured where the assay says it is absent
remains open. The attempts share one design problem. {{trial:tonic}} allocated patients between
priming cohorts and never compared them statistically [@voorwerk2019].
{{trial:pembrolizumab-plus-radiotherapy-in-metas}} had no control arm, so a shrinking unirradiated
lesion cannot be credited to an abscopal effect [@ho2019]. {{trial:topacio}} and {{trial:mediola}} are
single arms combining PARP and checkpoint inhibition, so neither drug's contribution can be isolated
[@vinayak2019; @domchek2020]. Only {{trial:tbcrc-043}} randomised the immune agent, and it favoured adding PD-L1 blockade to
carboplatin. Its benefit did not follow PD-L1 status [@lehmann2024]. That is the reverse of the selection the first-line trials were built on, and it is
an exploratory phase II.

Whether PD-L1 is the right marker at all is the question underneath the whole first line. It selects
imperfectly, the assays disagree with each other, and the biology in [[BC-250]] suggests
tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes carry information the receptor stain does not. A better marker would
change first-line practice more than a better drug would.

And the whole first line now rests on a conjugate for almost everybody, with no randomised evidence
on what should follow it. Until a conjugate arrives carrying a payload from a different class, the
choices after failure will continue to be made on antigen expression, which answers one question out
of six [[BS-4090]].
