## Thesis

This part teaches the reader to appraise the evidence that fills the rest of the book. It should
be written last and revised first.

and ex vivo sensitivity. Immune-competent and humanized models. Single-cell and spatial

### BC-740 · Clinical trials and evidence interpretation

**Carries** the methodology of proving less-is-not-worse. This chapter is the home of the
de-escalation thread and every de-escalation instance in the book should forward-reference it.

**Threads** Phases and development pathways. Biomarker-selected, basket, umbrella, and platform
designs. Adaptive designs. Endpoints and surrogacy. The non-inferiority margin problem. Dose
optimization and the move away from maximum tolerated dose. Representation and generalizability.
Decentralized conduct. Regulatory pathways.

**Anchors** The recurring reader error is conflating no significant difference with proven
equivalent. Write the non-inferiority section to correct it explicitly, with a worked margin.
{{trial:ispy2}} for adaptive platform design. The surrogate thread resolves here, having been
raised in [[BC-540]] with pCR and in [[BC-390]] with ctDNA.

**Controversy** Whether accelerated approval on PFS has outrun the evidence, given how many
recent conjugate and pathway trials show PFS without OS.

### BC-760 · Emerging therapeutic platforms

**Threads** Next-generation endocrine agents and targeted degradation. Novel conjugates and
payload classes. Bispecific and multispecific antibodies. Cellular therapies and vaccines.
Radiopharmaceuticals. Epigenetic and metabolic therapeutics. Microenvironment-directed therapy.

**Anchors** Conjugates are moving beyond HER2 and TROP2 to HER3, B7-H3 and H4, and LIV-1, and to
bispecific formats including EGFR by HER3 and biparatopic HER2. Targeted protein degradation has
its first approval. Vepdegestrant, a CRBN-recruiting ER PROTAC, was positive in ESR1-mutant
disease in {{trial:veritac2}}, PFS 5.0 against 2.1 months, but not in the population that
included ESR1 wild-type. That split is a precise illustration of biomarker-defined benefit and
should be taught as such rather than reported. Personalized mRNA neoantigen vaccines induced
durable T-cell responses up to 3.5 years in adjuvant TNBC, with {{trial:mrna4157}} plus
pembrolizumab now in phase III. CAR-T remains early with modest solid-tumor efficacy.
Radioligand therapy is largely preclinical or early phase, though FES imaging is already useful.

**Controversy** What separates a real target from a publishable one. Give the section criteria,
not opinions.

**Cross-refs** [[BC-200]] [[BC-430]] [[BC-460]] [[BC-470]]

### BC-780 · Unresolved questions and future directions

**Carries** the book's closing intellectual honesty.

**Threads** Preventing dissemination. Eliminating dormant disease. Overcoming heterogeneity and
acquired resistance. Durable immune control. Defining cure in metastatic disease. Where
heterogeneity sets a hard ceiling on precision medicine. Aligning innovation with affordability
and global access.

**Anchors** The ceiling argument is the payoff of Part VI. If the spatial proteomic paradox
[@mardamshina2025] and the conjugate cross-resistance problem are both real, then some of what
precision medicine promises is not achievable with current sampling. Say it.
