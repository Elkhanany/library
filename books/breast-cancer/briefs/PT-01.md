## Thesis

History is used as argument. Each conceptual model corrected a prior error and seeded the next.
The part must leave the reader holding the book's working vocabulary, which is disease-family
thinking, the spatial and temporal axes, and the distinction between biomarker, surrogate, and
endpoint, before any biology arrives.

---

### BC-010 · History of breast cancer

**Carries** that the pivot from anatomic to systemic thinking is what licenses everything in
Parts VI, IX, and X.

**Threads** The Halstedian centrifugal model and its refutation by Fisher's alternative
hypothesis is the spine. Beatson to tamoxifen establishes that endocrine therapy preceded
mechanistic understanding by a century. The high-dose chemotherapy and transplant episode is
the epistemics lesson, a field talking itself into harm, and it belongs here rather than in a
methods chapter. HER2 and trastuzumab should be foreshadowed as the template for
biomarker-defined development, not fully developed.

**Anchors** [@hortobagyi2020] for the arc. Keep the pre-1970 material short. The chapter earns
its length in the Fisher pivot and the transplant episode.

**Controversy** Whether the spectrum model means both Halsted and Fisher were partly wrong.

### BC-020 · Epidemiology and the changing burden of disease

**Carries** that incidence and mortality trends are not read off biology, and that separating
screening effects from disease biology is a skill the reader must acquire here.

**Threads** Global burden and the epidemiologic transition. Age, cohort, and geography. Rising
young-onset disease as a real signal. Overdiagnosis as a formal epidemiologic concept, not a
rhetorical complaint.

**Anchors** Use the GBD 2023 breast cancer analysis for burden [@gbd2023bc]. The 2017 to 2021
global survival estimates show five-year net survival below 30% in some low-income sub-Saharan
settings against above 90% in high-income countries. That single spread is the strongest
opening argument for Part XIII and should be planted here.

**Controversy** Whether rising young-onset incidence reflects detection, exposure, or biology.

### BC-030 · Conceptual frameworks for understanding breast cancer

**Carries** the book's vocabulary. Short chapter, heavy load.

**Threads** Breast cancer as a family of diseases. Local, systemic, and metastatic competence.
Tumor-intrinsic biology and host context as two separate variables. Spatial and temporal
evolution as the organizing axes. Biomarker, surrogate, and endpoint are three different words.

**Anchors** The claim that terminology follows trial eligibility rather than biology is now
vindicated by HER2-low and HER2-ultralow. The ultralow category exists because
{{trial:destiny-b06}} made IHC 0-with-staining an eligibility line, not because a distinct
biology was discovered. State it here, develop it in [[BC-110]], resolve it in [[BC-280]] and
[[BC-590]].

**Controversy** Whether cure is a coherent word in a disease with a decades-long recurrence
hazard. Raise it here, quantify it in [[BC-330]], answer it in [[BC-780]].
