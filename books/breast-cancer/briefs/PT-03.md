## Thesis

Classification is a contested, purpose-built construct. Every taxonomy chapter must state what
the taxonomy is for, whether prognosis, treatment selection, or biological understanding. It is
rarely all three.

**Carries** what morphology still predicts that molecular profiling does not.

### BC-100 · Clinical and molecular classification

**Carries** classification as a moving framework, not a fact.

**Threads** How PAM50 was derived and its moderate concordance with IHC surrogates.

**Controversy** What a subtype is. Close the chapter on the critique rather than opening with it.

### BC-110 · Receptor assessment and the measurement problem

**Carries** the analytic validity framework, built once here and referred back to everywhere else.
This is one of the most consequential chapters in the book because every later assay argument
depends on it.

**Threads** ER and PR thresholds and the ER-low category. HER2 IHC and ISH algorithms.
The IHC 0 against 1+ boundary, which is the least reproducible distinction in breast pathology
and is now therapeutically decisive. Ki-67. Preanalytics.

**Anchors** Pathologist concordance on the IHC 0 against 1+ distinction runs near 26%.
Three-tier scoring of 0, low, and ultralow reaches roughly 70% concordance with kappa near 0.53.
Targeted training and AI assistance raise agreement to about 81 to 84%. The 2023 ASCO and CAP
update explicitly declined to create new IHC categories, holding that HER2-low is a
treatment-eligibility construct rather than a distinct biological entity [@ascocap2023her2],
while NCCN now requires reporting IHC 0 with against without membrane staining. The
repurposing of a legacy assay for a purpose it was never validated for is the honest framing.

**Controversy** Whether HER2-low and ultralow is an entity or an assay artifact. Carry the
question here. Do not resolve it. It is resolved as a heterogeneity problem in [[BC-280]].

### BC-120 · Triple-negative and other biologically diverse phenotypes

**Carries** that no TNBC subtyping system is yet used in routine care, and why.

**Threads** Lehmann subtypes refined from six to four tumor-intrinsic classes once immune and
stromal contamination was recognized, which is BL1, BL2, M, and LAR [@lehmann_refined].
The Burstein and FUSCC schemes alongside. Basal-like against non-basal. BRCAness and the HRD
phenotype. Androgen receptor driven disease.

**Controversy** Why the classifiers disagree with one another, and whether the disagreement is
biological or methodological.
