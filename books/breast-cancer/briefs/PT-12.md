## Thesis

A full part, not an appendix. Most of what patients live with sits here. Toxicity is organized
by modality so it maps directly onto Parts VIII through X.

---

### BC-640 · Treatment toxicity and supportive care

**Threads** Cytotoxic toxicity. Endocrine symptom burden. Targeted agent toxicity by class.
Conjugate-specific toxicity. Immune-related adverse events. Cardio-oncology. Second malignancies
and clonal hematopoiesis.

**Anchors** Conjugate-associated interstitial lung disease runs roughly 12% any grade with
trastuzumab deruxtecan across the DESTINY-Breast programme, and this chapter should codify the
monitoring and dose-hold algorithm rather than describe the signal. Ocular and stomatitis
signals belong to datopotamab. Anthracycline and HER2-therapy cardiotoxicity differ in
reversibility, and that difference drives surveillance design.

### BC-670 · Communication and shared decision-making

**Carries** absolute against relative benefit, which is where genomic assay and CDK4/6 decisions
become preference-sensitive.

**Threads** Communicating prognosis and uncertainty. Decision aids. Explaining genomic results
and variants of uncertain significance. Cultural humility and language access. Misinformation.

**Controversy** How to communicate small absolute benefits derived from large relative-risk
reductions, for example adjuvant CDK4/6 inhibition and extended endocrine therapy, without
manufacturing either false hope or false reassurance. This is a skills chapter, so write it with
worked phrasings rather than principles alone.
