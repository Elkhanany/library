## Thesis

Screening and diagnosis are decision problems under uncertainty. The biomarker chapters build
on the analytic validity framework from [[BC-110]] rather than restating it.

---

### BC-360 · Screening and early detection

**Threads** Population strategies and the trial evidence. Risk-adapted screening. Modalities.
Dense breasts and notification policy. Interval cancers and what they reveal about biology.
Overdiagnosis quantified and communicated honestly. AI in screening including its equity effects.

**Controversy** Whether AI-augmented screening narrows or widens access gaps.

### BC-380 · Prognostic and predictive assays and somatic profiling

**Threads** Multigene assays and where they disagree. Somatic panels and actionability tiers.

### BC-390 · Liquid biopsy and longitudinal monitoring

**Carries** the unresolved clinical-utility question, stated plainly rather than hedged.

**Threads** ctDNA biology and what governs detectability. Tumor-informed against tumor-naive
MRD. Lead time. Mutation tracking for ESR1 and PIK3CA. CSF analysis in leptomeningeal disease.
Clonal hematopoiesis as a false positive source. Implementation thresholds.

**Anchors** MRD by ctDNA is strongly prognostic across subtypes. Post-surgical detection in the
PREDICT work carried an enormous recurrence hazard while ctDNA-negative patients had roughly 94%
five-year IDFS [@predictmrd]. Ultrasensitive whole-genome-based assays in HR-positive disease
detected relapse a median of about 1.5 years ahead of clinical recurrence. Tumor-informed and
tissue-free assays show comparable lead times, near 7 months in {{trial:ctrak-tn}}, but
tumor-informed platforms have superior analytical sensitivity.

The honest negative belongs here. {{trial:ctrak-tn}} found most ctDNA-positive patients already
had overt metastases and pembrolizumab did not clear ctDNA [@ctrak]. {{trial:trak-er}},
{{trial:dare-ctdna}}, and {{trial:persevere}} are the trials to watch. {{trial:serena6}} is the
proof of concept that ctDNA-guided switching can help, and it is the one place where acting on
molecular change before radiographic progression has improved an outcome [@turner2026serena6].

**Controversy** Whether MRD surveillance should be routine now. ASCO does not recommend it
outside trials, even as Medicare has approved serial testing in stage IIB and above. Present
both facts and let the tension stand.
