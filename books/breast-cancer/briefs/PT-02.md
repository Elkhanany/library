## Thesis

Establish normal tissue, the precursor continuum, the somatic genomic landscape, and germline
susceptibility as the substrate on which all later malignant biology and risk-stratified
prevention operate.

---

### BC-040 · Normal breast development and physiology

**Carries** that developmental biology is load-bearing rather than preliminary.

**Threads** Lineage hierarchy and candidate cells of origin. The biology of parity, a transient
rise in risk followed by long-term reduction. Breast density as both a normal variant and a
predictor. Postpartum involution, which returns in [[BC-610]] as the biology of postpartum
breast cancer.

### BC-050 · Breast carcinogenesis and precursor states

**Carries** the central unsolved problem, which is which in situ lesions progress.

**Threads** Field cancerization. The in situ to invasive transition and multiclonal invasion.
Early subclonal diversification already detectable in situ, which is the first appearance of
the heterogeneity argument. Cell-of-origin hypotheses across subtypes.

**Anchors** This chapter sets up the active-surveillance trials in [[BC-480]]. Write it so that
{{trial:comet}}, {{trial:loris}}, and {{trial:lord}} read as the direct experimental test of
the question posed here.

**Controversy** Whether the atypia-to-DCIS-to-invasion continuum is a real progression sequence
or a morphologic ordering imposed on non-obligate lesions.

### BC-060 · The somatic genomic landscape

**Carries** that mutational signatures are generative processes, not descriptive labels.

**Threads** Drivers and copy number by subtype. Signatures as processes, with APOBEC and
repair-associated signatures as the worked examples. Structural variation, chromothripsis, and
extrachromosomal DNA. Chromatin variants and proteogenomics as additional tiers [@visvader2024].

**Anchors** APOBEC3 mutagenesis is the key anchor because it reappears in Part VI as an active
driver of acquired resistance. APOBEC3-dominant tumors show shorter PFS on endocrine therapy
plus CDK4/6 inhibition and generate the very hotspots that define resistance, including PIK3CA
E545K and E542K, ESR1 E380Q, and RB1 loss [@gupta2025apobec]. This is the cleanest illustration
in the book that a mutational process is itself a therapeutic problem.

**Controversy** Selection against drift, and the difficulty of calling a driver.

**Cross-refs** [[BC-140]] [[BC-150]] [[BC-300]] [[BC-345]]

### BC-070 · Inherited susceptibility and familial breast cancer

**Carries** that BRCA1/2, moderate-penetrance genes, and polygenic risk are three conceptually
distinct things that clinical practice keeps collapsing.

**Threads** Homologous recombination biology. Moderate-penetrance genes and their counseling
problem. Ancestry-specific founder variants. Polygenic risk and its portability failure.
Germline and somatic interaction and biallelic loss.

**Anchors** Testing thresholds have broadened, and Parts IX and X depend on it for PARP
inhibitor eligibility. Contemporary ASCO and Society of Surgical Oncology guidance supports
BRCA1/2 testing for all patients diagnosed at or under 65 and many older patients
[@asco_sso_germline]. NCCN recommends germline testing for all triple-negative and all
metastatic disease. Polygenic risk scores derived in European-ancestry cohorts underperform in
African-ancestry women, with AUROC near 0.57 against 0.63, a concrete instance of the
disparities theme rather than an abstract one.

**Controversy** How to counsel on moderate-penetrance genes and variants of uncertain
significance when penetrance estimates carry wide confidence intervals, and how
reference-database bias raises VUS rates in non-European ancestries.

### BC-080 · Risk assessment and prevention

**Carries** that prevention fails on implementation, not on evidence.

**Threads** Gail, Tyrer-Cuzick, BOADICEA and CanRisk compared, with attention to what each was
built to predict. Density and benign risk lesions. Pharmacologic risk reduction and its
persistent underuse. Risk-reducing surgery.

**Controversy** Why an intervention with proven risk reduction is taken up by so few.
