## Thesis

Build compartments, then immune function, then spatial organization, then the host. Resist
starting at checkpoints. The luminal immune deficit is the recurring explanatory motif and
should be named in every chapter of this part.

---

### BC-210 · Breast cancer immunology

**Carries** why breast cancer response rates to immunotherapy sit where they do.

**Threads** Immunoediting. Neoantigen burden and antigenicity. Standardized TIL scoring and its
reproducibility. Exhaustion states. B cells and tertiary lymphoid structures. Subtype-dependent
immune contexture.

**Anchors** TILs are the best established prognostic immune biomarker in TNBC, with each 10%
increment associated with improved survival, and high-TIL early TNBC does well even without
chemotherapy, which is a de-escalation signal worth flagging forward to [[BC-510]]. Mature
tertiary lymphoid structures with germinal centers occur in roughly 15% of TNBC against under
1% of HR-positive HER2-negative tumors and independently predict better recurrence-free
survival.

**Controversy** Whether tertiary lymphoid structures drive antitumor immunity or merely mark
pre-existing immune activity. This is an unresolved causality question and it determines whether
they are a therapeutic target or a biomarker. Name it plainly.

### BC-220 · Cellular architecture of the tumor microenvironment

**Threads** The CAF-S1 through S4 taxonomy alongside the iCAF, myCAF, and apCAF states.
Fibroblast-mediated immune exclusion. Fibroblasts rewiring the ER response, which is a direct
link to endocrine resistance in [[BC-140]]. Neutrophil extracellular traps, which return in
[[BC-330]] as a dormancy-awakening mechanism. The adipose interface.

**Anchors** CAF-S1, which is FAP-positive, drives regulatory T cell recruitment and
immunosuppression and is enriched in TNBC. Fibroblasts orchestrate tertiary lymphoid structure
formation through the CXCL13 and CXCR5 axis, which makes the CAF compartment both
immunosuppressive and immune-organizing depending on state. That tension is the chapter.

**Cross-refs** [[BC-140]] [[BC-210]] [[BC-330]]

### BC-230 · Functional states of the microenvironment

**Threads** Hypoxia, acidosis, and nutrient competition. Checkpoint biology beyond PD-1 and
PD-L1. Hormone-receptor-driven immune evasion through effects on antigen presentation.
HER2-driven evasion including CD47 and p95HER2. TGF-beta. Drug delivery and interstitial
pressure. Primary and acquired resistance to checkpoint blockade.

**Anchors** ER signaling as an active immune-evasion programme explains the luminal deficit
mechanistically rather than descriptively, and it is the argument that connects [[BC-140]] to
this part.

**Cross-refs** [[BC-140]] [[BC-150]] [[BC-470]]

**Threads** Inflamed, excluded, and desert phenotypes. Platforms and what each resolves.

**Controversy** What spatial data adds over bulk profiling, and at what cost. Answer it honestly.
