## Thesis

Organized by biology rather than by line of therapy, so reordering agents does not require
restructuring chapters. The branches are ESR1, PIK3CA/AKT1/PTEN, HER2 expression level, and
BRCA. Write the chapters as branch points, not as sequences.

---

### BC-550 · General principles of metastatic management

**Threads** De novo against recurrent disease as different populations. Burden, tempo, and
symptoms. Rebiopsy at progression, which is where [[BC-290]] becomes operational.
Oligometastatic and oligoprogressive disease. Treatment-free intervals.

### BC-560 · Metastatic hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative disease

**Carries** the deepest sequencing argument in the book, organized by biomarker branch.

**Threads** Endocrine sensitivity and resistance defined in clinical language. First line.
Branch points at progression. Transition to conjugates. When chemotherapy is still right.
Visceral crisis. Lobular disease. The cost of staying on treatment.

**Anchors** First line remains endocrine therapy plus a CDK4/6 inhibitor, with ribociclib and
abemaciclib, not palbociclib, carrying the OS evidence. {{trial:sonia}} raises the real question
of whether first-line CDK4/6 inhibition is necessary for all, though roughly 90% palbociclib use
limits generalizability and that limitation should be stated.

At progression the branches are biomarker-defined.
ESR1-mutant leads to elacestrant [@bidard2022emerald], imlunestrant with or without abemaciclib
in {{trial:ember3}}, or a ctDNA-guided switch to camizestrant before progression in
{{trial:serena6}}, at 16.8 against 9.2 months [@turner2026serena6].
PIK3CA, AKT1, or PTEN alteration leads to capivasertib in {{trial:capitello291}} or, in
first-line endocrine-resistant disease, the inavolisib triplet with PFS 15.0 against 7.3 months
and OS 34.0 against 27.0 months [@jhaveri2025inavo].
PIK3CA wild-type leads to gedatolisib combinations in {{trial:viktoria1}}.
Then conjugates. Trastuzumab deruxtecan for HER2-low and ultralow in {{trial:destiny-b06}},
sacituzumab govitecan in {{trial:tropics02}} with an OS benefit, and datopotamab deruxtecan in
{{trial:tropion-b01}} with PFS but not OS.

**Controversy** Whether ctDNA-guided pre-emptive switching should generalize beyond ESR1 and
camizestrant.

**Interplay callout** PIK3CA mutation frequency differs by ancestry, which entangles biology
with eligibility and access. Point to [[BC-690]].

**Cross-refs** [[BC-140]] [[BC-170]] [[BC-300]] [[BC-390]] [[BC-430]] [[BC-460]] [[BC-690]]

### BC-570 · Metastatic HER2-positive disease

**Threads** First line and maintenance. Sequencing after first progression. Conjugates and the
reordering of later lines. Kinase inhibitor selection. CNS-active strategy. Triple-positive
disease. Heterogeneous or changing expression. Resistance after conjugate exposure. When to stop
HER2 blockade.

**Anchors** First line is shifting. {{trial:destiny-b09}} shows trastuzumab deruxtecan plus
pertuzumab superior to THP, PFS 40.7 against 26.9 months, and {{trial:patina}} adds palbociclib
maintenance for HR-positive HER2-positive disease, PFS 44.3 against 29.1 months. Second line is
{{trial:destiny-b03}}, PFS 29.0 against 7.2 months and OS 52.6 against 42.7 months. CNS disease
favors {{trial:her2climb}}, with CNS-PFS and OS benefit in the brain-metastasis subgroup, and
trastuzumab deruxtecan is also intracranially active.

**Controversy** Whether a chemotherapy-free first line is now the standard, and what is lost.

**Cross-refs** [[BC-150]] [[BC-280]] [[BC-460]] [[BC-600]]

### BC-580 · Metastatic triple-negative disease

**Threads** Biomarker-driven selection. Immunotherapy. DNA repair directed treatment. Conjugates.
Chemotherapy sequencing and realistic expectations. Refractory disease.

**Anchors** First line depends on PD-L1. CPS at or above 10 leads to chemotherapy plus
pembrolizumab, {{trial:keynote355}} with OS 23.0 against 16.1 months, and now potentially
sacituzumab govitecan plus pembrolizumab in {{trial:ascent04}}. PD-L1-ineligible first line
leads to sacituzumab govitecan in {{trial:ascent03}} or datopotamab deruxtecan in
{{trial:tropion-b02}} with OS 23.7 against 18.7 months. Later lines lead to sacituzumab
govitecan, {{trial:ascent}} with OS 12.1 against 6.7 months. Germline BRCA leads to olaparib or
talazoparib in {{trial:olympiad}} and {{trial:embraca}}, extended to PALB2 and somatic BRCA in
{{trial:tbcrc048}}.

**Controversy** The unmet need after conjugate failure, which is where this population now sits.

**Cross-refs** [[BC-120]] [[BC-180]] [[BC-460]] [[BC-470]]

### BC-590 · Expression and alteration-defined treatment across subtypes

**Carries** antigen thresholds as eligibility criteria, taught through HER2-low as the worked
example and then generalized.

**Threads** HER2-low and ultralow. The general lesson about antigen thresholds. Germline and
somatic repair alterations. Rare actionable mutations and fusions. Tumor-agnostic biomarkers.
Discordant or changing results. Competing targets and how to prioritize.

**Anchors** {{trial:destiny-b06}} created the ultralow category as an eligibility line. Read
alongside the 26% pathologist concordance at the IHC 0 against 1+ boundary from [[BC-110]], this
is the clearest case in oncology of a treatment decision resting on an assay boundary that
observers cannot reliably reproduce. Say that directly.

**Cross-refs** [[BC-110]] [[BC-280]] [[BC-460]]
