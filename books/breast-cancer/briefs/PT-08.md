## Thesis

A modality and drug-class reference written once, so the disease chapters in Parts IX and X stay
short and decision-focused. Escalation and de-escalation logic is introduced here as a
first-class concept and named, so later chapters can instantiate it rather than re-derive it.

---

### BC-400 · Principles of multidisciplinary treatment

**Carries** the name of the de-escalation movement, so every later instance can point back here.

**Threads** Curative intent against disease control. The neoadjuvant, adjuvant, and metastatic
strategic distinction. Escalation, de-escalation, and omission. Response-adapted treatment.
Shared decision-making.

**Anchors** Forward-reference the methodology of proving less-is-not-worse to [[BC-740]]. The
recurring reader error is conflating no significant difference with proven equivalent, and this
chapter should inoculate against it before any de-escalation trial is discussed.

### BC-460 · Antibody-drug conjugates and targeted delivery

**Carries** conjugate design as the mechanistic reference for Part X, because conjugate
sequencing is now a central clinical problem rather than a niche one.

**Threads** Antibody, linker, and payload design. Drug-to-antibody ratio and therapeutic index.
Internalization and trafficking. Bystander killing and why it matters in heterogeneous tumors.
Extracellular payload release and immunological effects. Payload class and cross-resistance.
Resistance mechanisms. Toxicity, specifically interstitial lung disease, ocular, and mucosal.

**Anchors** Three conjugates now span HER2-positive, HER2-low and ultralow, TNBC, and
HR-positive disease. The bystander effect is what makes trastuzumab deruxtecan active across a
heterogeneous and even IHC-0 antigen landscape [@daisy], which is the direct clinical payoff of
[[BC-280]]. Cross-resistance among topoisomerase-I-payload conjugates is unsolved.
Trastuzumab deruxtecan is more extracellular and more immunological than the internalization
model assumes, and that should be stated rather than glossed.

**Controversy** Optimal sequencing after trastuzumab deruxtecan, and whether payload switching
overcomes cross-resistance. This is the single most consequential open question in current
practice and the chapter should say so.

**Cross-refs** [[BC-280]] [[BC-345]] [[BC-560]] [[BC-570]] [[BC-580]] [[BC-590]] [[BC-640]]

### BC-470 · Immunotherapy

**Carries** why breast response rates sit where they do, which is the luminal immune deficit
from [[BC-210]] and [[BC-230]].

**Threads** Checkpoint blockade. Biomarker selection. Combination with chemotherapy. Vaccines.
Cellular therapies. Immune-related toxicity.

**Anchors** Why neoadjuvant initiation matters is demonstrated by the negative adjuvant-only
result in {{trial:impassion030}} [@impassion030]. That contrast with {{trial:keynote522}} is the
clearest available argument that timing relative to intact tumor and nodal priming is not
incidental.
