Part XII · Metastatic systemic therapy by subtype and line · Chapter 75

Metastatic triple-negative disease

A category defined by absence, approached through three positive markers, a tempo judgement and a stated goal.

1 · Biomarker-driven treatment selection

This chapter is about how to approach the patient. Risk, disease burden, symptom load, biomarker triage and what the line of treatment is for. The trial record itself, arranged by line, is in Systemic therapy in metastatic triple-negative disease from first line onwards. Where a decision here rests on a result, the evidence for it is tabulated there and is pointed to rather than restated.

The division matters because the two questions come apart in clinic. Which regimen has the better hazard ratio is answered by Systemic therapy in metastatic triple-negative disease from first line onwards. Whether this patient should start a regimen at all, and what to measure to find out if it worked, is answered here.

Triple-negative is defined by what is not there. Three receptor assays report below threshold and the tumour is assigned to a group on that basis. The category is therefore an artefact of an assay panel, and the biological diversity inside it is real1. The classification argument is developed in Triple-negative and other biologically diverse phenotypes.

What has changed is that management no longer runs on the absence. Three positive markers now determine the first decision, and they are checked in a fixed order.

PD-L1 determines whether first-line treatment includes a checkpoint inhibitor Immunotherapy-based strategies and PD-L1 assessment. It is an assay output with an antibody clone, a scoring algorithm and a cut-point attached, not a property of the tumour.

Germline BRCA1 and BRCA2 status, extended to PALB2 and to somatic BRCA alterations, determines eligibility for PARP inhibition DNA repair directed treatment. It also changes family counselling, which is a second reason to test early.

HER2 expression level determines whether trastuzumab deruxtecan is available, because a proportion of tumours called triple-negative score immunohistochemistry 1+ or 2+ without amplification. The eligibility argument is in Expression and alteration-defined treatment across subtypes.

Trop-2 expression is measured in trials and is not a selection criterion. In the biomarker analysis of ASCENT, sacituzumab govitecan outperformed chemotherapy across high, medium and low Trop-2 expression, and the low-expression group was too small for a firm conclusion2. Tumour mutational burden is high in only a small minority of breast cancers and is not a routine selection tool here3.

In practice

Order all three at the first metastatic presentation, not sequentially as options run out.

PD-L1 on the most recent available tissue, with the assay and cut-point named in the report.

Germline BRCA1 and BRCA2 if never tested, with PALB2 included.

HER2 immunohistochemistry on recent tissue, read for the 0 against 1+ boundary specifically.

The order matters because the first-line decision depends on PD-L1 and cannot wait for the others. The others determine the second and third decisions and there is time to get them right.

2 · Immunotherapy-based strategies and PD-L1 assessment

Checkpoint blockade works in this disease in a defined subgroup, and the boundary of that subgroup is an assay boundary rather than a biological one.

Advanced disease · TNBC · any

Finding something to aim at in a disease defined by absence

Does the PD-L1 assay identify who gains from a checkpoint inhibitor, whether it is added to first-line chemotherapy or given alone?

Rationale
Triple-negative tumours carry more lymphocytic infiltrate and more PD-L1 than the other subtypes do. Only a minority of them carry either. A checkpoint inhibitor can release a response only where one already exists, so each trial carried a companion assay meant to find the tumours where it does.
Experiment
Four randomised first-line trials added a checkpoint inhibitor to a chemotherapy backbone and compared it against the same backbone alone. They differ in the checkpoint antibody, in the chemotherapy partner, and in how PD-L1 was stained, scored and thresholded. Two used nab-paclitaxel, one a plain taxane, and one allowed a choice of backbones. A fifth trial gave the checkpoint inhibitor alone against the physician's choice of chemotherapy after prior treatment, with the score as the stratifying question.
Finding
Survival benefit is confined to the assay-positive population. The trial that read overall survival by score found it only above the higher cut-off, and it did not reach significance in everyone enrolled. Progression-free survival is less tidy than the selection story suggests, because in the first atezolizumab trial it improved in the whole enrolled population as well as in the assay-positive subgroup. Nothing appeared at all in the trial that paired the same antibody with a plain taxane, at any level of the score. Given alone after prior treatment, a checkpoint inhibitor did not beat chemotherapy at either score threshold tested.
Limitation
There is no single PD-L1 test in this set. The trials used different antibodies, scored different compartments, tumour cells against immune cells against a combined count, and set different cut-offs. A patient positive in one trial is not necessarily positive in another. The discordance between the two atezolizumab trials is a comparison across trials rather than a randomised one. Those two differ in the taxane, in the corticosteroid premedication a plain taxane requires, and in the population enrolled, so the failure cannot be assigned to the backbone alone. In the first atezolizumab trial the hierarchy stopped when overall survival in the whole enrolled population missed, so the survival figure clinicians quote for the assay-positive group was never formally tested.

The clinical shape of that table is a gradient. In KEYNOTE-355 the survival benefit is present at a combined positive score of 10 or more, weaker and not significant at a score of 1 or more, and absent in the whole randomised population6. Benefit concentrates where the marker is high and disappears as the threshold is lowered. That is what a genuine predictive biomarker looks like, and it is what makes the cut-point load-bearing rather than administrative.

The decision that follows is narrow. Most triple-negative disease does not reach the high cut-point, so this branch covers a minority of patients seen in clinic. For that minority the first line is settled and should not be delayed while other results are chased.

ASCENT-04 then asked whether the chemotherapy partner can be replaced by a conjugate in exactly this PD-L1-positive population, and it can4. The practical consequence is that a positive PD-L1 result no longer determines the whole regimen. It determines only that a checkpoint inhibitor belongs in it.

Caution

A PD-L1 result is the output of one antibody clone scored by one algorithm at one cut-point. It is not a property of the tumour, and the same specimen scored by a different assay can cross the boundary in either direction. The predictive biomarker problem is developed in Predictive biomarkers and patient selection and the immune escape biology in Immunotherapy resistance, primary and acquired. One further point restrains over-reading. The early disease result was obtained without PD-L1 selection at all9,10. The marker predicts benefit in the metastatic setting and does not in the neoadjuvant one, and nobody has explained why.

3 · DNA repair directed treatment

Germline BRCA status identifies a population in which a targeted oral agent outperforms chemotherapy. Both registration trials enrolled hormone receptor-positive and triple-negative patients, so the evidence is shared with Metastatic hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative disease.

Advanced disease · TNBC · any

Finding something to aim at in a disease defined by absence

Does any measure of defective DNA repair select who benefits from platinum, beyond a germline BRCA mutation?

Rationale
Triple-negative tumours carry genomic scars that resemble those of BRCA1-mutant cancers. Platinum kills cells that cannot repair a crosslink. If the resemblance is functional rather than cosmetic, an assay of repair ought to find platinum responders among the large majority who carry no germline mutation.
Experiment
Single-agent platinum was given in a single-arm trial with a gene expression predictor written in as a coprimary endpoint. Platinum was then randomised against a taxane in unselected advanced disease, with germline BRCA and several other repair-defect markers prespecified as interaction tests. A platinum doublet was randomised against a taxane doublet in untreated disease, gemcitabine sitting in both arms, and tested for non-inferiority before superiority. Two further trials asked the same question of PARP inhibition. One added a PARP inhibitor to platinum and taxane in germline carriers. The other gave a PARP inhibitor alone to tumours wild-type for BRCA1 and BRCA2 that carried a mutation in another repair gene.
Finding
Platinum holds its own against a taxane in unselected disease. The single-agent comparison found no difference in response rate between carboplatin and docetaxel, and the doublet comparison favoured the platinum arm on progression-free survival. Germline BRCA status is the one marker that tracks response wherever it was measured, raising the response to carboplatin in the randomised trial and to single-agent platinum in the single-arm one. Nothing else reproduced it. The prespecified expression predictor failed outright, the commercial instability score carried as an interaction test in the randomised trial showed no effect, and the panel of other repair genes gave regressions only in a single gene and only on very few patients.
Limitation
The predictive claims rest on subgroups and on single arms rather than on randomised selection. In the platinum monotherapy trial the choice between cisplatin and carboplatin was left to the treating physician, so the two agents are not being compared. Its germline carriers were a subgroup rather than an entry criterion, and there were very few of them. Its prespecified expression predictor was the negative half of a coprimary endpoint, while the genomic instability analyses that did separate responders there were exploratory and did not survive the randomised trial's prespecified test of an instability score. The randomised single-agent comparison read objective response rate as its primary endpoint, so a difference in how long benefit lasted would not have shown, and its overall survival is not yet reported. The doublet comparison enrolled untreated patients at Chinese centres only, so its superiority is not a general statement about platinum against a taxane at any line. The trial that added a PARP inhibitor to platinum and taxane required a germline mutation but allowed any HER2-negative disease, so hormone receptor-positive patients sit inside a result filed here.

Three things in that table govern the decision. OlympiAD and EMBRACA both improved progression-free survival and neither improved overall survival, which is discussed as an interpretive problem in PARP inhibition for germline carriers. TBCRC 048 widened the eligible set and then narrowed it, because confirmed responses occurred only with germline PALB2 and with somatic BRCA alterations13. A repair gene on a panel is not by itself an indication.

What the table does not carry is the reason to test early. The eligible population is defined by a germline result, so a patient never tested is a patient never eligible, and the result also changes counselling for relatives. Testing is therefore part of the first consultation rather than part of the second-line decision Disparities across the breast cancer continuum.

Platinum belongs in this section because it exploits the same defect. That shared mechanism also predicts cross-resistance, and the prediction holds. Secondary intragenic mutations that restore the BRCA2 reading frame confer resistance both to platinum and to PARP inhibition16. The full account is in PARP inhibitor and platinum resistance, including restoration of repair and the platinum trial record is in Chemotherapy sequencing when no target is present.

Caution

A homologous recombination deficiency score records past genomic instability. Scars persist after repair function returns, so a tumour can score Homologous recombination deficiency high while repairing normally at the moment of testing. The score describes history. The drug needs the present. Repair biology is in DNA repair, replication stress, and genomic instability and agent detail in Targeted therapy.

4 · Antibody-drug conjugates

Conjugates have moved from the refractory setting to the first line in this subtype within five years. The trials should be read as three separate populations rather than as one story.

Advanced disease · TNBC · any

Finding something to aim at in a disease defined by absence

Does an antibody-drug conjugate do better than the chemotherapy it replaces, and does it belong in the first line?

Rationale
TROP2 sits on the surface of most triple-negative tumours. A conjugate puts a topoisomerase inhibitor inside the cells that carry it, at a dose higher than free drug would allow. If chemotherapy earns its place in these regimens by killing cells, a conjugate should earn more of it.
Experiment
The conjugate was first randomised against the physician's choice of single-agent chemotherapy in patients who had already been through several lines. It was then moved to the first line in two randomised trials that split the population by whether checkpoint blockade was an option. Where PD-L1 was positive the conjugate was given with pembrolizumab against chemotherapy with pembrolizumab. Where it was not, the conjugate was given alone against chemotherapy. A second TROP2 conjugate was randomised against the investigator's choice of chemotherapy in the first line. A platform study gave that second conjugate with PD-L1 blockade in two single-arm first-line cohorts, one taking all comers and one restricted to PD-L1-high tumours.
Finding
The conjugate beat chemotherapy in every randomised comparison, in later lines and in the first line, with a checkpoint partner and without one. The second conjugate beat chemotherapy on both progression-free and overall survival in the first line. The single-arm cohorts of conjugate with PD-L1 blockade report the highest confirmed response rate of any first-line regimen recorded at this position.
Limitation
Those cohorts have no control arm. They cannot show what the immune agent adds to the conjugate, and they do not validate other pairings of a conjugate with immunotherapy. The two cohorts differ in selection rather than in treatment, so their response rates are not a comparison with each other, and the PD-L1-high cohort is early with immature duration of response. In the randomised first-line combination the control arm was chemotherapy with pembrolizumab rather than the conjugate alone, so the trial does not say whether the checkpoint inhibitor is still needed once the conjugate is in the regimen. Neither first-line trial of the conjugate that carries the later-line evidence has reported overall survival yet, so the move forward rests on progression-free survival for that agent while the competing conjugate already has a survival result to show.

ASCENT-03 and TROPION-Breast02 then took the class into the first line for patients who are not candidates for immunotherapy, which is most of them. Caution

The first-line conjugate trials differ in what they have shown. TROPION-Breast02 carried overall survival as a primary endpoint and met it17. ASCENT-03 reports progression-free survival with survival not yet mature, and a published critique argues that crossover-adjusted analysis is needed before first-line sacituzumab govitecan is treated as settled20. The two are frequently presented as equivalent first-line options. They are equivalent in indication and not in the strength of what has been demonstrated.

Interplay

Triple-negative disease is where the heterogeneity argument of Part VI meets a category built from three negative results. Trop-2 is expressed across most of these tumours and its level does not select patients2, while HER2 at immunohistochemistry 0 still supports conjugate responses21. Both findings point the same way. Antigen abundance is not the variable governing conjugate benefit at the low end of the range, and a subtype defined by receptor absence is not thereby a subtype without targets. What varies within these tumours is not only how much antigen is present but how the payload is handled once delivered, and no routine assay reports that.

5 · Chemotherapy sequencing and realistic expectations

Chemotherapy still carries most of the treatment time in this disease. The expectations attached to it should be stated rather than implied, and the honest source for them is the control arm column of the conjugate table in Antibody-drug conjugates.

Read the ASCENT and ASCENT-03 control arms together. Single-agent chemotherapy in the refractory setting delays progression by a matter of weeks. In the first line without immunotherapy it delays progression by several months. The difference between them is the effect of line rather than of agent, and it is the most useful single thing to have in mind before a fourth-line conversation.

Three principles follow.

Sequential single agents are preferred to combinations outside a situation requiring immediate response. Combinations raise response rate and toxicity without a consistent survival gain.

Platinum has a specific indication in germline BRCA-altered disease and no general superiority otherwise DNA repair directed treatment.

Response duration shortens with each line, and the shortening is steeper here than in hormone receptor-positive disease. A regimen that works in the fourth line is more often working for weeks than for months.

Agent-level detail belongs to Cytotoxic chemotherapy and the randomised comparisons between cytotoxics to Chemotherapy sequencing when no target is present. The point specific to this chapter is that expectation setting is a clinical act, and it is done with absolute medians rather than with hazard ratios.

Androgen receptor directed therapy is the best tested of these and the results are modest. Activity is real and it is not a treatment. No androgen receptor directed agent is approved in this setting.

The measurement problem is the reason, and it is worth being precise about. Eligibility in these trials was set at a nuclear staining threshold chosen inside the study rather than validated across observers25. Benefit was higher in the receptor-defined subgroup than in the whole enrolled population24, which is what an imperfect selection assay produces. A threshold that has not been reproduced between laboratories cannot support an indication however the responses distribute around it.

The general lesson recurs across this chapter. A transcriptional subtype is a research classification. Converting it into an eligibility criterion requires an assay that ordinary laboratories can run reproducibly, and the gap between those two things is the same gap described for HER2 scoring in HER2-low and ultralow reframed as a heterogeneity problem. 7 · Rapidly progressive and refractory disease

This subtype produces the fastest clinical deterioration in breast cancer, and the interval between a decision and its consequences is short.

Two practical features follow. The first is that a line of therapy must be assessed early. Waiting twelve weeks for a scan in a patient deteriorating over three is a decision to spend the remaining time on a failing regimen. The second is that performance status falls faster than in other subtypes, so eligibility for anything, including trials, is lost quickly.

The judgment that is hardest and most important is when not to treat. The evidence for it is indirect and consistent. Response rates on single-agent chemotherapy in the refractory setting are in single figures, as the control arm of ASCENT shows Antibody-drug conjugates. A patient with declining performance status has a low probability of benefit and a high probability of toxicity, and the toxicity arrives first.

In practice

Set a reassessment interval that matches the tempo of the disease, not the convention of the department. In rapidly progressive disease that is often six weeks.

Decide in advance what would make you stop, and say it out loud before starting. A named stopping rule is easier to act on than a judgment made in the room.

Involve palliative care in parallel with active treatment rather than after it26. The referral trigger that works is disease status, not exhaustion of options Palliative and end-of-life care.

Performance status is the variable that closes doors. Record it at every visit, because the trial that is open today will not be open at the next decline.

8 · The unmet need after conjugate failure

The population that defines this disease has moved. Five years ago the unmet need was after chemotherapy. It is now after conjugates, and the evidence base behind that position is retrospective.

The relevant data are the sequential conjugate series. Among 85 patients treated with both trastuzumab deruxtecan and sacituzumab govitecan, progression-free survival on the second conjugate was shorter than on the first in 75.2%. Fourteen of the 85 were still on treatment at the data cutoff27. Longer progression-free survival on the first conjugate predicted longer survival on the second, which is the signature of partial rather than complete cross-resistance. A second conjugate is therefore a reasonable option with a smaller expected magnitude.

What makes the position difficult is that three of the payloads now in routine use are topoisomerase I inhibitors, and one of the standard salvage chemotherapies, irinotecan, shares that mechanism. A patient who has received sacituzumab govitecan, datopotamab deruxtecan and trastuzumab deruxtecan has had three exposures to the same payload class delivered by three different antibodies. That is not three lines of non-cross-resistant therapy, whatever the line count says.

The mechanisms that are not antigen loss remain unmeasurable in clinic. PTEN loss was associated with de novo resistance to trastuzumab deruxtecan and not to sacituzumab govitecan despite the shared payload class27, which shows that the relevant variable is handling rather than target. The full taxonomy is in Conjugate resistance, antigen loss, payload efflux, and repair capacity.

TRADE-DXd will give the first prospective estimate of how much activity remains after a conjugate fails. Its cohorts are non-comparative, so it will not rank sequences, and what it will and will not settle is set out in Unresolved questions and what reads out next.

The honest summary is that this is now the largest gap in the management of the disease. Trial enrolment is the most useful thing on offer, and it should be discussed at the start of the post-conjugate line rather than after it fails Clinical trials and early integration of palliative care.
