Part X · Adjuvant and neoadjuvant therapy by subtype · Chapter 59

Neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy

The subtype that built the neoadjuvant platform, and the endpoint that platform cannot yet cash.

1 · Scope, and why this subtype made the neoadjuvant platform work

Almost every methodological advantage of the neoadjuvant setting is larger here than anywhere else in breast cancer.

The tumour is in place, so response is measured rather than inferred. Response arrives in months rather than years, at rates high enough for a few hundred patients to separate two regimens. And response carries more prognostic information in this subtype than in any other. The pooled analysis of 12 trials and 11,955 patients found that association strongest in HER2-positive, hormone receptor-negative disease treated with trastuzumab. There the hazard ratio for event-free survival was 0.15, with an interval of 0.09 to 0.271.

Two consequences follow and they pull in opposite directions.

The first is productive, because this is where HER2-directed drug development happens on a signal that is obtainable quickly and cheaply. Most of the agents in Targeted therapy and Antibody-drug conjugates and targeted delivery were first tested against this endpoint.

The second is a trap, since a measurement that predicts outcome for an individual patient is not thereby a measurement that predicts the effect of a drug on outcome. Dual blockade and what the pathological complete response gains bought is where that distinction is worked through, and the statistical form of it is in Endpoints, surrogates, and the overall survival question.

This chapter covers what is given before surgery. What is given afterwards, including the post-neoadjuvant intensification that makes the whole strategy worth pursuing, is in Adjuvant and post-neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy and Post-neoadjuvant intensification for residual disease. Hormone receptor-positive HER2-positive disease has a lower response rate and its own chapter in Early triple-positive disease, while scoring conventions, including Residual cancer burden, are in Pathological assessment after neoadjuvant treatment.

Neoadjuvant chemotherapy backbones and the anthracycline question

NOAH established the principle that matters most and it is easy to forget. Adding trastuzumab to neoadjuvant chemotherapy improved event-free survival, not only response. The trial ran in locally advanced and inflammatory disease, where the event rate was high enough to show it in 235 patients5.

The taxane is not contested. The anthracycline is.

TRYPHAENA was designed as a cardiac safety study rather than an efficacy comparison. Its useful output was that an anthracycline-free regimen with dual blockade produced pathological complete response rates in the same range as the anthracycline-containing regimens4. That is a compatibility result, not a demonstration of equivalence.

TRAIN-2 tested the question with a survival endpoint and this is where the reasoning has to be careful. Three-year event-free survival was nearly identical between the arms. The confidence interval around the hazard ratio ran from 0.50 to 1.632. That interval excludes neither a substantial benefit from omitting the anthracycline nor a substantial harm. No non-inferiority margin was set in advance and the trial was not powered against one.

So the case for omitting the anthracycline does not rest on proven equivalence. It rests on a toxicity difference measured in the same trial. Ejection fraction fell by 10 points or more to below 50% in 7.7% of patients given an anthracycline and 3.2% of those who were not. Two patients in the anthracycline group developed acute leukaemia2. That is the argument, and Designing de-escalation trials and the non-inferiority margin problem explains why it is a better argument than a null result would be.

In practice

Under dual blockade, treat anthracycline omission as the default question rather than the exception. The toxicity evidence is randomised and the efficacy evidence shows no signal of loss.

Do not describe the omission as proven non-inferior, but as a trade of an unmeasured and probably small efficacy risk against a measured cardiac and leukaemogenic one.

Keep the anthracycline in mind for very high-volume nodal disease, where the event rate is high and the confidence interval in TRAIN-2 is least reassuring.

Neoadjuvant dual HER2 blockade and the response gains it produced

Two antibodies against different epitopes of the same receptor raise pathological complete response reliably. NeoSphere raised it by 16.8 percentage points over trastuzumab with docetaxel. NeoALTTO raised it by 21.1 points over trastuzumab with paclitaxel, and both differences were large as well as statistically significant.

Now follow each one to its outcome, because the outcomes are the reason this section exists.

NeoSphere was followed for five years. Progression-free survival was 86% in the dual blockade group and 81% in the trastuzumab group, with a hazard ratio of 0.69 and an interval from 0.34 to 1.4012. A response difference of nearly 17 percentage points was followed by an outcome difference of about 5 points, in an estimate compatible with no benefit at all. The trial had roughly 107 patients per arm, so it could never have measured an outcome difference of that size. That is precisely the difficulty, because the trial that generates a response difference is never the trial that can price it.

NeoALTTO is the cleaner case, because its drug pair was then tested adjuvantly at scale. ALTTO randomised more than eight thousand patients and returned a disease-free survival hazard ratio of 0.84 against trastuzumab alone, which missed its significance threshold13. The neoadjuvant response gain was the larger of the two described above, and the outcome gain was the smaller, so the ordering reversed.

The general statement of this is trial-level surrogacy and it has been measured. Across 12 trials, the correlation between a treatment's increase in pathological complete response and its effect on event-free survival had a coefficient of determination of 0.03. For overall survival the coefficient was 0.24, and the authors judged neither value sufficient to validate the endpoint1. Endpoints, surrogates, and the overall survival question sets out why that is the quantity a surrogate has to satisfy.

Caution

None of this argues against dual blockade, since pertuzumab produces a real adjuvant benefit in node-positive disease Dual blockade in the adjuvant setting and the neoadjuvant response gain pointed in the right direction. What fails is the inference from the size of a response gain to the size of an outcome gain. A regimen that raises pathological complete response by 20 points has not thereby been shown to improve survival by any particular amount. In the lapatinib case it improved survival by an amount no trial could distinguish from zero.

The clinical consequence is narrow and worth stating, because a neoadjuvant trial reporting a response improvement licenses a hypothesis about outcome. It licenses a change of regimen only where response itself changes the next treatment, which in this subtype it does Post-neoadjuvant intensification for residual disease.

Neoadjuvant regimens that remove conventional cytotoxic chemotherapy

Three different things get called de-escalation here and they carry different risks. Removing the anthracycline is Chemotherapy backbones with trastuzumab, removing all conventional cytotoxic chemotherapy is this section, and removing treatment after a response is Response-adapted strategies and imaging-guided escalation.

KRISTINE is the trial that shows what the middle option costs. Substituting a conjugate for chemotherapy lowered pathological complete response. The three-year follow-up showed something more specific and more useful. The hazard ratio for an event-free survival event was 2.61 against the conjugate, with an interval from 1.36 to 4.98. Fifteen patients on the conjugate had locoregional progression before surgery, against none on chemotherapy18. Once surgery had happened, invasive disease-free survival was similar between the arms.

That is a failure mode peculiar to the neoadjuvant window. The tumour is still in the patient, so an underpowered regimen does not merely fail to shrink it. It permits local progression during the months the regimen is running. A de-escalation that would be acceptable after surgery is not automatically acceptable before it.

PHERGain-2 removed chemotherapy entirely from the preoperative phase and reported a high response rate. Read the eligibility before the rate, since entry required immunohistochemistry 3+, node-negative disease, and a tumour of 5 to 30 mm on magnetic resonance imaging14. That is a population selected for exactly the features that predict response. There is no comparator arm and the efficacy coprimary endpoint has not reported.

The equivalent question in hormone receptor-positive HER2-positive disease was tested directly by WSG TP-II, where replacing the taxane with endocrine therapy more than halved the response rate, a result that belongs to Early triple-positive disease and Chemotherapy omission and the de-escalation questions specific to this group19.

Caution

Single-arm response rates from selected populations are not comparable across trials, and they are routinely compared. A 60% rate in immunohistochemistry 3+ node-negative disease and a 60% rate in unselected stage II to III disease describe different achievements, so compare eligibility criteria before comparing numbers.

Response-adapted neoadjuvant strategies in HER2-positive disease

Response-adapted designs differ in when they look, and the timing decides what they can claim.

PHERGain looks early, using metabolic imaging after two cycles to decide whether chemotherapy is added. That is escalation on a surrogate of a surrogate. Metabolic response at six weeks is not pathological response at surgery, and the two are separate measurements with separate error.

What PHERGain established should be stated precisely, because it is usually overstated. The three-year rate in the response-adapted group was measured against a fixed threshold rather than against a randomised control, and the chemotherapy comparator group against which it is now read contained 71 patients15. Grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse events occurred in 33% of the response-adapted group against 62% of the chemotherapy group. That is a promising strategy with a real toxicity advantage. It is not an established equivalence, and Designing de-escalation trials and the non-inferiority margin problem sets out the difference.

PHERGain-2 and CompassHER2 pCR look late, at the surgical specimen. Late adaptation is the safer architecture, because the tumour is already out when the decision is made. It also inherits the strongest evidence in the subtype, since the same logic run in the other direction is what licenses post-neoadjuvant intensification Post-neoadjuvant intensification for residual disease.

CompassHER2 pCR is the largest test of this, with more than two thousand patients given a taxane with trastuzumab and pertuzumab and chemotherapy omitted after a pathological complete response20. Note what its design can and cannot deliver, because it will report only the recurrence rate in the patients from whom chemotherapy was withheld. It cannot report what would have happened to those same patients had the chemotherapy been given, because nobody was randomised to receive it.

In practice

Use response-adapted de-escalation inside a trial where one is open. Outside one, say that the supporting evidence is single-arm and that the omitted treatment has no comparator.

Distinguish the two adaptation points in conversation, since imaging-guided omission during treatment carries a progression risk that pathology-guided omission after surgery does not Response-adapted treatment.

Do not let a metabolic response report substitute for a pathology report. The decision rules in Post-neoadjuvant intensification for residual disease act on the specimen.

Antibody-drug conjugates tested before surgery in HER2-positive disease

Two conjugates have been tested preoperatively and they gave opposite answers. The difference is not the conjugate but what each conjugate was asked to replace.

KRISTINE asked a conjugate to replace chemotherapy and the response rate fell Chemotherapy-free and de-escalated neoadjuvant regimens. DESTINY-Breast11 asked a conjugate to replace the anthracycline component while keeping the taxane and both antibodies, and response rose against the dose-dense anthracycline sequence. The same trial also gave the conjugate alone, without the taxane and antibodies, and that arm was clearly inferior to the anthracycline sequence21.

Those three arms make one statement, which is that a conjugate is an addition to the taxane and antibody backbone rather than a substitute for it. The payload delivers cytotoxic chemistry to antigen-positive cells and to their immediate neighbours, and the reasons that is not the same as systemic chemotherapy are in Antibody, linker, and payload design and Bystander killing and why it matters in heterogeneous tumors.

The toxicity comparison favoured the conjugate sequence and this is the strongest part of the result. Grade 3 or higher adverse events occurred in 37.5% of the conjugate sequence against 55.8% of the anthracycline sequence, and all-grade left ventricular dysfunction in 1.3% against 6.1%. Adjudicated drug-related interstitial lung disease was similar across the arms, at 4.4% and 5.1%21.

Caution

Event-free survival in DESTINY-Breast11 was 4.5% mature at the reported analysis, with a hazard ratio of 0.56 and an interval from 0.26 to 1.1721. Everything in Dual blockade and what the pathological complete response gains bought applies to that pairing. The response difference is measured, while the outcome difference remains a hypothesis with a wide interval. It is the outcome that justifies exposing a curable patient to a conjugate.

Neoadjuvant HER2 trials whose efficacy endpoints have not reported

Both awaited trials test omission rather than addition, and each will settle less than its size suggests.

CompassHER2 pCR will report recurrence-free survival in patients who achieved a pathological complete response and then had chemotherapy omitted20. A low rate will make omission defensible without measuring the loss, because there is no arm that kept the chemotherapy.

PHERGain-2 will report a recurrence-free interval after a fully chemotherapy-free preoperative phase in a selected population, where the same limitation applies with a smaller sample14.

Four further questions have no trial designed to settle them.

Whether the response advantage in DESTINY-Breast11 becomes an event-free survival advantage. That requires follow-up rather than a new trial, and the current maturity is too low to support a decision Conjugates in the neoadjuvant setting.

Whether any assay selects patients for chemotherapy omission better than clinical features do. Genomic response predictors are being reported alongside these trials20, and none has been validated prospectively as a tool for assigning treatment.

Whether omission is safe in the population where it is most tempting. Hormone receptor-positive HER2-positive tumours respond least often and are the group in which a chemotherapy-free regimen is most often proposed Lower pathological complete response rates and what that does to the post-neoadjuvant decision.

Whether molecular residual disease after neoadjuvant therapy adds to pathology in deciding what comes next. The assay and its lead-time problem are in Molecular residual disease, tumor-informed and tumor-naive approaches and Lead time and the unresolved question of whether acting early helps.

Interplay

This chapter treats response as a property of a tumour and a drug. Part VI argues it is also a property of a population of cells with unequal antigen. A tumour scored HER2-positive on one core may contain a compartment that is not, and that compartment is what survives to become residual disease Consequences for pathologic complete response in the neoadjuvant setting. Read that way, the response rates in these tables are partly measurements of how uniform each enrolled population happened to be. It also explains why a bystander-capable payload changes the arithmetic in Conjugates in the neoadjuvant setting and a receptor-blocking antibody does not.
