Part X · Adjuvant and neoadjuvant therapy by subtype · Chapter 57

Early HER2-positive disease

The subtype where observed response, not predicted response, assigns the next year of treatment.

1 · Stage and risk-adapted treatment selection

Selection in this subtype turns on one question asked before any drug is given. Would knowing whether the tumour was eradicated change what happens next?

If the answer is yes, treat first and operate second. The post-neoadjuvant evidence in Post-neoadjuvant therapy for residual disease converts residual disease into a different regimen, and that option exists only if response was observed. If the answer is no, because the tumour is small and node-negative and the plan would be the same either way, then the neoadjuvant phase buys information nobody will act on.

That produces three bands in practice.

Small node-negative tumours, broadly up to 2 cm with clinically negative nodes, are treated with surgery first and a de-escalated adjuvant regimen De-escalation in small node-negative tumors.

Tumours of stage II or III, and any clinically node-positive disease, are treated with neoadjuvant systemic therapy. The backbone is in Neoadjuvant backbone and dual blockade and the response-adapted consequences in Pathologic response as a decision point.

Between those bands sits genuine uncertainty, and the commonest error is to resolve it with imaging alone. Clinical nodal staging is imperfect, so a tumour called node-negative before surgery may not be. Where that uncertainty is material, treating first preserves the option that surgery-first destroys. The general form of this reasoning is in Principles of multidisciplinary treatment and the staging limitations in Diagnostic evaluation and staging.

This chapter is the decision architecture. The randomised trial record it stands on is next door and is not restated here. Neoadjuvant regimens are in Neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy, with adjuvant and post-neoadjuvant regimens in Adjuvant and post-neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy. Hormone receptor-positive HER2-positive disease takes its own algorithm in Early triple-positive disease. This chapter carries only the part of it that changes a decision. 3 · Pathologic response as a decision point

Pathological complete response does two different jobs, and confusing them is the most common error in reading neoadjuvant trials.

The first job is prognostic, at the level of the individual patient. One pooled analysis covered 12 trials and 11,955 patients. Eradication of tumour from breast and nodes was associated with better event-free survival, with a hazard ratio of 0.48 and a 95% confidence interval of 0.43 to 0.54. For overall survival the hazard ratio was 0.36, with an interval of 0.31 to 0.4215. The association was strongest in HER2-positive, hormone receptor-negative disease treated with trastuzumab, which Scope, and why this subtype made the neoadjuvant platform work quantifies. Nowhere in breast cancer does response carry more information about an individual than here.

The second job would be surrogacy, at the level of the trial. In the same analysis, the correlation between a treatment's increase in pathological complete response and its effect on outcome was negligible. The coefficient of determination was 0.03 for event-free survival and 0.24 for overall survival, and the authors could not validate pathological complete response as a surrogate endpoint15.

Those two results are not in conflict. Patients who respond do better. Drugs that raise response rates do not reliably extend survival. The first is a statement about biology already present in the tumour. The second would be a statement about what a drug does, and it does not follow.

The definition matters for the first job. Eradication from breast and nodes, scored either as ypT0 ypN0 or as ypT0/is ypN0, was better associated with outcome than eradication from the breast alone. For eradication from the breast alone the event-free survival hazard ratio was 0.60, with an interval of 0.55 to 0.6615. A response report that does not state the nodal status is not reporting the quantity that carries the prognosis. Scoring conventions, including Residual cancer burden, are in Histopathology and the spectrum of breast neoplasia and the endpoint argument in full is in The neoadjuvant setting as a scientific platform.

The legitimate use of response in this chapter is therefore patient-level and observed. Nothing in Post-neoadjuvant therapy for residual disease depends on predicting who will respond. It depends on knowing who did.

The eligible populations are not the same, which is a distinction routinely missed. DESTINY-Breast05 required residual invasive disease together with node-positive disease at surgery, or inoperable disease at diagnosis16. A patient with small-volume residual disease and negative nodes was not studied in that trial and was studied in KATHERINE. Caution

The price is respiratory and it is not small. Adjudicated drug-related interstitial lung disease occurred in 9.6% of patients on trastuzumab deruxtecan against 1.6% on trastuzumab emtansine, and two patients with interstitial lung disease in the deruxtecan group died16. Overall survival in that trial is immature. So the current position is a large recurrence benefit, an unmeasured survival benefit and two treatment-related deaths from a toxicity that is detectable early. The surveillance that makes the difference between grade 1 and grade 5 is set out in Toxicity mechanisms, interstitial lung disease, ocular, and mucosal and should be in place before the first dose.

APT had no comparator. A single-arm study in a population with a very low event rate can show that outcomes are excellent. It cannot show that a shorter regimen is equivalent to a longer one, because there is no longer one in the dataset. The reason that distinction matters, and why de-escalation requires a stated margin rather than an impressive rate, is developed in Clinical trials and evidence interpretation and in Designing de-escalation trials and the non-inferiority margin problem.

Substituting a conjugate did not improve the trade. ATEMPT was powered on a protocol-defined composite of clinically relevant toxicity, which did not separate between the arms22. Matching totals are not the same as two regimens costing a patient the same things. Patients on the conjugate reported less neuropathy and less alopecia, which is a real difference hidden inside a null composite.

In practice

For a node-negative tumour of 2 cm or less, weekly paclitaxel with trastuzumab is the default and the evidence for it is a single-arm trial with an excellent rate23. Say that out loud rather than implying a randomised comparison exists.

Do not add pertuzumab in node-negative disease. The randomised trial that tested it found no benefit in that group24, and the evidence is in Adjuvant escalation and extended HER2-directed therapy.

Trastuzumab emtansine is an alternative where neuropathy or alopecia is the dominant concern, not where total toxicity is22.

The smallest tumours, T1a and T1b with negative nodes, sit outside even these datasets. Treatment there is extrapolation, and the conversation should say so.

7 · Hormone receptor-positive HER2-positive disease as a distinct algorithm

A tumour that is both hormone receptor-positive and HER2-positive carries two dependencies, and the treatment algorithm has to service both on different timescales. The trial record for this group is in Early triple-positive disease. This section carries only what changes a decision here.

The first consequence is that it responds differently to HER2-directed neoadjuvant therapy. In DESTINY-Breast11, the conjugate sequence gained 9.1 percentage points of pathological complete response over the anthracycline sequence in hormone receptor-positive disease, with an interval of 0.2 to 17.9. In hormone receptor-negative disease it gained 16.1 points, with an interval of 3.0 to 28.812. Both the absolute rates and the increment are lower when the oestrogen receptor is present.

The second consequence is that the benefit of HER2-directed escalation is not obviously smaller, which is counterintuitive. In the six-year APHINITY analysis the pertuzumab hazard ratio was 0.73 in hormone receptor-positive disease, with an interval of 0.59 to 0.92. In hormone receptor-negative disease it was 0.83, with an interval of 0.63 to 1.1024. An exploratory subgroup analysis of the same trial found the largest benefit in oestrogen receptor-positive tumours whose HER2 fluorescence in situ hybridisation ratio sat in the lower amplified range29. That analysis is hypothesis-generating and Subgroup evidence from the HER2 trials, and its limits reads it properly.

The third consequence is temporal. HER2-directed therapy finishes at one year. Endocrine therapy runs for five to ten, and the late recurrence hazard described in Late recurrence risk and surveillance implications applies to this population too. A patient who completes HER2-directed treatment has not completed treatment.

Interplay

Triple-positive disease is the clinical form of the two-receptor heterogeneity problem in HER2 heterogeneity and hormone receptor signaling in triple-positive disease. The compartment that survives HER2 blockade is not necessarily the compartment that survives oestrogen deprivation, and a report calling both receptors positive has averaged twice over a tumour that is uniform in neither. The lower pathological complete response rate in hormone receptor-positive disease12 is what that looks like from the clinic. It is also why residual disease in this subtype should not be read as HER2-directed failure alone, and why the conjugate literature in Antibody-drug conjugates and targeted delivery treats antigen level and dependence as separate variables.

Preoperative regimens that remove conventional cytotoxic chemotherapy. Compare the eligibility criteria before comparing the response rates.

The first line is direct and it sits in the dual-blockade table in Neoadjuvant backbone and dual blockade rather than in this one. NeoSphere carried an arm giving pertuzumab and trastuzumab with no chemotherapy at all. That arm recorded serious adverse events in 4% of patients against 10% to 17% in the arms containing docetaxel7. The toxicity is the whole appeal and the response rate in the same row is the whole problem.

The second line adds endocrine therapy to the antibodies in hormone receptor-positive disease. WSG TP-II tested exactly that against weekly paclitaxel and more than halved the pathological complete response rate31. Five-year survival was high in both arms, with intervals wide enough that the arms cannot be separated. That is what 207 patients buys.

The third line uses imaging to decide who continues without chemotherapy. PHERGain routed patients on a centrally reviewed positron emission tomography response after two cycles. Those who did not respond switched to a chemotherapy-containing regimen32. Grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse events occurred in 33% of the response-adapted group against 62% of the chemotherapy group. Among responders who reached a pathological complete response, 1% had a grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse event.

Caution

PHERGain is read as showing that about a third of patients can safely omit chemotherapy. What it demonstrated is a three-year rate in a phase 2 trial whose primary endpoint for that group was a single-arm rate against a fixed threshold32. There is no randomised comparison against standard chemoimmunotherapy, and the comparator group contained 71 patients. That is a promising strategy, not an established equivalence, and Designing de-escalation trials and the non-inferiority margin problem explains what would be needed to make it one.

In practice

Anthracycline omission is the de-escalation with the strongest evidence and the clearest toxicity argument9,12. It should be the default question rather than an exception.

Chemotherapy omission remains a trial question outside a trial. Where it is used off protocol, say that the supporting evidence is phase 2 and response-adapted32.

In hormone receptor-positive disease, replacing the taxane with endocrine therapy more than halves the pathological complete response rate31. If the plan depends on observing a response, that matters.

9 · Cardiac surveillance and cumulative toxicity

Two cardiac toxicities run through this chapter and they behave differently. Conflating them produces the wrong surveillance schedule.

Anthracycline injury is dose-dependent and largely irreversible. In a prospective cohort of 2,625 patients receiving anthracycline-containing therapy with serial ejection fraction measurement, cardiotoxicity occurred in 9%. The median interval from the end of chemotherapy to the event was 3.5 months, and 98% of events occurred within the first year. With heart failure therapy, 11% of affected patients recovered fully and 71% recovered partially. Two variables predicted the event independently. Ejection fraction at the end of chemotherapy carried a hazard ratio of 1.37 for each one-point decrement. Cumulative doxorubicin dose carried a hazard ratio of 1.09 for each 50 mg per square metre35.

HER2-directed injury is not dose-dependent in the same way and is usually reversible on stopping the drug. In the randomised adjuvant setting the rates are low. Primary cardiac events remained below 1% in both arms of APHINITY24.

The strongest cardiac intervention available is not surveillance. It is avoiding the anthracycline where the evidence permits, which is where the toxicity half of Neoadjuvant backbone and dual blockade gets priced. In TRAIN-2, an ejection fraction fall of 10 points or more to below 50% occurred in 7.7% of patients given an anthracycline and 3.2% of those who were not9. In DESTINY-Breast11, all-grade left ventricular dysfunction occurred in 6.1% of the anthracycline arm against 0.7% and 1.3% in the two conjugate-containing arms12.

Baseline risk stratification, monitoring schedules and the management of asymptomatic ejection fraction decline are codified in36, and the survivorship-phase management is in Treatment toxicity and supportive care.

In practice

Measure ejection fraction before the first anthracycline dose and again at the end of chemotherapy. The end-of-chemotherapy value was an independent predictor of subsequent cardiotoxicity, alongside cumulative anthracycline dose35.

Concentrate surveillance in the first year after treatment. That is when 98% of anthracycline events occurred35.

Start heart failure therapy when a decline is detected rather than when symptoms appear. Full or partial recovery occurred in 82% of affected patients treated on that basis35.

A fall on HER2-directed therapy usually warrants holding the drug and reassessing rather than abandoning it. A fall after anthracycline is treated, not observed35.

In a patient with cardiac risk factors, the anthracycline decision is the cardiology decision. The evidence that it can be omitted under dual blockade is randomised9.
