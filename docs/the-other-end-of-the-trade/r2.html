The maths on one page

Every formula, with where it is derived

This page collects the formulas the book relies on, grouped by part. Each one links to the numbered display where the book derives it or first works it with numbers, so you can go back to the reasoning when the formula alone is not enough.

Use it as a reminder, not a first introduction. A formula on its own hides its assumptions: the square-root-of-time rule needs independent returns, and Black-Scholes needs a volatility that does not change. The chapter behind each link states what the formula assumes and where it breaks.
