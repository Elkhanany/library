The Ages of Thought

Ten ages, and the people who argued their way from one to the next.

The same 2,600 years as The Long Argument, drawn rather than told. Ten ages down the left, time across the middle, and a line between two philosophers whenever one taught, read or argued with the other. It opens on the six who moved the most and widens from there.
