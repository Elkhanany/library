Part VI · Heterogeneity, evolution, and metastatic biology · Chapter 28

HER2 heterogeneity

Template chapter. It exists to exercise the citation, trial, cross-reference, and interplay machinery end to end. Replace the prose, keep the structure.

1 · Definitions and thresholds, spatial, clonal, and the ten percent convention

HER2 heterogeneity has no single definition. The working conventions describe different things and are not interchangeable. Keep the genetic and the protein-level claim apart from the first paragraph onward, because most published confusion collapses the two 1.

4 · Genetic and protein-level heterogeneity and their imperfect correlation

Amplification and overexpression are measured by different assays with different failure modes. The analytic validity framework set out in Receptor assessment and the measurement problem applies here without restatement.

5 · Amplicon topology, extrachromosomal DNA, and the mechanistic origin of instability

Extrachromosomal amplification segregates unequally at mitosis. That is a mechanism for heterogeneity rather than a description of it, and it predicts instability under treatment pressure, which is the argument carried forward in Temporal heterogeneity and clonal evolution.

11 · Implications for conjugate payload delivery and the bystander effect

Bystander killing is the reason a heterogeneous tumor can still respond. The payload chemistry that permits it is covered in Antibody-drug conjugates and targeted delivery and the clinical consequence in DESTINY-Breast04.

Interplay

HER2 heterogeneity, payload sensitivity, and bystander killing behave as one system. A tumor with low mean expression and high spatial variance is not the same problem as a tumor with low expression everywhere, even though the pathology report may read the same.
