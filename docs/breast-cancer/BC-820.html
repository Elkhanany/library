Part X · Adjuvant and neoadjuvant therapy by subtype · Chapter 58

Adjuvant and post-neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy

Two decisions that share a subtype and nothing else, and the trials that keep them apart.

1 · Scope, and the two distinct decisions this chapter serves

This chapter serves two decisions that share a subtype and almost nothing else.

The first belongs to a patient taken straight to surgery. The specimen is out and the pathology is final. No tumour in this patient was ever exposed to a drug under observation. Everything that follows is inferred from stage, nodal status, hormone receptor status, age and comorbidity. Sections The trastuzumab adjuvant trials and the duration question, Dual blockade in the adjuvant setting, De-escalated adjuvant therapy for small node-negative tumours and Extended adjuvant therapy after trastuzumab serve that decision.

The second belongs to a patient who received neoadjuvant therapy and has residual invasive disease at surgery. That tumour has already met HER2-directed therapy and survived it. The selection variable here is observed rather than predicted, which is why Post-neoadjuvant intensification for residual disease serves that decision and no other section does.

Merging the two is the commonest error in this subtype. The adjuvant trials randomised patients after definitive surgery, so their populations contain nobody whose response was known. The post-neoadjuvant trials enrolled only patients whose response was known and inadequate. A hazard ratio from one population does not transfer to the other.

Neighbouring chapters carry the rest, beginning with the choice between surgery first and treatment first in Early HER2-positive disease and Stage and risk-adapted treatment selection. Neoadjuvant regimens are in Neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy, with hormone receptor-positive HER2-positive disease taking its own algorithm in Early triple-positive disease. Conjugate design, resistance and toxicity are in Antibody-drug conjugates and targeted delivery and are cited here rather than repeated.

Read the tables this way, since each is generated from the trial registry at build time. The result and overall survival cells are taken from the publication named in the reference column, so a row and its citation always agree. Longer follow-up, subgroup structure and cross-trial comparison are argued in the prose.

The story below is the argument the rest of this chapter is evidence for, and it carries no figures at all. It states what each group of trials was built to find out, what came back, and what the design cannot settle, so the tables are read as answers to a question rather than as a list. Early disease · HER2+ · adjuvant

Matching adjuvant HER2 treatment to baseline risk

Before the antibody, HER2 amplification carried the worst prognosis in early breast cancer. It now carries one of the best, on a regimen whose chemotherapy backbone, whose duration and whose stopping point were all fixed in trials of node-positive disease. Most patients treated with it today sit well below that risk, so the questions at this position run along a single axis and run in both directions at once: what can be taken away at the bottom, and what has to be added at the top.

Does adding a year of trastuzumab to adjuvant chemotherapy lower recurrence?

Rationale
HER2 amplification marked the fastest-relapsing early breast cancer of the pre-antibody era. The antibody already worked against established metastatic disease. Micrometastatic disease left behind after surgery should be the easier target, being smaller and less heterogeneous than a measurable metastasis.
Experiment
Adjuvant programmes randomised trastuzumab against no trastuzumab on a chemotherapy backbone. They differ in whether the antibody ran concurrently with the taxane or began only after all chemotherapy, in whether the backbone contained an anthracycline, and in how long the antibody was continued.
Finding
Recurrence fell in every comparison. The separation was still open at ten years where follow-up ran that long. The anthracycline-free backbone gave disease control close to the anthracycline backbone with less cardiac dysfunction.
Limitation
A year of antibody was a protocol convention rather than a tested dose, so these trials establish that the antibody works without establishing how much of it is needed. The trial that compared the antibody against observation allowed its control group to cross over once the first results were public, so its long-term survival comparison is diluted by design. The two trastuzumab backbones in the anthracycline comparison were never set against each other under a non-inferiority design, so the absence of a significant difference between them is an absence of evidence and not a demonstration of equivalence. The short-course signal that prompted everything downstream came from a small HER2-positive substudy nested inside a randomisation of two different chemotherapies.

NSABP B-31/N9831 HERA BCIRG-006 FinHer

Can adjuvant trastuzumab be shortened below a year without losing disease control?

Rationale
The year was inherited from the pivotal protocols rather than derived from them. It carries cardiac risk, a year of infusions, and a cost that decides access in much of the world. The nested short course suggested the antibody might do most of its work early.
Experiment
Non-inferiority randomisations of six months against twelve, and of nine weeks against twelve months, each trial setting its own margin on its own scale. The pivotal observation trial had already tested the question from the other end by randomising a second year against the first.
Finding
A second year bought nothing over the first. Of the shorter comparisons, only one was declared non-inferior. The nine-week comparisons pointed against the short course, one of them with a point estimate a long way from equivalence. Cardiac events were consistently fewer on the shorter arms.
Limitation
These results do not stack, and estimates that look alike did not answer alike. The two six-month trials returned nearly identical hazard ratios and reached opposite verdicts, because one set its margin as a difference in percentage points of disease-free survival at a fixed time point and the other set its margin on the hazard ratio scale. The chemotherapy partners, and the point at which the antibody started relative to them, also differ across the four. None randomised duration inside a defined risk stratum, so failing non-inferiority in an unselected population says nothing about whether a small node-negative tumour needs the full year.
Next question
If the duration cannot be cut for everyone, can the chemotherapy be cut for the patients whose baseline risk is lowest?

HERA PERSEPHONE PHARE Short-HER SOLD

For a small node-negative HER2-positive tumour, how little chemotherapy is enough?

Rationale
The pivotal trials were built on node-positive disease and prescribed an anthracycline followed by a taxane. Small node-negative tumours were largely excluded from them. Those tumours still recur, so they are still treated, and they are asked to carry a toxicity that was justified against a much higher baseline risk.
Experiment
A single-group adjuvant course of a weekly taxane with trastuzumab in node-negative disease below a size cut-off. Then a randomised substitution of the antibody-drug conjugate for the taxane, with a protocol-defined composite of clinically relevant toxicity as a co-primary endpoint alongside disease-free survival. From the neoadjuvant side, a randomised test of whether early responders to dual blockade could be spared the taxane altogether.
Finding
Almost everyone given the short taxane-and-antibody course stayed free of invasive disease, and the rate held on extended follow-up. The conjugate gave comparable disease control without lowering the composite toxicity endpoint. Dropping the taxane from dual blockade lost pathological response, including among the early responders it was designed to protect.
Limitation
The single-group trial has no control arm, so its outcome measures the population selected into it as much as the regimen given to it. The substitution trial was powered for toxicity rather than for a disease-free survival comparison and allocated most of its patients to the conjugate, keeping only a small taxane comparator, so its outcome arms cannot establish equivalence. Its null toxicity result is also a statement about a composite: the totals matched, which is not the same as the two regimens costing a patient the same things. The taxane-omission question was settled on pathological response rather than on recurrence, in hormone receptor-negative disease only, with very few patients in each response group.
Next question
If the floor can be lowered this far for low-risk disease, does the ceiling need raising for node-positive disease?

APT ATEMPT WSG-ADAPT HER2+/HR−

Does adding pertuzumab to adjuvant trastuzumab and chemotherapy lower recurrence?

Rationale
A second antibody binds a different epitope and blocks the dimerisation that trastuzumab leaves intact. Neoadjuvant trials had already shown the pair raises pathological complete response above the single antibody. The adjuvant question is whether more response in the breast converts into fewer recurrences years later.
Experiment
One trial randomised pertuzumab against placebo on top of chemotherapy and a year of trastuzumab, in node-positive and high-risk node-negative disease. A second tested a different route to a dual block, randomising a kinase inhibitor given with trastuzumab or sequenced after it against trastuzumab alone. A third kept the dual block and replaced the taxane portion of the regimen with the conjugate.
Finding
The second antibody lowered invasive recurrence, with the gain sitting in the node-positive stratum and nothing to show in node-negative disease. The kinase inhibitor given concurrently fell short of its significance threshold, and the sequence added nothing at all. The conjugate substitution matched the taxane arm without beating it.
Limitation
The nodal split is a subgroup gradient inside one randomisation rather than a randomised comparison of two populations, and overall survival has not separated. The kinase inhibitor's concurrent arm was judged against a threshold tightened after a third arm closed for futility, so the same estimate reads as a narrow miss or as a small real benefit depending on which threshold is applied to it. The conjugate's null result is a failure to improve on an active control and not evidence of an inactive drug, and far fewer patients completed treatment on that arm, so it compares strategies as delivered rather than drugs as prescribed.
Next question
If a second antibody pays only at higher nodal risk, does a drug with a different mechanism help the patients still at risk once the year of blockade has finished?

APHINITY ALTTO KAITLIN

Does extending HER2-directed treatment beyond the first year with an oral kinase inhibitor add anything?

Rationale
Recurrences keep arriving after the antibody stops, and in hormone receptor-positive disease they arrive late. An irreversible pan-HER kinase inhibitor acts inside the cell and covers signalling the antibody does not, including the crosstalk with the oestrogen receptor that is one proposed route of escape.
Experiment
A randomisation of a further year of the kinase inhibitor against placebo in patients who had completed trastuzumab-based adjuvant therapy, stratified by hormone receptor status, with invasive disease-free survival measured at two years as the primary endpoint.
Finding
The extra year lowered invasive recurrence inside the two-year window. Severe diarrhoea was common and it arrived in the first weeks. By the final analysis the two arms had the same overall survival.
Limitation
A two-year endpoint is a short window for the very group the drug is now aimed at, whose recurrences come late, so the primary result is a measurement taken before the population it selects for has had time to relapse. Hormone receptor status was a stratification factor and not a randomised comparison of the strata. The trial enrolled before adjuvant pertuzumab and before conjugate treatment for residual disease, so almost nobody in it had received the treatment a candidate would receive today. No antidiarrhoeal strategy was part of the randomisation, so the tolerability on record is the tolerability of the drug given without one.
Next question
Every escalation here is allocated on nodal status and tumour size measured before a drop of treatment is given. Can the tumour's own response to treatment allocate it better?

Early disease · HER2+ · post-neoadjuvant

Escalating treatment on residual disease after HER2 blockade

Residual invasive disease at surgery is the strongest selection rule in early breast cancer, because it is measured rather than inferred. The tumour met HER2-directed therapy and was still there at operation. Every question in this position therefore concerns a population defined by a failure that has already happened, and the arguments turn on how much of that population each trial agreed to enrol.

For residual invasive disease at surgery, does switching to an antibody-drug conjugate lower recurrence more than completing the planned trastuzumab?

Rationale
Residual disease is observed resistance rather than inferred risk. The tumour met trastuzumab and a taxane and was still present at operation. Completing the same antibody asks a drug to finish work it has already been shown not to do in that patient. A conjugate keeps the target and changes the mechanism, by delivering a cytotoxic to a cell the antibody can still find. Escalation on residual disease had already lowered recurrence in HER2-negative disease, so the principle was not new when it reached this subtype.
Experiment
KATHERINE randomised patients with residual invasive disease in the breast or axilla after neoadjuvant taxane-based chemotherapy with trastuzumab to a conjugate or to completing trastuzumab, with invasive disease-free survival as the endpoint. Beside it, KAITLIN gave the same conjugate to unselected patients after surgery in place of the taxane, which is the same drug asked to work without the selection rule.
Finding
In selected residual disease the conjugate lowered invasive recurrence against continuing the antibody. Follow-up ran long enough for that recurrence benefit to become a survival benefit, which is uncommon in this setting. In the unselected adjuvant population the same conjugate improved nothing, and fewer patients finished the planned course.
Limitation
The control arm was the drug the tumour had already grown through. What the trial establishes is that a conjugate beats continuing an agent known to have failed in that patient, not that it beats any other escalation that might have been offered. The entry rule also rested on what counted as adequate neoadjuvant therapy, and that was never standardised. Most patients were treated before pertuzumab was routine, so residual disease in the trial followed a weaker regimen than residual disease in clinic today.
Next question
If the class is right, does a conjugate with a different payload beat the one that established the setting?

KATHERINE KAITLIN CREATE-X

Does a bystander-capable conjugate lower recurrence further than the conjugate that established this setting?

Rationale
The first conjugate carries a payload that stays inside the cell it enters, so it kills only cells that bind and internalise enough antibody. Residual disease after HER2 blockade is the compartment most likely to carry reduced or patchy antigen. A cleavable payload that crosses into neighbouring cells should reach what the first conjugate cannot, and a single-arm neoadjuvant study had already reported no responses at all to a non-bystander conjugate where HER2 was spatially heterogeneous.
Experiment
DESTINY-Breast05 randomised trastuzumab deruxtecan against trastuzumab emtansine in residual invasive disease, with entry restricted to node-positive disease at surgery or inoperable disease at diagnosis. The comparator was the established conjugate rather than the antibody, so this is the first trial in this position whose control arm was known to work.
Finding
The newer conjugate lowered invasive recurrence against an active comparator, by a margin larger than the one that established the older drug. Drug-related interstitial lung disease was far more frequent than on the older conjugate, and patients died of it.
Limitation
Recurrence was measured and survival was not, so a pulmonary toxicity that killed patients is currently being set against a surrogate. The enrolled population was narrower than that of the trial it displaced, because residual disease with negative nodes at surgery was not admitted. The antigen reasoning behind the whole comparison comes from a single-arm study whose heterogeneous and non-heterogeneous groups were prospectively defined but never randomised, resting on very few heterogeneous tumours.
Next question
Does the benefit reach the residual-disease patients the trial did not enrol?

DESTINY-Breast05 KATHERINE Neoadjuvant T-DM1-pertuzumab HER2-heterogeneity study

Does low-volume residual disease with negative nodes at surgery need the same escalation as residual nodal disease?

Rationale
Residual disease is a category rather than a quantity. A small deposit left in the breast with clear nodes carries a different hazard from disease still in the nodes after treatment. Trials have written that gradient into their entry criteria instead of testing it, which converts a biological question into an eligibility question.
Experiment
Read the entry criteria against each other rather than the results. The first conjugate trial admitted any residual invasive disease in the breast or axilla. The trial of the newer conjugate required node-positive disease at surgery or inoperable disease at diagnosis. The two randomised additions still running narrow entry further, to oestrogen receptor-negative or node-positive disease in one case and to high-risk residual disease in the other.
Finding
The randomised record is dense where risk is highest and thin where it is lowest. For residual disease with negative nodes, the only randomised evidence is still the older comparison against the antibody. The newer conjugate is untested in exactly the group whose absolute risk is smallest.
Limitation
Nodal status after neoadjuvant therapy is a staging convention as much as a biological variable. It depends on how the axilla was sampled and on how many nodes were examined, so a patient called node-negative after a sentinel procedure has not had the same test as one after a dissection. No trial randomised the threshold itself. The line between escalating and not escalating is an eligibility rule that was chosen once and then inherited.

KATHERINE DESTINY-Breast05 CompassHER2 RD ASTEFANIA

Can adding a second agent to the post-neoadjuvant conjugate lower recurrence further, and can it reach the brain?

Rationale
Once systemic control improves in this subtype, a large share of first relapses arrive in the brain, and a conjugate is a poor drug for that site. A small molecule with intracranial activity in metastatic disease is the obvious partner. Residual disease has also survived in an intact immune context, which is the argument for adding a checkpoint antibody instead.
Experiment
Two randomised trials add an agent to the established conjugate in residual disease. One adds tucatinib against placebo in oestrogen receptor-negative or node-positive disease, with brain metastasis-free survival among its outcomes. The other adds atezolizumab against placebo in high-risk residual disease.
Finding
Neither has reported. The only publication on the record of the immunotherapy trial is its design protocol, so nothing there says what the trial showed, and the enrolment figure attached to it is a plan rather than a count of patients randomised.
Limitation
Both trials were designed when the established conjugate was the only post-neoadjuvant option. A positive result will therefore show that an addition improves on a backbone that a newer conjugate has already beaten in node-positive disease. The comparison a clinic needs is between those two strategies, and no trial is making it. The immunotherapy arm also carries a warning from the preoperative setting, where a checkpoint antibody added to chemotherapy and dual blockade did not raise pathological complete response in this subtype.

CompassHER2 RD HER2CLIMB ASTEFANIA IMpassion050

After the post-neoadjuvant conjugate is completed, is there anything more to offer a patient whose risk continues?

Rationale
Recurrence in this population goes on after the planned cycles of conjugate end, so the escalation stops while the hazard is still running. Extending oral pan-HER inhibition for a further year is the only continuation that has been randomised in this subtype.
Experiment
ExteNET randomised a year of neratinib against placebo after completion of trastuzumab-based adjuvant therapy, stratified by hormone receptor status. The population was defined by having finished trastuzumab rather than by residual disease, so patients with residual disease sit inside the trial as a fraction of it.
Finding
Extension lowered invasive recurrence against placebo over the first years. The effect was concentrated in hormone receptor-positive disease, long-term survival was no different between the arms, and severe diarrhoea was common in a trial run without antidiarrhoeal prophylaxis.
Limitation
The trial predates routine pertuzumab and predates post-neoadjuvant conjugates entirely, so nobody in it had received the treatment that now defines this position. The hormone receptor restriction that governs current use is a comparison between two strata rather than a randomised comparison, however well balanced those strata were. Eligibility was also narrowed partway through accrual, so the population enrolled is not the one the protocol opened with.
Next question
If everything available after surgery is constrained by what was given before it, does a better preoperative regimen dissolve this question or only move it?

ExteNET KATHERINE DESTINY-Breast05

Does a stronger preoperative regimen remove the population this whole strategy was built for?

Rationale
Every question in this position presupposes residual disease at surgery. The preoperative regimen decides how many patients arrive with it and which ones they are. It also decides which drugs remain unused, because a conjugate spent before surgery is not available after it.
Experiment
Three trials move that boundary in different directions. DESTINY-Breast11 randomised a conjugate given alone, or followed by the taxane with dual antibody, against dose-dense anthracycline chemotherapy followed by the same triplet. CompassHER2 pCR gave a chemotherapy-light preoperative regimen in a single arm and omitted further chemotherapy after a pathological complete response. KRISTINE substituted a conjugate with dual blockade for chemotherapy altogether.
Finding
A conjugate-containing preoperative regimen raised pathological complete response against the anthracycline standard, which shrinks the residual-disease population. The chemotherapy-light regimen left most patients with residual disease, and those patients were predominantly oestrogen receptor-positive. Substituting the conjugate for chemotherapy lowered pathological complete response and therefore enlarged the residual-disease population, at much lower acute toxicity.
Limitation
All three moved a surrogate. Pathological complete response is the endpoint that reports first, the event-free survival of the conjugate trial was immature, and the response-adapted trial has not yet reported its recurrence endpoint at all. Nothing has randomised what to do about residual disease that persisted through a preoperative conjugate. Every post-neoadjuvant trial in this position enrolled tumours that survived an antibody and a taxane, and a tumour that survives trastuzumab deruxtecan before surgery is a different population with no evidence behind it.
Next question
Does residual disease mean the same thing when the regimen it survived has changed, and should the escalation rule be written in terms of what the tumour was exposed to rather than what is left in the specimen?

Adjuvant trastuzumab and the four trials that tested less than one year

The registration trials settled that adjuvant trastuzumab works without settling how long to give it. One year was a design assumption that became a standard by repetition, and it has since been tested in both directions.

Longer is now closed. HERA, the only trial that randomised a second year, gained nothing on disease-free survival while costing cardiac function. A fall in left ventricular ejection fraction occurred in 7.2% of the two-year group and 4.1% of the one-year group, and grade 3 or 4 adverse events in 20.4% against 16.3%8.

Shorter is not closed, and the four trials that tested it disagree. Read their margins before their results.

PERSEPHONE allowed three percentage points of four-year disease-free survival. Its control arm had an event rate near one in ten, so the margin permits that rate to rise by about a third in relative terms. PHARE allowed a hazard ratio of 1.15, Short-HER allowed 1.29 and SOLD allowed 1.3. Only PERSEPHONE declared non-inferiority against the margin it had set.

Now put the point estimates beside the margins. PHARE observed 1.08 and did not declare non-inferiority, while PERSEPHONE observed 1.07 and did. Two trials asking one question in broadly similar populations reached opposite conclusions on nearly identical effect estimates. What separated them was the margin and the scale on which it was set. A conclusion that flips on the margin is a conclusion about the margin, and Designing de-escalation trials and the non-inferiority margin problem sets out how to read one.

Nine weeks is a different proposition from six months. Both trials of nine weeks failed to exclude their margins, and SOLD's point estimate sat outside its own margin entirely.

In practice

One year remains the default on evidence that is a design convention validated by failure to improve on it, rather than a trial that compared durations prospectively at the outset.

Six months is defensible where cardiac risk, frailty or logistics make twelve unattractive. The supporting trial declared non-inferiority against a margin permitting a relative rise of roughly a third in events, which is a sentence the patient should hear1.

Nine weeks is for a patient who has already had a cardiac event on treatment, or who has very low baseline risk. Neither trial of nine weeks demonstrated non-inferiority3,4.

None of the four duration trials used dual blockade, and most predate routine pertuzumab. A duration result obtained under trastuzumab alone does not license a shortened course under trastuzumab and pertuzumab.

Adding a second HER2-directed agent to adjuvant trastuzumab

Three ways of adding a second HER2-directed agent have been tested after surgery, of which one produced a small benefit and two produced none.

APHINITY is the one that worked, and its benefit should be described honestly. The whole-trial absolute difference at the primary analysis was under one percentage point. Longer follow-up did not widen it much. At six years the node-positive cohort gained about five percentage points of invasive disease-free survival, and the node-negative cohort gained nothing at all12. Overall survival did not cross its significance boundary.

That split is the whole clinical content of the trial. Nodal status is not a subgroup here in the usual weak sense. It is the variable that decides whether the drug has any measurable effect, and it was a stratification factor rather than a post-hoc slice.

ALTTO is the instructive failure, because its drug pair had raised pathological complete response substantially in the neoadjuvant setting. The adjuvant trial then enrolled more than eight thousand patients and missed its threshold. That sequence is the subject of Dual blockade and what the pathological complete response gains bought and its methodology is in Endpoints, surrogates, and the overall survival question.

KAITLIN tested a different idea, which was substituting a conjugate for the taxane rather than adding an agent. Invasive disease-free survival was unchanged in both coprimary populations. Completion of the planned 18 cycles fell from 88.4% to 65.0%, largely through discontinuation for laboratory abnormality9. A regimen that two patients in five do not finish is a different regimen from the one tested.

In practice

Add pertuzumab for node-positive disease, quoting the absolute figure rather than the hazard ratio, because the relative effect flatters it Absolute and relative benefit, and why the difference matters.

Do not add pertuzumab for node-negative disease, where the randomised evidence shows no benefit at all12.

Do not substitute trastuzumab emtansine for the taxane after an anthracycline. It did not improve outcome and it was completed by far fewer patients9.

De-escalated adjuvant regimens for stage I HER2-positive disease

Patients with small node-negative tumours were largely excluded from the registration trials15. The regimen used for them was therefore built rather than inherited, and the way it was built constrains what can be claimed for it.

APT has no comparator, so a single-arm trial in a population with a very low event rate can show only that outcomes are excellent. It cannot show that a shorter regimen is equivalent to a longer one, because the longer one is absent from the dataset. No margin was stated and none could have been tested.

ATEMPT was randomised, and its primary endpoint was toxicity rather than efficacy. It was designed to detect a difference in clinically relevant toxicity, not to exclude a loss of efficacy. The 3:1 allocation left roughly 120 patients in the comparator arm. An efficacy non-inferiority claim from that arm is not available at any margin a clinician would accept.

The honest position is therefore narrow. Twelve weeks of paclitaxel with trastuzumab produces excellent outcomes in node-negative tumours of 3 cm or less, and that is the whole of what has been shown. Trastuzumab emtansine produces excellent outcomes in stage I disease with a different toxicity profile rather than a smaller one. Neither statement is a demonstration of non-inferiority against a full regimen, and Designing de-escalation trials and the non-inferiority margin problem explains what such a demonstration would require.

Caution

An impressive event-free rate in a low-risk population is not evidence of equivalence. It is mostly evidence that the population was low risk. The rate would also have been high on a longer regimen, and nothing in either trial measures the gap between the two. Treat the phrase "non-inferior" as unavailable here, because no margin was ever set.

In practice

For a node-negative tumour of 2 cm or less, weekly paclitaxel with trastuzumab is the default14. Describe the evidence as a single-arm trial with an excellent rate.

Choose trastuzumab emtansine where neuropathy or alopecia dominates the conversation rather than where total toxicity does, since clinically relevant toxicity was not reduced13.

T1a and T1b node-negative tumours sit outside even these datasets. Treatment there is extrapolation, and the consultation should say so.

Post-neoadjuvant therapy for residual invasive HER2-positive disease

Residual invasive disease after adequate neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy is the strongest selection rule available in early breast cancer. It identifies a tumour that met the drug and survived it. That is a measured property of this tumour rather than a prediction about tumours like it.

KATHERINE is now mature and the maturity matters. At a median follow-up of 8.4 years, seven-year invasive disease-free survival was 80.8% with the conjugate against 67.1% with trastuzumab. The hazard ratio for death was 0.66 with an interval of 0.51 to 0.87, and seven-year overall survival was 89.1% against 84.4%20. An early recurrence benefit was followed long enough to become a survival benefit, which is uncommon in this book.

DESTINY-Breast05 replaced the comparator with the drug KATHERINE established. Its eligible population is narrower and this is routinely missed. Entry required residual invasive disease together with node-positive disease at surgery, or inoperable disease at diagnosis16. A patient with small-volume node-negative residual disease was studied in KATHERINE and was not studied in DESTINY-Breast05.

Caution

The recurrence benefit in DESTINY-Breast05 is large and the price is respiratory. Adjudicated drug-related interstitial lung disease occurred in 9.6% of patients on trastuzumab deruxtecan against 1.6% on trastuzumab emtansine, and two patients with it died16. Overall survival is immature, so the current position is a measured recurrence benefit, an unmeasured survival benefit, and two treatment-related deaths from a toxicity that is detectable early. The surveillance that separates grade 1 from grade 5 is in Toxicity mechanisms, interstitial lung disease, ocular, and mucosal and belongs in place before the first dose.

In practice

Ask one question before choosing the agent: was the disease node-positive at surgery, or inoperable at diagnosis? If yes, the patient matches the DESTINY-Breast05 population. If no, the patient matches KATHERINE and the randomised evidence for the newer conjugate does not cover them.

Adequate neoadjuvant therapy is part of the selection rule. Residual disease after an inadequate regimen is a different finding, because the tumour was never properly tested.

Score the specimen so that the rule can be applied. Nodal status after treatment is the variable both trials selected on, and the conventions are in Pathological assessment after neoadjuvant treatment.

Extended adjuvant HER2-directed therapy after completion of trastuzumab

ExteNET added a year of oral pan-HER inhibition after a year of trastuzumab. The five-year analysis gave a hazard ratio of 0.73 with an interval of 0.57 to 0.92, and five-year invasive disease-free survival of 90.2% against 87.7%22.

State the trade plainly, because it comes to about two and a half percentage points of five-year invasive disease-free survival. The price was grade 3 diarrhoea in 40% of patients, against 2% on placebo, in a trial conducted without antidiarrhoeal prophylaxis21,22.

The hormone receptor-positive subgroup is where this drug is now used, and the claim needs handling. Hormone receptor status was a randomisation stratification factor, so the subgroups are balanced. Balance is not the issue, because a comparison between two subgroups is not itself a randomised comparison, and a difference in effect across strata carries far less weight than the overall result. Treat the restriction as a plausible reading of a stratified analysis rather than as a demonstrated interaction.

A second feature of the trial constrains the population further. Eligibility was amended during recruitment from stage 1 to 3c down to stage 2 to 3c22. The enrolled population therefore changed partway through accrual, so a reader applying the result should be clear which population they mean.

Caution

ExteNET predates routine adjuvant pertuzumab and predates post-neoadjuvant conjugates entirely. A patient who has completed trastuzumab emtansine or trastuzumab deruxtecan for residual disease was not represented in it. Extending with neratinib after a conjugate is extrapolation, and the toxicity is not.

7 · Cardiac safety across the adjuvant trials

The two cardiac toxicities in this chapter behave differently and the mechanisms are set out in Cardiac surveillance and cumulative toxicity. Surveillance schedules and the management of asymptomatic decline are in Treatment toxicity and supportive care and Cardio-oncology, surveillance and prevention. This section adds only what the adjuvant trials themselves settle.

The duration trials are the cleanest cardiac dataset in breast oncology, because they randomised exposure time rather than observing it. HERA randomised a second year, with cardiac events rising alongside it. Secondary cardiac endpoints occurred in 7.3% of the two-year group, 4.4% of the one-year group and 0.9% of the observation group5. PERSEPHONE randomised half the exposure and early discontinuation for cardiotoxicity fell from 8% to 3%1. Short-HER randomised nine weeks and reported a cardiac event risk ratio of 0.33, with an interval of 0.22 to 0.503. SOLD reported better maintenance of ejection fraction with nine weeks4.

Read those four results together. Trastuzumab cardiotoxicity is usually described as idiosyncratic and reversible, in contrast to the dose-dependent anthracycline injury. The duration trials show that exposure time behaves as a dose. That is a randomised dose-response relationship, and it is the reason a shortened course is a genuine cardiac intervention rather than a concession.

The anthracycline is the other lever and it was tested directly. In BCIRG-006, congestive heart failure and cardiac dysfunction were significantly more frequent with the anthracycline and trastuzumab regimen than with docetaxel, carboplatin and trastuzumab. Seven of the eight reported leukaemias arose in anthracycline-containing groups7.

In practice

Treat planned trastuzumab duration as a modifiable cardiac exposure in a patient with cardiac risk factors. The evidence that shorter means safer is randomised in four trials.

Treat anthracycline omission as the larger cardiac decision, since a non-anthracycline regimen with trastuzumab was not less effective in BCIRG-006 and was clearly less cardiotoxic7.

Measure ejection fraction before the anthracycline and again at the end of chemotherapy, and concentrate surveillance in the first year Cardiac surveillance and cumulative toxicity.

A fall on HER2-directed therapy warrants holding the drug and reassessing. A fall after an anthracycline is treated rather than observed.

Adjuvant and post-neoadjuvant HER2 trials still running

CompassHER2 RD asks whether adding tucatinib to trastuzumab emtansine improves on trastuzumab emtansine alone in high-risk residual disease, with brain metastasis-free survival among its outcomes23. Its answer will arrive against a changed background, since it was designed when trastuzumab emtansine was the only established post-neoadjuvant option. DESTINY-Breast05 has since displaced that comparator in the node-positive population, so a positive result will still leave the comparison that matters unmade.

Four questions have no trial addressing them at all.

Whether the DESTINY-Breast05 benefit extends to node-negative residual disease. The trial excluded that group and there is no plan to study it separately.

Whether trastuzumab deruxtecan or trastuzumab emtansine with tucatinib is the better choice for a patient at risk of brain metastasis, a comparison that no randomised trial exists or is planned to make.

Whether a shortened trastuzumab course is acceptable under dual blockade. Every duration trial predates routine pertuzumab, and the cardiac argument for shortening is strongest in exactly the patients who now receive two antibodies.

Whether molecular residual disease can select post-neoadjuvant intensification better than pathology can. The assay question is in Molecular residual disease, tumor-informed and tumor-naive approaches and the lead-time problem in Lead time and the unresolved question of whether acting early helps.

One older question remains open on its own terms. Overall survival in APHINITY has not crossed its boundary at six years12. The trial continues, and a node-positive patient consenting to pertuzumab today is consenting on a recurrence benefit rather than a survival one.

Interplay

Both decisions in this chapter are attempts to act on a tumour that is no longer in the body. The adjuvant decision infers risk from a resected specimen. The post-neoadjuvant decision infers it from what a specimen looked like after treatment. Part VI argues that a single specimen misrepresents a heterogeneous tumour, and residual disease is where that argument bites hardest. The surviving compartment after HER2 blockade need not carry the antigen level the diagnostic biopsy reported HER2 loss as an acquired resistance mechanism. That is one reason a bystander-capable conjugate outperforms a non-bystander one in exactly this population, and the mechanism is in Bystander killing and why it matters in heterogeneous tumors rather than here.
