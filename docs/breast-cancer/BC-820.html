Part X · Adjuvant and neoadjuvant therapy by subtype · Chapter 58

Adjuvant and post-neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy

Two decisions that share a subtype and nothing else, and the trials that keep them apart.

1 · Scope, and the two distinct decisions this chapter serves

This chapter serves two decisions that share a subtype and almost nothing else.

The first belongs to a patient taken straight to surgery. The specimen is out and the pathology is final. No tumour in this patient was ever exposed to a drug under observation. Everything that follows is inferred from stage, nodal status, hormone receptor status, age and comorbidity. Sections The trastuzumab adjuvant trials and the duration question, Dual blockade in the adjuvant setting, De-escalated adjuvant therapy for small node-negative tumours and Extended adjuvant therapy after trastuzumab serve that decision.

The second belongs to a patient who received neoadjuvant therapy and has residual invasive disease at surgery. That tumour has already met HER2-directed therapy and survived it. The selection variable here is observed rather than predicted, which is why Post-neoadjuvant intensification for residual disease serves that decision and no other section does.

Merging the two is the commonest error in this subtype. The adjuvant trials randomised patients after definitive surgery, so their populations contain nobody whose response was known. The post-neoadjuvant trials enrolled only patients whose response was known and inadequate. A hazard ratio from one population does not transfer to the other.

Neighbouring chapters carry the rest, beginning with the choice between surgery first and treatment first in Early HER2-positive disease and Stage and risk-adapted treatment selection. Neoadjuvant regimens are in Neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy, with hormone receptor-positive HER2-positive disease taking its own algorithm in Early triple-positive disease. Conjugate design, resistance and toxicity are in Antibody-drug conjugates and targeted delivery and are cited here rather than repeated.

Read the tables this way, since each is generated from the trial registry at build time. The result and overall survival cells are taken from the publication named in the reference column, so a row and its citation always agree. Longer follow-up, subgroup structure and cross-trial comparison are argued in the prose.

Adjuvant trastuzumab and the four trials that tested less than one year

The registration trials settled that adjuvant trastuzumab works without settling how long to give it. One year was a design assumption that became a standard by repetition, and it has since been tested in both directions.

Longer is now closed. HERA, the only trial that randomised a second year, gained nothing on disease-free survival while costing cardiac function. A fall in left ventricular ejection fraction occurred in 7.2% of the two-year group and 4.1% of the one-year group, and grade 3 or 4 adverse events in 20.4% against 16.3%8.

Shorter is not closed, and the four trials that tested it disagree. Read their margins before their results.

PERSEPHONE allowed three percentage points of four-year disease-free survival. Its control arm had an event rate near one in ten, so the margin permits that rate to rise by about a third in relative terms. PHARE allowed a hazard ratio of 1.15, Short-HER allowed 1.29 and SOLD allowed 1.3. Only PERSEPHONE declared non-inferiority against the margin it had set.

Now put the point estimates beside the margins. PHARE observed 1.08 and did not declare non-inferiority, while PERSEPHONE observed 1.07 and did. Two trials asking one question in broadly similar populations reached opposite conclusions on nearly identical effect estimates. What separated them was the margin and the scale on which it was set. A conclusion that flips on the margin is a conclusion about the margin, and Designing de-escalation trials and the non-inferiority margin problem sets out how to read one.

Nine weeks is a different proposition from six months. Both trials of nine weeks failed to exclude their margins, and SOLD's point estimate sat outside its own margin entirely.

In practice

One year remains the default on evidence that is a design convention validated by failure to improve on it, rather than a trial that compared durations prospectively at the outset.

Six months is defensible where cardiac risk, frailty or logistics make twelve unattractive. The supporting trial declared non-inferiority against a margin permitting a relative rise of roughly a third in events, which is a sentence the patient should hear1.

Nine weeks is for a patient who has already had a cardiac event on treatment, or who has very low baseline risk. Neither trial of nine weeks demonstrated non-inferiority3,4.

None of the four duration trials used dual blockade, and most predate routine pertuzumab. A duration result obtained under trastuzumab alone does not license a shortened course under trastuzumab and pertuzumab.

Adding a second HER2-directed agent to adjuvant trastuzumab

Three ways of adding a second HER2-directed agent have been tested after surgery, of which one produced a small benefit and two produced none.

APHINITY is the one that worked, and its benefit should be described honestly. The whole-trial absolute difference at the primary analysis was under one percentage point. Longer follow-up did not widen it much. At six years the node-positive cohort gained about five percentage points of invasive disease-free survival, and the node-negative cohort gained nothing at all12. Overall survival did not cross its significance boundary.

That split is the whole clinical content of the trial. Nodal status is not a subgroup here in the usual weak sense. It is the variable that decides whether the drug has any measurable effect, and it was a stratification factor rather than a post-hoc slice.

ALTTO is the instructive failure, because its drug pair had raised pathological complete response substantially in the neoadjuvant setting. The adjuvant trial then enrolled more than eight thousand patients and missed its threshold. That sequence is the subject of Dual blockade and what the pathological complete response gains bought and its methodology is in Endpoints, surrogates, and the overall survival question.

KAITLIN tested a different idea, which was substituting a conjugate for the taxane rather than adding an agent. Invasive disease-free survival was unchanged in both coprimary populations. Completion of the planned 18 cycles fell from 88.4% to 65.0%, largely through discontinuation for laboratory abnormality9. A regimen that two patients in five do not finish is a different regimen from the one tested.

In practice

Add pertuzumab for node-positive disease, quoting the absolute figure rather than the hazard ratio, because the relative effect flatters it Absolute and relative benefit, and why the difference matters.

Do not add pertuzumab for node-negative disease, where the randomised evidence shows no benefit at all12.

Do not substitute trastuzumab emtansine for the taxane after an anthracycline. It did not improve outcome and it was completed by far fewer patients9.

De-escalated adjuvant regimens for stage I HER2-positive disease

Patients with small node-negative tumours were largely excluded from the registration trials15. The regimen used for them was therefore built rather than inherited, and the way it was built constrains what can be claimed for it.

APT has no comparator, so a single-arm trial in a population with a very low event rate can show only that outcomes are excellent. It cannot show that a shorter regimen is equivalent to a longer one, because the longer one is absent from the dataset. No margin was stated and none could have been tested.

ATEMPT was randomised, and its primary endpoint was toxicity rather than efficacy. It was designed to detect a difference in clinically relevant toxicity, not to exclude a loss of efficacy. The 3:1 allocation left roughly 120 patients in the comparator arm. An efficacy non-inferiority claim from that arm is not available at any margin a clinician would accept.

The honest position is therefore narrow. Twelve weeks of paclitaxel with trastuzumab produces excellent outcomes in node-negative tumours of 3 cm or less, and that is the whole of what has been shown. Trastuzumab emtansine produces excellent outcomes in stage I disease with a different toxicity profile rather than a smaller one. Neither statement is a demonstration of non-inferiority against a full regimen, and Designing de-escalation trials and the non-inferiority margin problem explains what such a demonstration would require.

Caution

An impressive event-free rate in a low-risk population is not evidence of equivalence. It is mostly evidence that the population was low risk. The rate would also have been high on a longer regimen, and nothing in either trial measures the gap between the two. Treat the phrase "non-inferior" as unavailable here, because no margin was ever set.

In practice

For a node-negative tumour of 2 cm or less, weekly paclitaxel with trastuzumab is the default14. Describe the evidence as a single-arm trial with an excellent rate.

Choose trastuzumab emtansine where neuropathy or alopecia dominates the conversation rather than where total toxicity does, since clinically relevant toxicity was not reduced13.

T1a and T1b node-negative tumours sit outside even these datasets. Treatment there is extrapolation, and the consultation should say so.

Post-neoadjuvant therapy for residual invasive HER2-positive disease

Residual invasive disease after adequate neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy is the strongest selection rule available in early breast cancer. It identifies a tumour that met the drug and survived it. That is a measured property of this tumour rather than a prediction about tumours like it.

KATHERINE is now mature and the maturity matters. At a median follow-up of 8.4 years, seven-year invasive disease-free survival was 80.8% with the conjugate against 67.1% with trastuzumab. The hazard ratio for death was 0.66 with an interval of 0.51 to 0.87, and seven-year overall survival was 89.1% against 84.4%20. An early recurrence benefit was followed long enough to become a survival benefit, which is uncommon in this book.

DESTINY-Breast05 replaced the comparator with the drug KATHERINE established. Its eligible population is narrower and this is routinely missed. Entry required residual invasive disease together with node-positive disease at surgery, or inoperable disease at diagnosis16. A patient with small-volume node-negative residual disease was studied in KATHERINE and was not studied in DESTINY-Breast05.

Caution

The recurrence benefit in DESTINY-Breast05 is large and the price is respiratory. Adjudicated drug-related interstitial lung disease occurred in 9.6% of patients on trastuzumab deruxtecan against 1.6% on trastuzumab emtansine, and two patients with it died16. Overall survival is immature, so the current position is a measured recurrence benefit, an unmeasured survival benefit, and two treatment-related deaths from a toxicity that is detectable early. The surveillance that separates grade 1 from grade 5 is in Toxicity mechanisms, interstitial lung disease, ocular, and mucosal and belongs in place before the first dose.

In practice

Ask one question before choosing the agent: was the disease node-positive at surgery, or inoperable at diagnosis? If yes, the patient matches the DESTINY-Breast05 population. If no, the patient matches KATHERINE and the randomised evidence for the newer conjugate does not cover them.

Adequate neoadjuvant therapy is part of the selection rule. Residual disease after an inadequate regimen is a different finding, because the tumour was never properly tested.

Score the specimen so that the rule can be applied. Nodal status after treatment is the variable both trials selected on, and the conventions are in Pathological assessment after neoadjuvant treatment.

Extended adjuvant HER2-directed therapy after completion of trastuzumab

ExteNET added a year of oral pan-HER inhibition after a year of trastuzumab. The five-year analysis gave a hazard ratio of 0.73 with an interval of 0.57 to 0.92, and five-year invasive disease-free survival of 90.2% against 87.7%22.

State the trade plainly, because it comes to about two and a half percentage points of five-year invasive disease-free survival. The price was grade 3 diarrhoea in 40% of patients, against 2% on placebo, in a trial conducted without antidiarrhoeal prophylaxis21,22.

The hormone receptor-positive subgroup is where this drug is now used, and the claim needs handling. Hormone receptor status was a randomisation stratification factor, so the subgroups are balanced. Balance is not the issue, because a comparison between two subgroups is not itself a randomised comparison, and a difference in effect across strata carries far less weight than the overall result. Treat the restriction as a plausible reading of a stratified analysis rather than as a demonstrated interaction.

A second feature of the trial constrains the population further. Eligibility was amended during recruitment from stage 1 to 3c down to stage 2 to 3c22. The enrolled population therefore changed partway through accrual, so a reader applying the result should be clear which population they mean.

Caution

ExteNET predates routine adjuvant pertuzumab and predates post-neoadjuvant conjugates entirely. A patient who has completed trastuzumab emtansine or trastuzumab deruxtecan for residual disease was not represented in it. Extending with neratinib after a conjugate is extrapolation, and the toxicity is not.

7 · Cardiac safety across the adjuvant trials

The two cardiac toxicities in this chapter behave differently and the mechanisms are set out in Cardiac surveillance and cumulative toxicity. Surveillance schedules and the management of asymptomatic decline are in Treatment toxicity and supportive care and Cardio-oncology, surveillance and prevention. This section adds only what the adjuvant trials themselves settle.

The duration trials are the cleanest cardiac dataset in breast oncology, because they randomised exposure time rather than observing it. HERA randomised a second year, with cardiac events rising alongside it. Secondary cardiac endpoints occurred in 7.3% of the two-year group, 4.4% of the one-year group and 0.9% of the observation group5. PERSEPHONE randomised half the exposure and early discontinuation for cardiotoxicity fell from 8% to 3%1. Short-HER randomised nine weeks and reported a cardiac event risk ratio of 0.33, with an interval of 0.22 to 0.503. SOLD reported better maintenance of ejection fraction with nine weeks4.

Read those four results together. Trastuzumab cardiotoxicity is usually described as idiosyncratic and reversible, in contrast to the dose-dependent anthracycline injury. The duration trials show that exposure time behaves as a dose. That is a randomised dose-response relationship, and it is the reason a shortened course is a genuine cardiac intervention rather than a concession.

The anthracycline is the other lever and it was tested directly. In BCIRG-006, congestive heart failure and cardiac dysfunction were significantly more frequent with the anthracycline and trastuzumab regimen than with docetaxel, carboplatin and trastuzumab. Seven of the eight reported leukaemias arose in anthracycline-containing groups7.

In practice

Treat planned trastuzumab duration as a modifiable cardiac exposure in a patient with cardiac risk factors. The evidence that shorter means safer is randomised in four trials.

Treat anthracycline omission as the larger cardiac decision, since a non-anthracycline regimen with trastuzumab was not less effective in BCIRG-006 and was clearly less cardiotoxic7.

Measure ejection fraction before the anthracycline and again at the end of chemotherapy, and concentrate surveillance in the first year Cardiac surveillance and cumulative toxicity.

A fall on HER2-directed therapy warrants holding the drug and reassessing. A fall after an anthracycline is treated rather than observed.

Adjuvant and post-neoadjuvant HER2 trials still running

CompassHER2 RD asks whether adding tucatinib to trastuzumab emtansine improves on trastuzumab emtansine alone in high-risk residual disease, with brain metastasis-free survival among its outcomes23. Its answer will arrive against a changed background, since it was designed when trastuzumab emtansine was the only established post-neoadjuvant option. DESTINY-Breast05 has since displaced that comparator in the node-positive population, so a positive result will still leave the comparison that matters unmade.

Four questions have no trial addressing them at all.

Whether the DESTINY-Breast05 benefit extends to node-negative residual disease. The trial excluded that group and there is no plan to study it separately.

Whether trastuzumab deruxtecan or trastuzumab emtansine with tucatinib is the better choice for a patient at risk of brain metastasis, a comparison that no randomised trial exists or is planned to make.

Whether a shortened trastuzumab course is acceptable under dual blockade. Every duration trial predates routine pertuzumab, and the cardiac argument for shortening is strongest in exactly the patients who now receive two antibodies.

Whether molecular residual disease can select post-neoadjuvant intensification better than pathology can. The assay question is in Molecular residual disease, tumor-informed and tumor-naive approaches and the lead-time problem in Lead time and the unresolved question of whether acting early helps.

One older question remains open on its own terms. Overall survival in APHINITY has not crossed its boundary at six years12. The trial continues, and a node-positive patient consenting to pertuzumab today is consenting on a recurrence benefit rather than a survival one.

Interplay

Both decisions in this chapter are attempts to act on a tumour that is no longer in the body. The adjuvant decision infers risk from a resected specimen. The post-neoadjuvant decision infers it from what a specimen looked like after treatment. Part VI argues that a single specimen misrepresents a heterogeneous tumour, and residual disease is where that argument bites hardest. The surviving compartment after HER2 blockade need not carry the antigen level the diagnostic biopsy reported HER2 loss as an acquired resistance mechanism. That is one reason a bystander-capable conjugate outperforms a non-bystander one in exactly this population, and the mechanism is in Bystander killing and why it matters in heterogeneous tumors rather than here.
