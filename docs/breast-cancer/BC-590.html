Part XI · Metastatic disease: principles and cross-cutting management · Chapter 65

Expression and alteration-defined treatment across subtypes

What happens when a line drawn on a continuum becomes an eligibility criterion, and observers cannot reproduce the line.

1 · HER2-low and HER2-ultralow disease

Three categories now sit inside what the guideline still calls HER2-negative disease, and all three are assay outputs rather than entities.

HER2-low means immunohistochemistry 1+, or 2+ with negative in situ hybridisation. HER2-ultralow means immunohistochemistry 0 with faint incomplete membrane staining in a small proportion of cells. HER2-negative without qualification means everything below that. The categories were created by trial eligibility criteria rather than by a finding about biology1. HER2-low

Prevalence follows from where the lines were drawn. Roughly a third to a half of all breast cancers score HER2-low, and among HER2-negative tumours the proportion is higher in hormone receptor-positive than in triple-negative disease2. In one series of matched specimens, the proportion of all cases scored HER2-low was 34.2% on the primary and 37.3% on the relapse sample. Among HER2-negative cases it was 47.3% in hormone receptor-positive against 35.4% in triple-negative disease on primary samples3.

DESTINY-Breast04 established activity below the amplification threshold. Eligibility was immunohistochemistry 1+, or 2+ with negative in situ hybridisation, and immunohistochemistry 0 was excluded4.

DESTINY-Breast06 moved the line down again and created the ultralow category as a recruitment rule. Of 866 randomised patients with hormone receptor-positive disease after at least one line of endocrine-based therapy and no prior chemotherapy for metastatic disease, 713 had HER2-low disease and 153 had HER2-ultralow disease5. In the HER2-low population, median progression-free survival was 13.2 months with trastuzumab deruxtecan against 8.1 months with the physician's choice of chemotherapy. The hazard ratio was 0.62, with a 95% confidence interval of 0.52 to 0.75. Results were consistent in the exploratory ultralow population. Overall survival data were immature at that analysis.

The toxicity belongs next to the efficacy. Grade 3 or higher adverse events occurred in 52.8% against 44.4%. Adjudicated interstitial lung disease or pneumonitis occurred in 49 patients, which is 11.3%, three of those events being fatal, against one patient on chemotherapy5.

The drug also works below the eligibility line. In DAISY, confirmed objective responses occurred in 29.7% of patients whose tumours scored immunohistochemistry 0, and within that group ERBB2 messenger RNA above the median did not predict who responded6.

2 · Antigen thresholds as eligibility criteria, the general lesson

The structure of this problem recurs across the field, and it is worth setting out in the abstract before returning to HER2.

A drug's activity varies continuously with a target that is continuously distributed across tumours. A trial needs a binary rule to decide who enters. So a cut-point is placed on the continuum. Everything downstream inherits it. The label, the reimbursement decision, the reported efficacy, the patient's access, and the number a pathologist has to produce all rest on that one line.

Three separate questions then get collapsed into one.

Does the drug work better at higher target expression? For trastuzumab deruxtecan the answer is yes. Confirmed response rates across the expression range were 70.6% in HER2-overexpressing disease, 37.5% in HER2-low disease and 29.7% in HER2 non-expressing disease6.

Is there a level below which it does not work? Not established. Activity at immunohistochemistry 0 is well above zero, and within that group the amount of messenger RNA did not separate responders from non-responders6.

Can observers reliably place a case on one side of the line? No. When 18 breast pathologists from 15 institutions scored 170 breast cancer biopsies, agreement on cases scored immunohistochemistry 0 was 25%, with a Fleiss kappa of 0.49. Agreement across the full four-category system was 28.8%. On the question the assay was built to answer, 3+ against not 3+, agreement reached 87.1%7.

That third answer is the one to say out loud rather than to imply. A treatment decision affecting a large number of patients now rests on an assay boundary that trained specialist observers reproduce about a quarter of the time. This is the clearest case in oncology of a therapeutic eligibility line drawn where reproducibility is poorest, in a setting where the evidence that the line marks a real difference in drug behaviour is weak.

The guideline is unusually candid about it. The 2023 ASCO and College of American Pathologists update affirmed the existing recommendations and declined to create new result categories, calling it premature to do so1. It acknowledged in the same document that the distinction between 0 and 1+ is now clinically relevant, because of the entry criteria of the trial that supported regulatory approval. The analytic validity framework is in Receptor assessment and the measurement problem and HER2-low and HER2-ultralow, entity or assay artifact. The same problem read as a heterogeneity problem is HER2-low and ultralow reframed as a heterogeneity problem.

The lesson generalises to every threshold of this kind. PD-L1 combined positive score at 10 governs first-line immunotherapy in triple-negative disease Immunotherapy-based strategies and PD-L1 assessment. An androgen receptor percentage governed eligibility in a trial that set its own threshold Androgen receptor and other subtype-directed targets. Ki67 governs risk categories Ki-67, proliferation indices, and interobserver reproducibility. In each case the same three questions apply, and in each case the third is the one nobody asks.

Trop-2 is the instructive counterexample, because somebody checked. In the biomarker analysis of ASCENT, sacituzumab govitecan outperformed chemotherapy across high, medium and low Trop-2 expression, so no threshold was imposed8. An antigen whose benefit does not require a cut-point does not acquire one.

Caution

A single immunohistochemistry 0 result is weak evidence that a patient is ineligible for a HER2-directed conjugate. Two legitimate actions follow and they differ from each other. Asking whether the slide was read with the 0 against 1+ distinction specifically in mind is a request that the assay answer the question being asked of it. Testing a different or more recent specimen is a request for a different sample. Repeating the same test on the same block until it crosses the line is neither, and the difference is whether a reason for the retest existed before it was ordered Discordant or changing biomarker results.

3 · Germline and somatic DNA repair alterations

Germline BRCA1 and BRCA2 pathogenic variants identify a population in which an oral targeted agent outperforms chemotherapy, in both hormone receptor-positive and triple-negative disease.

OlympiAD randomised 302 patients with germline BRCA mutations and HER2-negative metastatic disease to olaparib or to the physician's choice of single-agent chemotherapy9. Median progression-free survival was 7.0 months against 4.2 months, with a hazard ratio of 0.58, and the response rate was 59.9% against 28.8%. EMBRACA randomised 431 patients to talazoparib or single-agent chemotherapy, with median progression-free survival of 8.6 months against 5.6 months and a hazard ratio of 0.5410.

TBCRC 048 asked which other repair genes behave the same way, and the useful part of the answer is which do not. Among 54 patients, confirmed responses occurred in two groups only. Germline PALB2 alterations gave a response rate of 82% and median progression-free survival of 13.3 months. Somatic BRCA1 or BRCA2 alterations gave 50% and 6.3 months. No responses occurred in patients with ATM or CHEK2 alterations alone11.

That is the shape of the actionability gradient, and it should be read carefully. Membership of the category of DNA repair genes does not predict benefit. The specific gene does, and the difference between PALB2 and ATM is the difference between a treatment and none.

Homologous recombination deficiency scores are a different kind of measurement and a common source of error. They record genomic scarring accumulated in the past. Scars persist after repair function returns, so a tumour can score high while repairing normally at the moment of testing Homologous recombination deficiency. The score predicts response to platinum-containing neoadjuvant chemotherapy in triple-negative disease12, which is a validated use. It is not a measurement of current repair capacity.

Resistance follows the same logic in reverse. Secondary intragenic mutations restoring the BRCA2 reading frame confer resistance to platinum and to PARP inhibition alike13, because both exploit the same defect.

Germline testing carries a second purpose that somatic testing does not, which is the implication for relatives. That is a reason to order it early rather than when a treatment decision forces it Inherited susceptibility and familial breast cancer.

4 · Rare actionable mutations and fusions

Activating HER2 mutations occur in tumours without HER2 amplification and are genuine oncogenic drivers14. This is a different disease from HER2-positive breast cancer and it is routinely filed under the same heading, which produces the wrong drug.

The agent that works is an irreversible pan-HER kinase inhibitor rather than an antibody. In a basket study, neratinib produced responses across HER2-mutant and HER3-mutant cancers, with the rate depending on the specific allele15. The breast-specific cohort held 71 patients with hormone receptor-positive, HER2-mutant metastatic disease who had progressed on a CDK4/6 inhibitor. The 57 of them who received neratinib with fulvestrant and trastuzumab had an objective response rate of 39%, with a 95% confidence interval of 26% to 52%, and a median progression-free survival of 8.3 months. Those are rates on 57, not on 71. No responses occurred with fulvestrant alone or with fulvestrant and trastuzumab16. That last sentence is what attributes the effect to the kinase inhibitor rather than to the combination.

NTRK fusions are the tumour-agnostic example. Larotrectinib produced an overall response rate of 75% by independent review, with a 95% confidence interval of 61% to 85%, in 55 patients with 17 different TRK fusion-positive tumour types17. The relevance to breast cancer is limited by prevalence rather than by the size of the effect. These fusions are very rare outside secretory carcinoma, so the pre-test probability determines whether looking for them is sensible.

PIK3CA, AKT1 and PTEN alterations form the largest actionable group in this disease and they belong to Metastatic hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative disease, where the trials and the sequencing are set out18,19. ESR1 mutations belong there too, and the selection that produces them is described in Endocrine therapy and the emergence of ESR1 alterations.

The organising principle is that actionable is not a binary property. A tiering framework ranks alterations by the strength of the evidence linking them to benefit in a specific tumour type. The breast-specific application of that framework separates alterations with randomised support from those resting on case reports20.

The practical rule follows from prevalence rather than from biology. A rare alteration is worth looking for when a panel is already being run for another reason, or when histology raises the pre-test probability, as lobular histology does for HER2 mutation Invasive lobular disease in the metastatic setting.

5 · Tumor-agnostic biomarkers

A tumour-agnostic approval says that a biomarker predicts benefit across tissue of origin. It does not say that the biomarker is common in any particular disease, and in breast cancer that distinction does most of the work.

Mismatch repair deficiency was the first. Objective radiographic responses occurred in 53% of patients with advanced mismatch repair-deficient cancers across 12 tumour types treated with PD-1 blockade, and complete responses in 21%21. The proposed mechanism is that defective mismatch repair generates a large burden of mutant neoantigens.

High tumour mutational burden followed. A prospective biomarker analysis within a multi-cohort phase 2 study set the threshold at 10 mutations per megabase22. Objective responses to pembrolizumab occurred in 30 of 102 patients above it, which is 29%, against 43 of 688 patients below it, which is 6%.

That study is worth reading for what it did not include. The eligible tumour types were anal, biliary, cervical, endometrial, mesothelioma, neuroendocrine, salivary, small-cell lung, thyroid and vulvar22. Breast cancer was not among them. The tumour-agnostic claim reaches breast cancer by inference rather than by measurement, and that should be said when the option is offered to a patient.

NTRK fusions complete the set17, with the prevalence caveat in Rare actionable mutations and fusions.

The breast-specific arithmetic governs whether to test. High tumour mutational burden occurs in a small minority of breast cancers and is concentrated in particular contexts rather than distributed evenly23. Mismatch repair deficiency is rarer still. A test with a low positive rate is worth running as part of a panel ordered for other reasons, and is hard to justify as a standalone investigation.

One reading habit is worth acquiring here. When a tumour-agnostic result is quoted, look for the number of patients with the disease in front of you. The per-disease denominator inside a basket trial is frequently in single figures, and a response rate pooled across tissue types is not a response rate in any one of them.

6 · Discordant or changing biomarker results

A result that disagrees with a previous result has three possible explanations and they call for different responses. True biological change. Assay variation, including antibody clone, platform, fixation and scoring. Sampling, because two specimens are two different pieces of tissue Receptor discordance and conversion.

HER2-low is the most unstable of the categories now governing eligibility. Across 547 patients with matched primary and relapse specimens, overall HER2 discordance was 38.0%. Movement from HER2-0 to HER2-low occurred in 15% of cases and from HER2-low to HER2-0 in 14%3. Two movements of similar size in opposite directions are what measurement variation looks like. Some of it is certainly real change, and that design cannot separate the two components.

Hormone receptor and HER2 conversion between primary and distant metastasis is the better-characterised version of the same problem24.

Plasma and tissue disagree for reasons that are not error. A lesion that does not shed is invisible to plasma. A plasma result integrates across lesions and cannot say which one it came from. And variants arising in blood cells rather than in tumour appear in plasma unless matched white cell sequencing removes them25. The blind spots on each side are set out in Liquid biopsy and longitudinal monitoring.

The rule that follows is decision-theoretic rather than biological, and saying so is better than pretending otherwise. When two adequate specimens disagree and the result governs eligibility, acting on the one that opens a treatment option is usually right. The justification is asymmetric consequences, not a claim about which measurement was correct.

Caution

That rule sits one step away from testing repeatedly until a result crosses a threshold, and the two are not the same thing. The difference is whether a reason for the repeat existed before the repeat was ordered. A newer specimen, a different lesion, a change in disease behaviour and a laboratory reporting with the relevant boundary in mind are all reasons. Dissatisfaction with the previous result is not.

7 · Competing actionable targets and how to prioritize

The common situation is not an absence of targets. It is several at once. One report can carry a PIK3CA mutation, HER2-low expression, a germline BRCA variant and a fusion of unknown significance on the same page.

Five criteria order them, and applying them in sequence is better than weighting them informally.

Evidence level for that alteration in this disease. A tiering framework exists for exactly this purpose and it separates randomised support from anecdote20.

Magnitude and durability of the expected benefit, with the comparator named. A hazard ratio of 0.62 against the physician's choice of chemotherapy5 is not measured on the same scale as a response rate of 82% in a single-arm cohort of germline PALB2 carriers11. Ranking one against the other requires saying which quantity each is.

Whether the option is line-restricted. An agent available only before chemotherapy is lost by giving chemotherapy first, which is a reason to sequence it earlier rather than a reason to prefer it.

Toxicity against the patient's current function. Adjudicated interstitial lung disease occurred in 11.3% of patients receiving trastuzumab deruxtecan in one phase 3 trial, with fatal cases5. That is a different proposition in a patient with baseline lung disease than in one without.

Whether the alteration is truncal or subclonal. A target present in a fraction of cells leaves a compartment untouched, which is the argument in HER2 heterogeneity and Temporal heterogeneity and clonal evolution. Variant allele fraction in plasma gives a crude indication and tissue gives a better one.

A molecular tumour board exists to do this ordering, and the reporting standards that make it possible are in Reporting standards and molecular tumor boards.

In practice

Order the full panel once and early, rather than sequentially as options run out Biomarker-driven treatment selection.

Write the competing options as a list with the comparator attached to each, before discussing them with the patient.

Say which option is being deferred and why, so that the deferral is a recorded decision rather than an omission.

Re-check a panel result against the most recent specimen before acting on an alteration found years earlier Discordant or changing biomarker results.

Interplay

This chapter is the heterogeneity argument of Part VI arriving as an eligibility form. Every biomarker on that form is a summary statistic over a structured population. An immunohistochemistry score is a mean over cells that vary. A variant allele fraction is a proportion over clones that vary. A panel result is a snapshot of a population that is changing under the treatment the patient is currently receiving. The thresholds applied to those summaries were chosen for reproducibility and for trial design, not because biology changes at those values Definitions and thresholds, spatial, clonal, and the ten percent convention. Treating the form as a description of the tumour, rather than as a set of measurements with known failure modes, is the error this part of the book is written against.
