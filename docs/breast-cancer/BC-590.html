Expression and alteration-defined treatment across subtypes

What each expression threshold and each alteration actually buys, measured against the treatment it displaces.

1 · HER2-low and HER2-ultralow disease

Three categories now sit inside what the guideline still calls HER2-negative disease, and all three are assay outputs rather than entities.

HER2-low means immunohistochemistry 1+, or 2+ with negative in situ hybridisation. HER2-ultralow means immunohistochemistry 0 with faint incomplete membrane staining in a small proportion of cells. HER2-negative without qualification means everything below that. The categories were created by trial eligibility criteria rather than by a finding about biology1. HER2-low

Prevalence follows from where the lines were drawn. Roughly a third to a half of all breast cancers score HER2-low, and among HER2-negative tumours the proportion is higher in hormone receptor-positive than in triple-negative disease2.

The question underneath all of this is whether the amount of antigen selects anyone, or whether the antigen only has to be there. The story below states what each group of trials was built to find out, and it carries no figures at all.

Advanced disease · HR+/HER2- · later

Antibody-drug conjugates after endocrine therapy

Does the amount of antigen on the tumour select who benefits, or does the antigen only have to be present?

Rationale
A conjugate is named for its target. The natural expectation is therefore that more target means more benefit, and that below some level the drug stops working. HER2-low was written as an eligibility category for a trial rather than as a description of a biological class. TROP2 is expressed across almost all of this disease, which makes it a poor candidate for grading anyone.
Experiment
The HER2-directed conjugate was tested first in disease scored at the bottom of the conventional HER2 scale. It was then tested in disease scored below even that, including tumours read as negative with only faint membrane staining. Separately, a single-arm study gave the same drug in cohorts spanning the whole expression range, from overexpressing down to no detectable staining. The TROP2 trials did not select on expression at all. One of them measured TROP2 by immunohistochemistry, by immunofluorescence and in plasma, testing each measure against outcome.
Finding
In the group scored below the threshold that defined the conjugate's own registration trial, the result was consistent with the group in which that trial's primary comparison was made. Response falls as staining falls and does not disappear where staining is absent. The TROP2 results held across expression subgroups rather than concentrating in the high ones. Where TROP2 was measured by immunohistochemistry, by immunofluorescence and in plasma in one trial, no measure tracked outcome.
Limitation
HER2-low and ultralow are scoring conventions on an immunohistochemistry assay designed to find amplification. They are read at the bottom of that assay's range, where agreement between pathologists is at its worst. The category is a property of the assay before it is a property of the tumour. The cohort study spanning the expression range is single-arm. A lower response in a lower-expression cohort therefore carries no chemotherapy comparison inside that cohort and cannot say whether the advantage over chemotherapy differs by expression. The ultralow group in the trial that extended the threshold was exploratory. It sat alongside a primary analysis in the scored-low population rather than testing the threshold itself. No trial has yet randomised anyone on the score.

2 · Antigen thresholds as eligibility criteria, the general lesson

The structure of this problem recurs across the field, and it is worth setting out in the abstract before returning to HER2.

A drug's activity varies continuously with a target that is continuously distributed across tumours. A trial needs a binary rule to decide who enters. So a cut-point is placed on the continuum. Everything downstream inherits it. The label, the reimbursement decision, the reported efficacy, the patient's access, and the number a pathologist has to produce all rest on that one line.

Three separate questions then get collapsed into one.

Does the drug work better at higher target expression? For trastuzumab deruxtecan, response falls as the score falls, and the cohort gradient in the table above is the measurement4. That is a gradient in response rate across single-arm cohorts rather than a gradient in benefit over chemotherapy.

Is there a level below which it does not work? Not established. Activity at immunohistochemistry 0 is well above zero. Within that group the amount of ERBB2 expression did not separate responders from non-responders4.

Can observers reliably place a case on one side of the line? No. When 18 breast pathologists from 15 institutions scored 170 breast cancer biopsies, agreement on cases scored immunohistochemistry 0 was 25%, with a Fleiss kappa of 0.49. Agreement across the full four-category system was 28.8%. On the question the assay was built to answer, 3+ against not 3+, agreement reached 87.1%7.

That third answer is the one to say out loud rather than to imply. A treatment decision affecting a large number of patients now rests on an assay boundary that trained specialist observers reproduce about a quarter of the time. This is the clearest case in oncology of a therapeutic eligibility line drawn where reproducibility is poorest. The evidence that the line marks a real difference in drug behaviour is weak.

If the assay cannot place the case, the obvious response is to measure the target some other way. That has been tried, and what came back is argued in Heterogeneous or changing HER2 expression.

The guideline is unusually candid about the same problem. The 2023 ASCO and College of American Pathologists update affirmed the existing recommendations and declined to create new result categories, calling it premature to do so1. It acknowledged in the same document that the distinction between 0 and 1+ is now clinically relevant, because of the entry criteria of the trial that supported regulatory approval. The analytic validity framework is in Receptor assessment and the measurement problem and HER2-low and HER2-ultralow, entity or assay artifact. The same problem read as a heterogeneity problem is HER2-low and ultralow reframed as a heterogeneity problem.

The lesson generalises to every threshold of this kind. PD-L1 combined positive score at 10 governs first-line immunotherapy in triple-negative disease First-line chemoimmunotherapy and the PD-L1 threshold. An androgen receptor percentage governed eligibility in a trial that set its own threshold Androgen receptor and other subtype-directed targets. Ki67 governs risk categories Ki-67, proliferation indices, and interobserver reproducibility. In each case the same three questions apply, and in each case the third is the one nobody asks.

Trop-2 is the instructive counterexample, because somebody checked. In the biomarker analysis of ASCENT, sacituzumab govitecan outperformed chemotherapy across high, medium and low Trop-2 expression, so no threshold was imposed8. An antigen whose benefit does not require a cut-point does not acquire one.

Caution

A single immunohistochemistry 0 result is weak evidence that a patient is ineligible for a HER2-directed conjugate. Two legitimate actions follow and they differ from each other. Asking whether the slide was read with the 0 against 1+ distinction specifically in mind is a request that the assay answer the question being asked of it. Testing a different or more recent specimen is a request for a different sample. Repeating the same test on the same block until it crosses the line is neither, and the difference is whether a reason for the retest existed before it was ordered Discordant or changing biomarker results.

3 · Germline and somatic DNA repair alterations

Germline BRCA1 and BRCA2 pathogenic variants identify a population in which an oral PARP inhibitor outperforms chemotherapy. The two registration trials entered HER2-negative advanced disease and selected on the germline mutation alone.

The question that followed was how far out from those two genes the benefit extends. The story below is what the trials were built to settle, and it carries no figures.

Advanced disease · HR+/HER2- · later

Molecular exceptions to the endocrine sequence

Does PARP sensitivity extend beyond a germline BRCA mutation?

Rationale
The licence for PARP inhibition is written around germline BRCA1 and BRCA2. The mechanism it exploits is a failure of homologous recombination. BRCA is not the only gene whose loss produces that failure. A somatic mutation disables the protein as completely as an inherited one does. If the repair defect is what matters, germline status is a fact about the patient rather than about the tumour.
Experiment
Two single-arm trials went outside germline BRCA. One gave olaparib to a germline cohort of repair-gene carriers and to a somatic cohort. The other gave talazoparib to patients wild-type for BRCA1 and BRCA2 whose tumours carried a mutation in a different homologous recombination gene. Standing behind both are the randomised trials that established the class, which set a PARP inhibitor against single-agent chemotherapy of the physician's choice in germline carriers.
Finding
Activity did not spread evenly across the genes. It concentrated in germline PALB2 and in somatic BRCA, where the response looked like what germline carriers get from the same drugs in the randomised trials. The remaining repair genes contributed very little. A homologous recombination gene list is therefore not one biomarker. It is several biomarkers that do not behave alike.
Limitation
Both trials are single-arm. Both report gene by gene from cohorts of a few patients each. A gene that looks inert here is therefore as uncertain as a gene that looks active. The olaparib trial's registry row carries a BRCA subtype tag although most of its patients had hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative disease. The row reads as a germline BRCA result when it is not one. The repair deficiency scores that tracked response in the talazoparib study were measured in tumours already chosen for carrying one of these mutations. That correlation cannot show that a score would have selected anyone on its own. Neither trial randomised against the chemotherapy the germline trials used as their comparator. What the drug is worth against that alternative outside BRCA is unmeasured.
Next question
A marker confirmed only inside a population already selected on it is not yet doing the selecting. The next exception is not a mutation at all. It is a receptor present in most of these tumours that the field has spent its effort trying to block. Does its pharmacology run the other way here?

PARP inhibitor trials entered on a DNA repair alteration rather than on a line of treatment. The two registration trials sit alongside the studies that went looking for a wider eligible population.

OlympiAD and EMBRACA are read together and they agree12,11. In germline BRCA1 or BRCA2 carriers with HER2-negative disease, an oral PARP inhibitor delays progression and produces responses more often than the single-agent chemotherapy a physician would otherwise choose. Neither converted that into longer overall survival13,14. The EMBRACA investigators repeated the survival analysis with adjustment for later PARP inhibitor and platinum exposure, and the estimate moved towards benefit without excluding no difference. Crossover is a candidate explanation that the trials cannot test, and the general form of that problem is in PARP inhibition for germline carriers.

BROCADE3 reads as the weaker result in the table and is a different experiment9. Its control arm is carboplatin with paclitaxel. It therefore asks what veliparib adds to a platinum doublet, not whether PARP inhibition beats the alternative to chemotherapy. The dose was one a full-dose doublet tolerates, and it was raised only when chemotherapy stopped. A combination design of that shape trades target engagement for haematological headroom. Reading its hazard ratio beside OlympiAD's sets a drug-against-chemotherapy result next to a drug-added-to-chemotherapy result. The drug is not the variable that differs.

TBCRC 048 went outside germline BRCA, and the useful part of its answer is which genes did not respond10,15. Confirmed responses were confined to germline PALB2 and to somatic BRCA1 or BRCA2. Carriers of ATM or CHEK2 alterations alone had none. Talazoparib Beyond BRCA gave talazoparib to patients wild-type for BRCA1 and BRCA2 who carried a mutation in another repair gene. Its activity again concentrated in germline PALB216. A homologous recombination gene list is not one biomarker. It is several, and they do not behave alike.

Two cautions travel with that gradient. The cohorts hold a few patients per gene, so a gene that looks inert is as uncertain as a gene that looks active. And the TBCRC 048 row carries a BRCA subtype tag although most of its patients had hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative disease. The row reads as a germline BRCA result. That is a filing convention rather than a population.

What no reported trial here has done is randomise a PARP inhibitor against chemotherapy outside germline BRCA. Somatic BRCA and germline PALB2 are treated on response rates from single arms, against a comparator that exists only in the germline trials. EvoPAR-Breast01 does not close that gap, and it asks the question the clinic now has instead. It sets a PARP1-selective inhibitor with an oral SERD against a CDK4/6 inhibitor with endocrine therapy in the first line, in disease carrying a germline or somatic BRCA1, BRCA2 or PALB2 alteration. That is the comparison which decides whether a repair defect displaces the endocrine sequence or waits behind it. It has not reported.

Homologous recombination deficiency scores are a different kind of measurement and a common source of error. They record genomic scarring accumulated in the past. Scars persist after repair function returns, so a tumour can score high while repairing normally at the moment of testing. The score predicts response to platinum-containing neoadjuvant chemotherapy in triple-negative disease17, which is a validated use. It is not a measurement of current repair capacity.

Resistance follows the same logic in reverse. Secondary intragenic mutations restoring the BRCA2 reading frame confer resistance to platinum and to PARP inhibition alike18, because both exploit the same defect.

Germline testing carries a second purpose that somatic testing does not, which is the implication for relatives. That is a reason to order it early rather than when a treatment decision forces it Inherited susceptibility and familial breast cancer.

4 · Rare actionable mutations and fusions

Activating HER2 mutations occur in tumours without HER2 amplification and are genuine oncogenic drivers19. This is a different disease from HER2-positive breast cancer and it is routinely filed under the same heading, which produces the wrong drug. The agent that works is an irreversible pan-HER kinase inhibitor rather than an antibody. In a basket study, neratinib produced responses across HER2-mutant and HER3-mutant cancers, with the rate depending on the specific allele20.

Advanced disease · HR+/HER2- · later

Molecular exceptions to the endocrine sequence

Does an activating HER2 mutation make a HER2-negative tumour treatable with HER2-directed therapy?

Rationale
HER2 status is assigned by protein overexpression and by gene copy number. An activating mutation switches the receptor on without amplifying it. A tumour can therefore signal through HER2 and still score negative on the test that decides who is offered HER2-directed treatment. If the drug answers to the signalling rather than to the copy number, the conventional assay is looking for the wrong thing in these patients.
Experiment
Three mutation-selected cohorts gave HER2-directed treatment to disease that was HER2-negative on conventional local testing. One used neratinib with fulvestrant and trastuzumab. One used neratinib with fulvestrant, in cohorts split by previous fulvestrant exposure. One used tucatinib with trastuzumab, with fulvestrant added for the hormone receptor-positive patients. Selection was on the mutation in every case, never on amplification and never on protein expression.
Finding
Responses occur in all three. Some of them last. They appear across several different mutations of the receptor, in the kinase domain and in the extracellular domain, and in lobular as well as ductal disease. Selecting on the mutation identifies a treatable population that the amplification assay does not see.
Limitation
Every cohort here is single-arm apart from one small randomised substudy inside a basket trial. That substudy set the triplet against fulvestrant-based arms rather than against the chemotherapy these patients would otherwise be offered. The tucatinib cohort is one arm of a solid-tumour basket and reports its intervals at a lower confidence level than the rest of the registry uses. Its response rate therefore does not sit beside the others as a like comparison. Mutations are pooled for the reported result in each trial. One trial did report a class of kinase-domain substitutions that looked worse than the other mutations it enrolled, in an exploratory analysis far too small to act on.
Next question
Is the mutant receptor an independent driver, or is it an escape route out of oestrogen dependence that hands the tumour back to the endocrine agent once the receptor is blocked?

Its cohorts are strata of oestrogen receptor status and previous fulvestrant exposure rather than of mutation23. SUMMIT and SGNTUC-019 report objective response. SUMMIT carries the decision, and not because of its response rate22. It is the only study here with anything randomised inside it. Neither fulvestrant alone nor fulvestrant with trastuzumab produced a response. Patients crossing over to the triplet responded. That is what attributes the effect to the kinase inhibitor rather than to endocrine therapy or to antibody blockade of an unamplified receptor. Allele-level selection is a hypothesis here rather than a rule. HER2-negative status was also assigned locally in these studies, so the population is defined by whichever laboratory reported the case.

NTRK fusions are the tumour-agnostic example. Larotrectinib produced an overall response rate of 75% by independent review, with a 95% confidence interval of 61% to 85%, in 55 patients with 17 different TRK fusion-positive tumour types24. Entrectinib produced an objective response in 31 of 54 patients, 57%, in an integrated analysis of three early-phase trials, with a median duration of response of 10 months25. Repotrectinib, a next-generation inhibitor, gave responses in 59% of 51 inhibitor-naive patients and 48% of 69 previously treated with a TRK inhibitor26. The relevance to breast cancer is limited by prevalence rather than by the size of the effect. These fusions are very rare outside secretory carcinoma, so the pre-test probability determines whether looking for them is sensible.

PIK3CA, AKT1 and PTEN alterations form the largest actionable group in this disease and they belong to Endocrine resistance, and the presentations the sequence does not fit, where the trials and the sequencing are set out27,28. ESR1 mutations belong there too, and the selection that produces them is described in Endocrine therapy and the emergence of ESR1 alterations.

The organising principle is that actionable is not a binary property. A tiering framework ranks alterations by the strength of the evidence linking them to benefit in a specific tumour type. The breast-specific application of that framework separates alterations with randomised support from those resting on case reports29.

The practical rule follows from prevalence rather than from biology. A rare alteration is worth looking for when a panel is already being run for another reason, or when histology raises the pre-test probability, as lobular histology does for HER2 mutation Invasive lobular disease in the metastatic setting.

5 · Tumor-agnostic biomarkers

A tumour-agnostic approval says that a biomarker predicts benefit across tissue of origin. It does not say that the biomarker is common in any particular disease, and in breast cancer that distinction does most of the work.

Mismatch repair deficiency was the first. Objective radiographic responses occurred in 53% of patients with advanced mismatch repair-deficient cancers across 12 tumour types treated with PD-1 blockade, and complete responses in 21%30. The proposed mechanism is that defective mismatch repair generates a large burden of mutant neoantigens. The United States approval of pembrolizumab for these tumours followed in May 2017, on an overall response rate of 39.6% in 149 patients with 15 tumour types from five single-arm trials31. It was the first approval of a cancer treatment defined by a biomarker rather than by the site of origin. Dostarlimab has since given a response rate of 44.0% in 327 patients with mismatch repair-deficient tumours, most of them endometrial or colorectal32.

High tumour mutational burden followed. A prospective biomarker analysis within a multi-cohort phase 2 study set the threshold at 10 mutations per megabase33. Objective responses to pembrolizumab occurred in 30 of 102 patients above it, which is 29%, against 43 of 688 patients below it, which is 6%.

That study is worth reading for what it did not include. The eligible tumour types were anal, biliary, cervical, endometrial, mesothelioma, neuroendocrine, salivary, small-cell lung, thyroid and vulvar33. Breast cancer was not among them. The tumour-agnostic claim reaches breast cancer by inference rather than by measurement, and that should be said when the option is offered to a patient.

The inference has since been tested in breast cancer directly. In the TAPUR basket trial, a breast cohort with high tumour mutational burden treated with pembrolizumab had disease control in 37% and objective response in 21% of 28 patients34. Molecular exceptions to the endocrine sequence

Can tumour mutational burden select hormone receptor-positive disease for checkpoint blockade?

Rationale
This is the least immunogenic of the subtypes. Checkpoint blockade has found no place in it. A small fraction of these tumours are nonetheless hypermutated, often after long endocrine exposure or on a mismatch repair defect. If immune benefit follows neoantigen load rather than receptor status, those tumours should behave like hypermutated cancers elsewhere.
Experiment
A single-arm trial gave nivolumab with low-dose ipilimumab to HER2-negative disease entered on a tumour mutational burden above a fixed threshold, with a prespecified correlative analysis at a higher threshold. Beside it sit two studies that did not select on burden at all. A randomised trial added pembrolizumab to a TROP2 conjugate in this subtype without selecting on PD-L1. A rare-histology cohort of a basket study gave the same dual blockade on metaplastic histology alone.
Finding
Responses occurred in hypermutated disease. The patients above the higher burden threshold responded more often than those below it. In the randomised setting the checkpoint inhibitor added nothing to the conjugate on the primary endpoint. Its PD-L1-defined subgroup gave a direction rather than a result. The rare-histology cohort cuts the other way. Its few responses were durable. They arose in tumours with low burden, low PD-L1 and no tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes, which is the opposite of what the burden argument would predict.
Limitation
The burden-selected trial entered patients on burden within HER2-negative disease, and most of its patients were hormone receptor-positive. The registry tags it across both subtypes for that reason. Its comparison by receptor status was exploratory, so no hormone receptor-positive result can be read out of it. The threshold analysis carrying the selection message rests on a handful of patients and was prespecified as a correlative outcome rather than randomised. It nominates a cut-off and does not validate one. The PD-L1 subgroup in the conjugate trial is a secondary analysis confined to the patients with evaluable tissue, and it was not significant. The rare-histology cohort is one arm of a basket study whose report describes most of its tumours as triple-negative, so it cannot be placed in this subtype at all. Burden is also a property of the panel that counts it. A threshold expressed per megabase of sequence travels between assays less well than it reads.
Next question
Every exception in this story depends on a test the receptor workup does not perform. Most of these alterations are acquired under treatment rather than present at diagnosis. Which sample is that test supposed to be run on?

NIMBUS SACI-IO HR+ DART / SWOG S1609 cohort 36

The three studies behind that question do not point the same way, and the disagreement is about what each one selected on rather than about the drugs.

NIMBUS is the study built on the burden argument35. It gave nivolumab with low-dose ipilimumab to HER2-negative disease entered on a tumour mutational burden above a fixed threshold, with a prespecified correlative analysis at a higher one. Responses occurred. They were commoner above the higher cut-off. Two things stop that from selecting a patient in clinic. Most of its patients had hormone receptor-positive disease, but its comparison by receptor status was exploratory, so no result for this subtype reads out of it. And the threshold analysis rests on a handful of patients and was a correlative outcome rather than a randomised comparison. It nominates a cut-off. It does not validate one.

SACI-IO HR+ is the randomised comparison, and it did not select on burden at all36. Adding pembrolizumab to a TROP2 conjugate in hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative disease did not improve progression-free survival. Its PD-L1-defined subgroup gives a direction rather than a result. That analysis was secondary, confined to the patients with evaluable tissue, and not significant. This is the load-bearing study here, because it is the only one that set checkpoint blockade against the treatment it was being added to.

DART / SWOG S1609 cohort 36 cuts the other way37. A rare-histology cohort of a basket study produced few responses, and those it produced were durable. They arose in tumours with low burden, low PD-L1 and absent tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes, which is the opposite of what the burden argument predicts. The report describes most of its tumours as triple-negative, so the cohort cannot be placed in this subtype at all. Selection was on histology, and histology appears to be doing the work.

Burden is also a property of the panel that counts it. A figure expressed as mutations per megabase depends on the size of the panel, on what it calls a somatic variant, and on how germline variants are filtered out. It travels between assays less well than it reads on a report. A patient is not above or below ten on a biological scale. She is above or below ten on one laboratory's.

NTRK fusions complete the set24, with the prevalence caveat in Rare actionable mutations and fusions. RET fusions joined them in September 2022, when selpercatinib received accelerated approval in the United States for fusion-positive solid tumours38. In 41 evaluable patients with tumours other than lung or thyroid cancer, the response rate was 43.9%39. Breast cancer was among the tumour types in which a response occurred38.

The breast-specific arithmetic governs whether to test. High tumour mutational burden occurs in a small minority of breast cancers and is concentrated in particular contexts rather than distributed evenly40. Mismatch repair deficiency is rarer still. A test with a low positive rate is worth running as part of a panel ordered for other reasons, and is hard to justify as a standalone investigation.

One reading habit is worth acquiring here. When a tumour-agnostic result is quoted, look for the number of patients with the disease in front of you. The per-disease denominator inside a basket trial is frequently in single figures, and a response rate pooled across tissue types is not a response rate in any one of them.

6 · Discordant or changing biomarker results

A result that disagrees with a previous result has three possible explanations and they call for different responses. True biological change. Assay variation, including antibody clone, platform, fixation and scoring. Sampling, because two specimens are two different pieces of tissue Receptor discordance and conversion.

HER2-low is the most unstable of the categories now governing eligibility. Across 547 patients with matched primary and relapse specimens, overall HER2 discordance was 38.0%. Movement from HER2-0 to HER2-low occurred in 15% of cases and from HER2-low to HER2-0 in 14%41. Two movements of similar size in opposite directions are what measurement variation looks like. Some of it is certainly real change, and that design cannot separate the two components.

Hormone receptor and HER2 conversion between primary and distant metastasis is the better-characterised version of the same problem42.

Plasma and tissue disagree for reasons that are not error. A lesion that does not shed is invisible to plasma. A plasma result integrates across lesions and cannot say which one it came from. And variants arising in blood cells rather than in tumour appear in plasma unless matched white cell sequencing removes them43. The blind spots on each side are set out in Liquid biopsy and longitudinal monitoring.

The rule that follows is decision-theoretic rather than biological, and saying so is better than pretending otherwise. When two adequate specimens disagree and the result governs eligibility, acting on the one that opens a treatment option is usually right. The justification is asymmetric consequences, not a claim about which measurement was correct.

Caution

That rule sits one step away from testing repeatedly until a result crosses a threshold, and the two are not the same thing. The difference is whether a reason for the repeat existed before the repeat was ordered. A newer specimen, a different lesion, a change in disease behaviour and a laboratory reporting with the relevant boundary in mind are all reasons. Dissatisfaction with the previous result is not.

7 · Competing actionable targets and how to prioritize

The common situation is not an absence of targets. It is several at once. One report can carry a PIK3CA mutation, HER2-low expression, a germline BRCA variant and a fusion of unknown significance on the same page.

Five criteria order them, and applying them in sequence is better than weighting them informally.

Evidence level for that alteration in this disease. A tiering framework exists for exactly this purpose and it separates randomised support from anecdote29.

Magnitude and durability of the expected benefit, with the comparator named. A hazard ratio against the physician's choice of chemotherapy is not measured on the same scale as a response rate in a single-arm cohort of germline PALB2 carriers. The tables under HER2-low and HER2-ultralow disease and Germline and somatic DNA repair alterations carry both quantities, and ranking one against the other requires saying which each is.

Whether the option is line-restricted. An agent available only before chemotherapy is lost by giving chemotherapy first, which is a reason to sequence it earlier rather than a reason to prefer it.

Toxicity against the patient's current function. Interstitial lung disease on a deruxtecan conjugate is the example that changes most decisions, and its frequency is argued in HER2-low and HER2-ultralow disease. That is a different proposition in a patient with baseline lung disease than in one without.

Whether the alteration is truncal or subclonal. A target present in a fraction of cells leaves a compartment untouched, which is the argument in HER2 heterogeneity and Temporal heterogeneity and clonal evolution. Variant allele fraction in plasma gives a crude indication and tissue gives a better one.

A molecular tumour board exists to do this ordering, and the reporting standards that make it possible are in Reporting standards and molecular tumor boards.

In practice

Order the full panel once and early, rather than sequentially as options run out Biomarker-driven treatment selection.

Write the competing options as a list with the comparator attached to each, before discussing them with the patient.

Say which option is being deferred and why, so that the deferral is a recorded decision rather than an omission.

Re-check a panel result against the most recent specimen before acting on an alteration found years earlier Discordant or changing biomarker results.

Interplay

This chapter is the heterogeneity argument of Part VI arriving as an eligibility form. Every biomarker on that form is a summary statistic over a structured population. An immunohistochemistry score is a mean over cells that vary. A variant allele fraction is a proportion over clones that vary. A panel result is a snapshot of a population that is changing under the treatment the patient is currently receiving. The thresholds applied to those summaries were chosen for reproducibility and for trial design, not because biology changes at those values Definitions and thresholds, spatial, clonal, and the ten percent convention. Treating the form as a description of the tumour, rather than as a set of measurements with known failure modes, is the error this part of the book is written against.
