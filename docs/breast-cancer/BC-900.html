Part XII · Metastatic systemic therapy by subtype and line · Chapter 72

HER2-directed therapy in metastatic disease from first line onwards

The line structure, what each line rests on, and where the evidence for sequencing runs out.

1 · Scope, and the line structure this chapter assumes

This chapter enumerates the systemic evidence for HER2-positive metastatic disease, line by line. The decision architecture, meaning how to weigh a patient's disease burden, symptoms and comorbidity against these options, is in Metastatic HER2-positive disease. Conjugate mechanism and toxicity are in Antibody-drug conjugates and targeted delivery. Disease that is both hormone receptor-positive and HER2-positive is treated in Metastatic triple-positive disease, because its sequencing question is different.

Two features of this subtype shape everything below. Survival is long, so a patient may receive five or more lines and the order matters more than in any other subtype. And the lines were established sequentially against the standard of their day, which means the trial that defined second line enrolled patients who had not received what is now first line.

That second point is the one to hold onto. The evidence base is a stack of comparisons, each valid against its own control, and the stack does not tell you what to do after a modern first line. The tables below give each trial with the population it actually enrolled.

2 · First-line dual blockade with a taxane

Pertuzumab, trastuzumab and a taxane was the first-line standard for a decade, and the reason is in the durability rather than the response rate. Dual blockade at the dimerisation interface and the juxtamembrane domain blocks two routes to signalling, which is the mechanism in HER family signaling.

DESTINY-Breast09 is the change. Trastuzumab deruxtecan with pertuzumab against the taxane triplet moves a conjugate into first line. The argument for doing so is not that the conjugate is more active, which was already known. It is that giving it first may convert responders into durable responders, before the resistance described in Mechanisms of therapeutic resistance is selected.

The argument against is specific and worth stating. Moving the best agent to first line removes it from later lines, and the trials that established later lines enrolled patients who still had it available. A patient who progresses on first-line trastuzumab deruxtecan is in a setting no randomised trial has studied.

Caution

Interstitial lung disease is cumulative and does not respect line of therapy. Moving a deruxtecan conjugate earlier lengthens the exposure of patients who will live for years. The toxicity data in Antibody-drug conjugates and targeted delivery were generated in later-line populations with shorter exposure.

3 · Second-line conjugate therapy

DESTINY-Breast03 established trastuzumab deruxtecan over trastuzumab emtansine in second line, and it did so against the comparator that EMILIA had established a decade earlier. That lineage is what makes the result interpretable. Each step was tested against the standard it replaced.

What the trial does not settle is the second-line choice for a patient who received a deruxtecan conjugate first. If DESTINY-Breast09 moves that agent into first line, this section's evidence becomes evidence about a population that no longer exists in practice.

In practice

When a patient progresses after a conjugate, the question is not which conjugate next but whether the resistance was to the target, the linker-payload, or the delivery. Antibody-drug conjugates and targeted delivery separates the three. Only the first is addressed by switching target.

4 · Third line and beyond

Beyond second line the evidence thins and the trials diverge in what they were trying to show.

HER2CLIMB combined tucatinib with trastuzumab and capecitabine and enrolled patients with active brain metastases, which almost no other registration trial did. TH3RESA established trastuzumab emtansine over physician's choice in a heavily pretreated population. NALA compared two tyrosine kinase inhibitors on the same capecitabine backbone and met one of two coprimary endpoints.

SOPHIA is the instructive negative. Margetuximab is Fc-engineered to bind CD16A more tightly, which should improve antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity, and the final overall survival analysis did not favour it. The CD16A genotype analysis was exploratory and hypothesis-generating, and the standard for what an exploratory subgroup can support is in Clinical trials and evidence interpretation.

5 · Central nervous system disease and the agents that reach it

Central nervous system involvement is common in this subtype and was an exclusion criterion in most registration trials, which is why the evidence is concentrated in a few studies that deliberately enrolled it.

HER2CLIMB enrolled patients with active brain metastases and reported intracranial outcomes as a prespecified analysis. DESTINY-Breast12 studied trastuzumab deruxtecan with and without brain metastases. NALA reported fewer interventions for central nervous system disease with neratinib.

The mechanistic framing that the blood-brain barrier excludes large molecules has not survived contact with the conjugate data, and Emerging treatment strategies takes that up. Established metastases disrupt the barrier. The practical consequence is that a large molecule can be active intracranially, and excluding it on molecular weight alone is reasoning from a model rather than from data.

6 · Maintenance, de-escalation and treatment interruption

A patient in prolonged response on a taxane triplet faces a question the trials did not ask. Taxane is usually stopped for cumulative neuropathy while antibody therapy continues, and that practice is near-universal and rests on very little randomised evidence.

Whether HER2-directed therapy itself can ever stop is unresolved, and it is the same question Unresolved questions and future directions raises about defining cure in metastatic disease. There is no assay that distinguishes eradicated disease from controlled disease, so the decision is made without the information that would settle it.

For hormone receptor-positive HER2-positive disease, endocrine maintenance after induction has randomised support, and that evidence is in Metastatic triple-positive disease.

7 · HER2-mutant disease as a distinct target

Activating HER2 mutations occur in tumours that are HER2-negative by immunohistochemistry and in situ hybridisation. That is the whole point: the gene is altered rather than amplified, so the assay that defines HER2-positive disease does not detect it.

SUMMIT tested neratinib with fulvestrant and trastuzumab in this population. The separation of biology from measurement here is exact and is worth stating to trainees. A HER2-negative report means the protein is not overexpressed and the gene is not amplified. It says nothing about whether the gene is mutated, which requires sequencing.

Acquired HER2 mutation is also a described resistance mechanism to endocrine therapy, which connects this section to the evolution argument in Temporal heterogeneity and clonal evolution.

8 · Unresolved questions and what reads out next

Three questions matter most and none has an answer.

What follows first-line trastuzumab deruxtecan. If DESTINY-Breast09 moves the agent forward, the whole sequence below it is built on trials that enrolled patients for whom it was still available. Nobody has randomised the post-conjugate setting.

Whether conjugates with the same payload class are cross-resistant. Practice already sequences them and the evidence does not yet support it. This is the problem flagged in Antibody-drug conjugates and targeted delivery and generalised in Unresolved questions and future directions.

Whether HER2-directed therapy can be stopped in prolonged responders. No trial has asked, and the absence is a reflection of how hard such a trial is to run rather than of the question's importance.
