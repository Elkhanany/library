How it is built

Written for someone who can grasp anything but wants the bricks laid one at a time.

Rule

Nothing asserted

No “it can be shown that”. Where a result is quoted rather than derived it carries a ⚑, so you always know what you are standing on.

Pacing

One step per line

Routine algebra folds into collapsible boxes, but the reasoning stays on the page. A reader who opens none of them can still follow every argument.

Figures

Only when they teach

Each interactive figure has to show something a static picture cannot, and most of them measure the result rather than illustrating it.
